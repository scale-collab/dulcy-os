## How money comes in (Alex, 2026-11-25)
- Clients pay through **Commas** (formerly Fanbasis). Alex pushes everyone to Commas.
- Some clients pay through PayPal, Wise or Ramp.
- Everything lands in the **Entry Checking** account (Mercury).

## How it is split (Lucid chart, Profit First)
From Entry Checking, Mercury auto-transfers to:

| Account | Share | Job |
|---|---|---|
| OPEX | 40% | Tools, software, services. Spending goes on cards, then cards are paid from OPEX |
| Owner's Compensation | 30% | Pays Alex every month |
| Tax | 15% | Never touched. Moved to the AmEx High Yield Savings ("ghost account") |
| Treasury | 10% | Savings and emergencies |
| Quarterly Bonuses (Profit) | 5% | Untouched until quarter end, then paid to Alex or the team |

Rules: money is not moved between accounts. Expenses must fit inside the OPEX 40%. If they don't: grow revenue or cut costs.

## Spending
- All payments go on **American Express Business cards** (Platinum, Plus, Gold) and sometimes the **Chase Business Cash Back** card. Not debit cards.
- These cards are not in Mercury, so the Mercury connector does not show them. All cards (AmEx Platinum, Plus, Gold and Chase) feed into Xero (Alex, 2026-11-25).

## Twice-monthly manual run: the 14th and 28th (Alex, 2026-11-25)
1. Pay out Owner's Comp.
2. Pay off the card statement balances.
3. Pay employees.
4. Move the Tax account money into the AmEx HYSA.
Each quarter: pay out the Profit account.
