## Applied methods

1. Resolve the correct client/workspace and exact record before every operation. Verify the onboarding automation outputs, client-role mapping and portal isolation. Keep campaigns, lists, sources, scripts, approval state, owners and statuses linked. Do not use the historical document's table/view names as a substitute for inspecting the current schema.
   Sources: [Confirm Client Setup](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KdCaLm_8yg4Nu2zSyKqpUczNVf4WtfEjYFMCPUw0Olc/edit), [Client Portal](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FldKCzKg21m1AgGw2WOaYEbnvWKiiE_X_rpA_v61AMI/edit), [Airtable + EmailBison](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13BUhYja1JRa1M0TGBkDmVQ8OoGR4ZpSv5D4hDzVpB-g/edit).
2. Check reply/booking sync and failure queues, correct sender/platform, warm-up exclusions, backlog, filters and active manager status. A hidden filter can mimic missing replies. Missing calendar integration cannot be cured by merely pasting a booking link.
   Sources: [RevReply Navigation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16nVGjqdipynDzJpexJ4ONoxZkaNd-unNrFwurflXBhA/edit), [Inbox Setup](https://docs.google.com/document/d/18W05eyWLtCpzmlKKoJRfdNzcQREXaRw1_Eg0NUrkf0Q/edit).
3. Preserve the documented current-pilot design of human approval and at-most-once sends. Resume requests a fresh draft. Approve Draft and Send Edited Reply are send actions; manual-send plus approval must not both be applied. Sending, Outbox and uncertain submission require reconciliation before retry. Scheduled is not Sent; Skipped is not Closed; meeting requested is not booked; Won in this inbox means booked, not sales revenue. Later meeting-support email does not re-open sales follow-ups.
   Source: [Dulcy Reply Agent Operating SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RCCbXeUQJRXP8h0nsHnnqbGZNwwfIBKNTEAu67R7A-Q/edit). Its documented Dulcy-only/HITL, calendar, asset and office-hour settings must be checked live before being asserted current.
4. Cold-funnel automation should test new-versus-existing lead matching, custom-field/question order, timezone, qualification path, sender/template mapping, confirmation and future reminders. Reschedule removes stale reminders and creates the replacement reminders; rebook is a separate route. Test both booking and rescheduling end to end. Idempotency and duplicate prevention matter more than copying every legacy module.
   Source: [Cold Funnel Automation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ye6Tw9E76Cqhq7mY8fyIOFpAPsXigaRaT2ZdYKrzkm8/edit).
5. Email health diagnosis: read bounce codes, not just the aggregate; separate invalid recipient, full mailbox, domain/security block, disconnected sender, provider/recipient cohort and content/placement issues. Verify DNS/authentication and account health through the authorized tools. Review suppression/verification history and compare the observed cohort with the selected settings. Use account and domain views without automatically deleting or replacing anything.
   Sources: [Campaign Navigation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13BUhYja1JRa1M0TGBkDmVQ8OoGR4ZpSv5D4hDzVpB-g/edit), [EmailGuard](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1i9OZF1ga02UD0mdS_9t9kuml-MHKGZ_2C1lXcMhpeQg/edit), [Campaign Analysis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d52I_uWQomZ9qujYWOPWt9xcQbn0c68BrMVThwrZtVY/edit).
6. Record each metric's numerator, denominator, time range, timezone, source and campaign/variant/cohort. Interested/replies differs from interested/contacted and interested/sent. Meeting booked/interested differs from show/booked and closed/shows. Do not silently use lifetime per-variant figures beside a rolling campaign window.
   Source: [Email Campaign Reporting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19QmUQcwcXGVqWxGxOjsZbsPbAixOKfXrztiaSlAverk/edit).
7. Keep external identity/access changes, credential handoffs, purchases, external sends, file sharing and live launches behind the applicable explicit permissions. A document saying a human employee should do something does not grant the assistant standing authority.

### Attributed metric references, not universal action thresholds

The [Airtable + EmailBison navigation SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13BUhYja1JRa1M0TGBkDmVQ8OoGR4ZpSv5D4hDzVpB-g/edit) labels these as average benchmarks: 2% reply, 10–20% positive reply, 20% booked-call ratio, 20% closing, and under 2.5% bounce. Its worked example is 10,000 emails → 200 replies → 20–40 positives → 4–8 bookings, so the positive percentage in that example is positives/replies and the booking percentage is bookings/positives. Do not silently equate this with positives/sent or assume the sending platform's reply denominator matches the example. Current date range, unique-recipient versus message count, treatment of automated replies, attribution and authorized client goals must be explicit.

The same document prescribes tagging accounts below 60% health as Quarantine, reducing to one daily send and retesting in 2–3 days; [Campaign Analysis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d52I_uWQomZ9qujYWOPWt9xcQbn0c68BrMVThwrZtVY/edit) repeats below-60 and above-2.5%-bounce checks. These are historical source procedures, not authority for a live mutation or guarantees of recovery. The placement-test SOP reverses the sign, so verify the score definition and current approved trigger before recommending a settings change. The source's fixed weekly volume ramps are examples, not a permission or reason to increase volume before delivery and fit are verified.

For low-positive diagnosis, the analysis SOP says read actual replies and separate positive, soft objection, hard objection and no-response patterns, then examine timing, differentiation, relevance, pain, CTA, ICP and decision-maker fit. Do not copy its broad “not interested” recovery template over an explicit stop, fabricate urgency, or classify no-response as a reply category in the denominator. Its A/B instruction requires equal samples and one changed variable, while one illustrative example changes both subject and first line; retain the one-variable test principle and flag the example mismatch. A statistically small positive-reply burst alone is not proof of product-market fit.

## Relevant source conflicts

- Placement sign: Running Inbox Placement Tests says report >60% for replacement; Campaign Analysis and EmailGuard say below 60 is problematic. Exact boundary at 60 and score meaning require live verification. Source URLs: [placement](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V4Lx9g_7B0_TS_jOIcwVbV3EqwnNVZRco2cFATj9bdI/edit), [EmailGuard](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1i9OZF1ga02UD0mdS_9t9kuml-MHKGZ_2C1lXcMhpeQg/edit).
- Bounce denominator: Email Campaign Reporting labels Bounced as replies/contact, evidently inconsistent with the label. Preserve the discrepancy and use validated provider definitions. Legacy 2.5% is source-specific, not a universal safety guarantee.
- Quarantine versus replace: older navigation says tag Quarantine, daily limit 1, retest in 2–3 days; vendor MI reference says burned domains cannot recover and should be canceled/deleted. Do not infer causality or authorize destructive changes from either claim.
- Verification stack: legacy basic-list SOP uses EmailGuard → LeadMagic → MillionVerifier by credits; newer Triple Verify uses MillionVerifier then selective Clay recovery/LeadMagic and fit; EmailGuard navigation calls its verifier optional. Select the current approved workflow and document deviations.
- Portal visibility: onboarding says unlinked users see all clients; portal-specific SOP says they see nothing. Neither is a current access audit. Verify tenant isolation directly before granting/sharing.
- Reply/booking policy: legacy Inbox/RevReply auto-mode advice conflicts with later Dulcy-only HITL pilot design. Apply current live workspace overrides and human-send permission, not historical settings.
- Unsubscribe controls: navigation says no unsubscribe links; vendor reference says one-click unsubscribe. Do not disable required opt-out controls based on a deliverability heuristic; follow current applicable requirements and configured policy.
- Reminder timings conflict within Cold Funnel itself: 24h/4h/15m/meeting versus pasted 6h/1h/5m variants, including a 4-hour heading with 6-hour text. Verify selected automation timing and rendered copy together.
- Vendor MI deliverability text is advice, not independent validated fact. Its mailbox/name randomization, false titles, domain-ban avoidance, absolute provider-routing/copy-causality claims, hard volume thresholds and delete-all guidance are not safe universal operating rules. Keep only testable diagnosis and approved experiments; retain truthful sender identity and opt-outs. Reference [MI native doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1_iXti1duQtIy3cGsdyISXLzL04WzihEwne5spP9RWTA/edit), [MI PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OvTia-uNllGz-kzXELA7rXkAw3KuelEP/view).
- MI reply-routing one-pager usefully suggests preserving the original engaged thread/sender and separating internal forwarding from external reply identity. Its claimed zero risk and guaranteed spam effects are not established. Never change Reply-To or filters without checking current workflow and authorization. Reference [reply-routing PDF](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1JUwaNRRxFg7rO58vsWIrut8GUGDyBb7b/view).
- Historical prices, domain TLD instructions, sender volumes, seats and contract/refund terms vary. They are not standing purchase/contract authorization or current provider pricing.

The user-demonstrated managed Campaign/Lead List Center sequence remains the operational authority for stage controls. These older SOPs do not permit direct status/checkbox edits or provider calls to bypass its review gates.

## Completed MI PDF text and provider cross-check

The full available [MI Deliverability Cheatsheet PDF text](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1OvTia-uNllGz-kzXELA7rXkAw3KuelEP/view) was reviewed in the continued October 9 pass: all twenty principles and the action sheet, 35,482 extracted characters. Decorative headings are extraction-garbled; visuals remain unreviewed. The existing vendor-conflict exclusions above still apply. For placement diagnosis, record the actual seed-provider and recipient-type coverage; do not extrapolate an untested population or every mailbox from one result. Correlated cohort changes suggest hypotheses, not proof of one cause.

Current primary-source check, October 9, for personal Gmail recipients: [Google's sender guidelines](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/81126) require accurate sender identity/content and prohibit misleading reply/forward framing. They distinguish volume-specific authentication requirements, require TLS and valid DNS, and describe additional bulk-sender requirements including DMARC alignment and one-click unsubscribe for relevant messages. They recommend wanted/opted-in mail, gradual sending and monitoring actual provider responses; compliance never guarantees delivery. A low open rate alone does not establish delivery failure. Treat Gmail spam-report rates separately from bounce, reply or placement metrics.

The [Google FAQ](https://support.google.com/mail/answer/14229414) counts bulk volume across the same primary domain and treats bulk status as persistent. It distinguishes RFC 8058 headers from a body/mailto link and discusses honoring unsubscribe requests within 48 hours. Recheck current provider scope and requirements before live advice or changes; these dated facts do not authorize sends, DNS/security edits or new infrastructure. Do not mislabel permission-based provider requirements as a blanket legal/compliance clearance for cold email.

## Campaign compilation arithmetic check

The fully reviewed available text of the [2-Hour Campaign compilation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pnNUb_SX_aid2fI9gNPX-U_DrnLXdUkcS7BIpYqAhsM/edit) contains a tenfold first-stage error: 5,000 emails at 1% replies produces 50 replies, not 500. Recalculate every funnel stage from its named denominator; separate fractional expected outcomes from actual counts. Include approved fulfillment/incentive costs and held/qualified outcomes when modeling economics. Its example supplies no permission to spend or send gifts. Preserve raw-to-derived traceability and inspect missing/ambiguous enrichment before a managed import.
