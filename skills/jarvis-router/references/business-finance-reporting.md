# Finance Reporting Formats

## Daily Finance Summary

### Cash position
- Bank/account balances by account
- Total immediately available cash

### Recent movement
- Cash collected
- Large/important inflows
- Large/important outflows
- Refunds/disputes/failed payments

### Forward view
- Upcoming committed expenses
- Expected upcoming billables/collections
- Committed EOM cash forecast
- Expected EOM cash forecast, if assumptions are available

### Exceptions
- Missing settlements
- Failed/overdue invoices
- Unreconciled transactions
- Unusual spend
- Any data-source mismatch

## Month-End Summary

### Revenue and collections
- Revenue
- Cash collected
- Refunds
- Disputes
- A/R ending balance

### Cost and profitability
- Direct fulfillment cost
- Gross profit
- Gross margin
- Operating expenses
- Operating profit

### Cash
- Beginning cash
- Ending cash
- Net cash movement

### Billing exceptions
- Failed payments
- Overdue invoices
- Canceled subscriptions
- Unreconciled processor/bank/accounting items

### Management actions
Return only the highest-priority actions with owner and reason when known.
