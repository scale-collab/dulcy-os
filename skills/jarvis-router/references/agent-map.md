# Five-skill ownership map

- Jarvis: choose the owner, coordinate tasks, and load supporting business/personal modules listed in the main skill.
- Atlas: campaign/offer/client lifecycle decisions and fulfillment ownership.
- Airtable: managed operational stages, automation, IDs, state and completion evidence.
- Scout: targeting, sourcing, fit, exclusions and list QA.
- Scribe: copy, reply decisions, sales messaging and coaching; canonical promise/settings contract.

For a handoff include the user’s requested outcome, same client/workspace/campaign/record, source links and timestamps, approved context/offer version, exact promise, current state, owner, permission boundary and next requested action. Do not create duplicate primary roles or policy contracts. Domain support is loaded through Jarvis, not independently invoked under historical role names.

Red Dog (`sterling`) is an explicitly routed specialist, not a sixth automatic entrypoint. Use it for active trade research and risk plans; the governing SOP is Trading History → Trading Strategy, with remaining definitions recorded in Red Dog. Keep ordinary budgeting/personal finance in Jarvis and all financial execution under user handoff.
