## Scope and routing

Use Jarvis for executive support, personal administration, travel, research, documents, finance support, people, real estate, and specialist coordination. Use Airtable for approved managed operational records, campaign/project tracking, reporting lineage, and automation controls. Maintain one actual source of truth; link execution tools to it rather than creating parallel masters.

The linked written SOPs are covered. Duplicate copies use canonical sources. Embedded screenshots, videos, linked templates and current platform/legal claims have not been independently verified. Template examples, placeholder owners, historical third-party policies and file timestamps do not establish current preferences or standing permission. Preparation is distinct from sending, spending, recording, granting access, changing security settings or making consequential commitments.

## Inbox and communication

[Email Labelling — dedicated procedure](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1eupf3Go_A23cbMsWOWWSnGZ7TeWbOAv3n3MviUbgyZw/edit?usp=drivesdk): read the full thread, assign one priority label plus contextual tags, label before archiving, and keep unresolved decisions visible. Urgent means executive/high-stakes/time-sensitive input; Review means approval pending; Actioned means actually completed within authorized scope; FYI means no action required. Classify content, not sender category: a board email is not automatically FYI. Source suggests 24-hour Review follow-up and one-hour unresolved Urgent escalation; apply only an approved cadence/channel.

[SOP: Drafting and Answering Emails on Behalf of the Executive](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1PgGts0kLM8JzxSaRjRrY-duQsD1L8Pc4ojGJ0SdJs7w/edit?usp=drivesdk): write in the user's actual voice, stay concise, tailor templates, check identities/links/attachments, and proofread. Escalate legal, finance, HR, board/CEO or explicit executive-decision requests. Track source thread, pending action and follow-up date; never commit, share pricing/contracts or send without scope. Its targets are one-hour time-sensitive response, same-business-day other response and 3–4-business-day important follow-up.

[Email Labelling — combined email-drafting versions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MoF7SKb80cKV_1eznHypRO3oOjr4QN7XZgeWXvOmTSk/edit?usp=drivesdk) is misnamed and contains two conflicting procedures: 3–5 daily checks versus every 30–60 minutes; urgency-specific follow-ups versus uniform 3–4 business days; differing signatures and phrase advice. Preserve that unresolved boundary and use current explicit instructions. Notifications, filters, unsubscribes, calls, forwarding and external platform drafts each retain applicable permissions.
