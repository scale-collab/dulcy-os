# Sales Framework

Apply this agency-sales framework only when it fits the approved client context and promise contract. Technical, manufacturing, and procurement-led clients may need different stages and pace. A call that delivers an agreed free asset must actually fulfill it; these sales-call steps must not replace that fulfillment.

## Cold lead context

Cold outbound creates interest; the sales process converts that interest into a decision. Do not expect the cold email itself to close a high-ticket service.

### Booked call -> call
Target an 80%+ show rate using a nurture sequence that can include:
- 5 email touches
- 5 SMS touches
- 1 confirmation call
- Pre-call VSL, proof, case studies, resources, and clear meeting expectations

Use these as an operating model, not a rigid requirement when the client has a better proven system.

## 45-minute call structure

### 1. Discovery — about 20 minutes
Understand:
- Why they took the call
- Current process
- What they want instead
- What's preventing it
- What they have tried
- Cost/impact of the problem
- Urgency
- Decision process
- Fit

The goal is to make the problem and desired outcome explicit enough that the pitch can be tailored directly to them.

### 2. Pitch — about 20 minutes
- Recap their situation in their language
- Explain the mechanism
- Tie each relevant part of the offer to a stated need
- Use proof that matches their context
- Explain delivery and next steps
- Check for questions and unresolved concerns

### 3. Close — 5–20 minutes
- Surface remaining objections
- Resolve material concerns
- Present pricing/terms clearly
- Ask for a decision
- If a second call is genuinely required, schedule it with a specific purpose

## Objection categories

### Trust gap
They do not yet believe the company can deliver.
Use relevant proof, process clarity, references, or a risk-reducing mechanism that is actually authorized.

### Value gap
They understand the offer but do not see enough upside.
Return to quantified pain, desired outcome, economics, or the gap between current state and future state.

### Timing
Clarify whether there is a real external constraint or low urgency.
If real, establish a concrete future trigger/date. If not, surface the real concern.

### Price/budget
Do not reflexively discount. Clarify whether the issue is affordability, ROI, cash timing, or lack of conviction.

### Decision authority
Identify who else must be involved and why. Bring them into a specific next call when needed.

### Competitor / alternative
Understand what they value about the alternative and compare only on dimensions relevant to the prospect's stated goals.

## Follow-up principles

Follow up around a reason:
- unanswered question
- requested asset
- decision date
- missing stakeholder
- implementation detail
- agreed check-in
- material new proof

Avoid empty "checking in" messages.
