# Sienna organic-content module Platform Playbook

## Account scope

Business accounts only (Alex founder brand, Dulcy, clients). Personal gaming content is outside this repo.

## LinkedIn formats

### Operator lesson
Hook: specific claim or lesson.
Body: what happened, what changed, why it matters.
Close: takeaway or optional CTA.

### Breakdown
Hook: result/problem.
Body: 3-5 concrete components.
Close: principle that generalizes.

### Founder story
Hook: decision/tension.
Body: brief context, mistake or choice, lesson.
Close: what the founder now does differently.

### Case study
Hook: outcome or problem, only if approved/supported.
Body: starting point -> intervention -> result -> lesson.
Avoid turning the post into an ad.

## X formats

### Single post
One observation + implication. Usually 1-4 short paragraphs.

### Mini framework
Strong opening + 3-5 concise bullets + takeaway.

### Thread
Use only if sequential explanation adds value. Put the core value in the first post; do not rely on "thread below" as the hook.

## Short-form video formats

### Mistake hook
"If your cold email is getting replies but no meetings, this is probably the problem."

### Result hook
"We found one change that matters more than adding another 10,000 leads."

### Contrarian hook
"Your lead list can be 95% valid and still be terrible."

### Screen-recording breakdown
Show the system/UI, explain one decision, end with the practical lesson.

## Script template

**Hook (0-3 sec):** [tension/result/claim]
**Context (3-8 sec):** [minimum needed context]
**Value (8-35 sec):** [1-3 points]
**Proof/example (optional):** [supported detail]
**Close:** [takeaway or low-friction CTA]

## Caption guidance

Captions should either:
- summarize the useful lesson,
- add a detail that did not fit the video,
- point to a resource,
- or invite a relevant conversation.

Do not fill captions with generic hashtags. Use hashtags only when the platform/account strategy has evidence they help.
