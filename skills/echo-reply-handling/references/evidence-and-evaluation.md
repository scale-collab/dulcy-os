## Source hierarchy and transferable findings

Prefer explicit current user instructions, actual prospect promises, effective settings and approved client facts over historical examples. Use creators/training for bounded strategy hypotheses. No cited creator result, training number, historical client price or pitch claim becomes an approved commercial fact.

The user explicitly requires all-client applicability, an actual reusable reply brain, meaningful nurture/questions instead of indiscriminate meeting asks, direct openings without thanking people for replies, and output shaped by selected settings. Explicitly agreed delivery of a free list on a call is legitimate; a promise to send material cannot acquire a hidden call gate.

## Evaluation and acceptance

Separate three layers:
1. Contract lint: bundled synthetic positive/negative fixtures catch declared hard-gate violations. Actual execution and counts belong in the save receipt, not a performance claim.
2. Independent draft replay: hide later outcomes and unobserved messages; use only sources available at the decision point. Score natural-language behavior and source fidelity.
3. Separately authorized live pilot: prove draft creation → reviewer visibility → explicit exact-draft send → provider success → correct notification. This skill creation does not authorize or run the pilot.

Hold out 30–50 distinct canonical dialogues/accounts, stratified across business models, free-asset/price/technical/meeting/referral/timing/OOO/stop/current-customer cases and missing-evidence negatives. Split by dialogue/account before replay to avoid duplicate mirrors in both development and holdout. Historical cases used to write this skill are development examples, not unexposed holdout tests. Use unseen cases for generalization claims.

Proposed rubric, not measured performance: 0–2 each for correct current state; request/promise fulfillment; approved facts; intent/fit separation; action; meaningful next step; low friction; appropriate timing; accountable ownership; identity/evidence/current-review control. Target average at least 18/20, no case below 16/20, at least 95% human-label agreement for intent/action and 100% for critical stops. Do not mask hard failures with averages.

Critical failures: autonomous/duplicate/stale send, opt-out leak, wrong client/person, unsupported asset/completion/price/proof/certification/availability, hidden condition, booking-to-revenue inference. Critical cases must yield safe blockers when evidence is missing. Test false positives too: legitimate call delivery and explicit meeting intent must not be stopped by a blanket anti-call rule.

Track requested→delivered assets, unanswered-question age, staffed response time with verified timestamps, review/edit burden and client-specific progression. Measure positive→booked, booked→held, held→client-accepted-qualified and opportunities/cash separately using explicit denominators/windows and pending-outcome censoring. A selected booked cohort cannot establish population conversion, optimum touch count or causal lift.
