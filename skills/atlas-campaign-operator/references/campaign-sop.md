# Alex's campaign SOP, summarised (source: Drive "X - # 2-Hour Cold Email Campaign SOP", read 2026-11-25)

Rules in `rules/RULES.md` win where they differ. The conflicts are listed at the end.

## Steps (about 2 hours in total)

1. **Define the campaign (10 min).**
   - Write four things:
     - **ICP:** who exactly.
     - **Pain:** one problem only.
     - **Dream outcome:** money, time or status.
     - **Proof:** one client, one source or one specific insight.
   - "Do not move on until this is written."
   - Save it as the Campaign Thesis, named `[CLIENT] ICP - FILTERS - SOURCE (ANGLE)`.
   - The offer comes from the Client Offer Sheet. To build lead magnets, mine sales calls for the top 3 pains, the top 3 reasons people buy and the top 3 reasons they don't.

2. **Pick one structure (5 min).**
   - **Question → Proof → CTA:** when the prospect may not fully see the problem yet.
   - **Offer → Proof → CTA:** when the outcome is already easy to want.
   - "Do not blend them."

3. **Write Step 1 (20 min).**
   - Write 3–5 versions, then choose one winner. "Do not keep rewriting forever."
   - One problem, one proof, one CTA, 30–75 words.

4. **Write Steps 2 and 3 (20 min).**
   - Step 2 clarifies the first email and makes it easier to believe.
   - Step 3 adds trust, proof or ideas.
   - "Do not invent a new offer in the follow-ups."
   - Then add spintax and the signature block.

5. **Checklist (10 min).**
   - Dream outcome, trust and proof.
   - Exactly one CTA, 30–75 words.
   - "Could a scammer have sent this?"
   - "If it passes, stop editing."

6. **Lead filter (15 min).**
   - Write which companies qualify, which titles to keep and cut, and the minimum volume.
   - Run DiscoLike with the ICP prompt and validate the first 100.
   - Scale 100 → 1,000 → 4,000 → 10,000.

7. **Split the list (15 min).**
   - Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Cut.
   - Contacts: leadership, executives, founders and owners. Not entry or mid-level roles.
   - Verify, then upload.

8. **Final send check (10 min).**
   - Why would I care? Why would I believe it? Why reply? Is the ask small?
   - "Rewrite once. Once."

9. **Launch (5 min).**
   - Mon–Fri, 8:XX to 18:XX.
   - Check the copy, leads and variables in EmailBison, add inboxes, launch.
   - LinkedIn (HeyReach) is broken today, so skip it.
   - Text the client.

**Hard rules:**
- No copy while the ICP is fuzzy.
- Don't test five ideas in one campaign.
- Don't reword a script that works.
- Don't chase perfect data.

## Copy rules

- **Length and shape:** 30–75 words, 3–4 sentences, one CTA, one solution. Proof must match the dream outcome.
- **CTA:** answerable in one word. No links. Interest, resource or permissionless. Bare "Interested?" or "Worth a chat?" is too generic.
- **Subjects:** 1–3 words, 3 spintax variants (one each of 1, 2 and 3 words), no punctuation.
- **Style:**
  - Simple words (6th-grade level or lower).
  - Dashes written " - ". No semicolons.
  - Never "this isn't just" or "I am following up".
- **Variables:** `{FIRST_NAME}`, `{COMPANY}` and custom ones. Never spin a variable.
- **Spintax:** `{a|b|c}`, every combination grammatical, at most 4 sets per sentence.
- **Offers:** "Offer is everything". Ideally a lead magnet or front-end offer before the core service.

## Next campaign (SOP method)

- **Email-to-lead ratio near the agency average:** top off with lookalikes.
- **40% or more below it:** same offer, new vertical, as a fresh campaign.
- **Locked into one industry:** a new offer, otherwise copy tests.
- **Also:** mine interested and objection replies plus call notes, and build one angle per buying situation.
