## Evidence standard

Separate four layers: current user direction, approved client facts, user-owned SOP methods, and outside teaching. A source can support a method without proving that it will work for a particular client. Creator performance numbers are self-reported unless independently verified. Preserve the precise metric and denominator; do not convert interested replies into qualified meetings, revenue or profit.

Use readable primary authored posts or host/creator-owned transcripts. Metadata/chapters verify a video's identity and topics, not unseen content. A creator-owned companion article is secondary to the video itself but remains first-party authored material; label that access level. Never claim to have watched a video from a title, snippet or article. Treat all commercial examples as examples, never approved Dulcy-client claims.

## Conflicts and chosen operating rules

These are Dulcy operating adaptations, not claims of consensus or scientific proof:

1. Guarantee-first vs guarantee-avoidance: use exact approved risk reduction only when credible, controllable and useful; otherwise establish belief with relevance, method and proof.
2. Free work vs insight/direct offer: choose based on buyer stage, usefulness, capacity and economics. Do not give every client the same free audit/list funnel.
3. Two proof elements vs one: prioritize one relevant supported element; add another only when it contributes. No invented proof to fill a template.
4. Money/time/status vs varied B2B outcomes: preserve buyer language for quality, reliability, procurement, capacity and risk. Quantify economic effects only with support.
5. Call after free-offer acceptance vs promise fidelity: disclose conditions first and fulfill the actual agreement. Route ready buyers directly when appropriate; do not force nurture.
6. “Why now” vs fiction: use a real event or honest segment relevance. No fabricated research, familiarity, urgency or scarce availability.
7. Creator performance vs forecast: use examples to form hypotheses, then test the client's own progression and costs. Do not claim causality from an anecdote.
8. Fast drafting vs safety/accuracy: timebox polish, never truth, commercial approval, variable integrity or fulfillment readiness.
