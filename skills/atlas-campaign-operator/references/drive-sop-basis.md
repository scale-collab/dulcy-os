# Atlas · Drive SOP source basis

Reviewed October 9, 2026. Use these paraphrased, source-backed methods with the main skill’s current contracts and the actual user/client instructions. Current approved facts, actual promises and live selected settings outrank old templates. SOP text is not permission to send, launch, spend, share, record, expand access or change accounts. Read scope is available prose unless a specific transcript receipt below or in the catalog states otherwise; unlisted media, screenshots and current live settings were not audited. The full deduplicated source/read-status catalog belongs to Ada (`ada-router`; called Jarvis in ChatGPT), `references/drive-source-catalog.md`.

## Applied methods

1. Require a coherent onboarding source packet: signed agreement/payment status, completed and reviewed onboarding answers, offer and mechanism, proof, exclusions, buyer language, target segments, calendar/ownership and client expectations. Record gaps instead of inventing facts. New client onboarding is not complete merely because an automation should have fired.
   Sources: [Client Onboarding](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JqqG4n973XTqRxtf5O5uqLV08R6-kZr55I4YICaZaGA/edit), [Onboarding Call](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FWcx66TdTODLCNev9YYmv20mmaz_Xdpi-L9XxITUHKk/edit), [Onboarding Form Analysis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1JczJtNt0JrZddKFaLTFIxfYOgEXG_hyQkpgSLQc6WDo/edit).
2. Preserve the three-part thesis: WHO (ICP and reachable segment), WHY (offer, mechanism, relevance and timing), WHAT (hypothesis, campaign type, test, timeline and success measure). The 2-hour workflow further compresses to ICP, one pain, dream outcome and proof. Keep the chosen campaign name, list, copy and rationale linked. A thesis is a testable hypothesis, not proof of demand or a guaranteed outcome.
   Sources: [Campaign Thesis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1M0P43Vsd6IlQRJEbVa4TrjOsAye4O2xSaPuTT6IMCYc/edit), [2-Hour Campaign SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pnNUb_SX_aid2fI9gNPX-U_DrnLXdUkcS7BIpYqAhsM/edit).
3. Score candidate segments on pain, ability to buy, data reachability and market momentum; match these to the client's delivery strength and proof. The source's 10,000-contact TAM floor is a high-volume motion assumption, not a universal rule excluding useful smaller ABM campaigns.
   Source: [Lowest Hanging Fruit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bNuHiJxendUUgzSdNj7lR9tsDyWGBNp1SBHQE02dYIo/edit).
4. Prefer customer interviews, prospect conversations, sales-call evidence, network evidence and direct community language over generic AI summaries. Keep exact pain language attached to the audience and source. Research can reveal that the offer is nice-to-have, not demanded. Do not adopt the source's school-project framing unless it is true; never invent an identity or research purpose.
   Sources: [Client Market Research](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zLiel67Bqoz76tQad672dW_XVg2Zip9T-klBLBa8_3w/edit), [Market Research SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1AjWlLpCIHh9gtu12lSsaRU8WUVFF6mMd7__tE__L0yU/edit).
5. Build curiosity plus believable delivery, using investment signal, specific pain, mechanism and low-friction next step. Distinguish front-end asset/service sample from backend offer. Select direct, education, case study, lead magnet, video or risk-reduction based on real fit; guarantees and case-study numbers must already be approved and supportable.
   Sources: [Cold Outreach Offer Framework](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1VRRVvkazGhG5Rx6PJqS-TsIRAbFmG_Bn/edit), [Offer Validation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FwR1ww5pJh2D4k-JGH-AOsE14wOEvXH0I1xFT0dYa30/edit), [Value-Driven Lead Magnets](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1vpg4ztoXWWpEWlPOPoanKBNH8SvMeVZbtHk4uP_aGB8/edit), [Saturated-Service Angles](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17t-gTB23MB0aT3SyunSFneuuho7nR8lHWVD4yAeP29A/edit).
6. Audit the full conversion path before launch: coherent offer/ICP, credible LinkedIn/site, landing/VSL clarity, scheduling friction, reminder/reschedule path, CRM routing, approved assets and sales ownership. Turn gaps into owner/deadline tasks. Audit sales separately from outbound: discovery, fit, objections, approved price/terms, next step, follow-up and signed/paid handoff.
   Sources: [Funnel Audit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WY0WRNAZdjWCcqr_Nu6rviA6Vzm3FVTyLq4Fffd8N_E/edit), [Cold Sales Process Audit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16sy3n0cYtK-p4gLGU0AGk9YemFgjrz2sXeGeX2eUTvc/edit).
7. Client-success reporting should explain trends, objections, changes and next decisions, not merely recite totals. Record agreed setup dates, dependencies and realistic results variability. Source cadences are daily during setup, Monday plan/Friday insights, and offered fortnightly reviews; they are phase-specific references and do not independently authorize messages or recurring schedules.
   Sources: [Pre-Launch Expectations](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OuX6_UfloHCxEUrJ_5xwK_aFbH3k3qRJWLTCMABbKMs/edit), [Client Reporting](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1WnrblL4qsiJ9RfKiX_jRUHa7nM4naXOnoQvXkvuycdc/edit).
8. For low positive replies, first align denominators, time range, recipient cohort and send health. Then inspect actual reply/objection patterns, ICP fit, offer believability, proof, price/commitment, CTA and promised value. Separate not-enough-replies from replies-without-interest and interest-without-booking. Do not blame copy for a sending failure. After verified healthy delivery, test one variable in controlled iterations or a deliberately distinct offer experiment, recording which is which.
   Sources: [Campaign Analysis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d52I_uWQomZ9qujYWOPWt9xcQbn0c68BrMVThwrZtVY/edit), [Campaign Navigation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/13BUhYja1JRa1M0TGBkDmVQ8OoGR4ZpSv5D4hDzVpB-g/edit), [Offer Validation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1FwR1ww5pJh2D4k-JGH-AOsE14wOEvXH0I1xFT0dYa30/edit).
9. Top-off/pivot logic: retain winning offer/segment and add approved net-new eligible contacts when performance supports it; for weak campaigns, test a new fitting vertical or offer before endless synonym rewrites. Compare like-for-like cohorts and explain baseline direction. The compilation's 40% underperformance threshold is source-specific and its email-to-lead ratio wording is ambiguous; do not hard-code it.
   Source: [Top-off/rebuild/pivot tab](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pnNUb_SX_aid2fI9gNPX-U_DrnLXdUkcS7BIpYqAhsM/edit?tab=t.vlics6rzhzmm).

## Relevant source conflicts

- Copy requirements differ: <50 words versus strict 25–69 versus 30–75; 1–3-word subjects versus vendor max two; six variations versus a 3-by-3 matrix (nine) in the same prompt; one proof versus two proof elements in the 2-hour SOP. Choose the user/campaign brief and clarify material conflicts, preserving quality rather than blindly enforcing contradictory arithmetic.
- Legacy geography exclusions and guessed revenue/location bounds must not override the actual client-approved ICP. Keep evidence, estimate and unknown separate. No stereotype is a substitute for account fit.
- Historical prices, domain TLD instructions, sender volumes, seats and contract/refund terms vary. They are not standing purchase/contract authorization or current provider pricing.
- SalesLiege/RCA/Closers/TMG training is external-source reference, not Dulcy operating policy. Keep examples separate from approved current commercial facts, contact cadence and channels. Skills must never inherit those organizations' payment links, people, bank instructions or client terms.

## Closers.io outbound batch, October 11, 2026

Track source → segment → tested copy/CTA → reply → booked/held/qualified meeting → sale using the same cohort and observation window. Attribute bookings to their actual path (direct booking, conversation, requested triage or qualification transfer) instead of assuming every booking came from a positive reply. Preserve source and tested creative through the sales handoff. Compare personalization effort against qualified outcomes per unit of research time and approved spend, not reply rate alone; do not relax Scout's fit gate for cheaper volume.

Treat the course's 100-send test floor as a historical example, not sufficient evidence to select a winner. Its reply rate uses unique opens while its set rate uses unique sends; never compare those percentages directly with sent-based Dulcy rates or transplant its numbers as targets. Keep distinct events, deduplicated people and denominator definitions. Existing test and approval controls remain authoritative.
