Use when generating offer alternatives from a client’s existing capability. Do not copy commercial claims from examples.

## Choose a pattern from a real advantage

- **Available supply, demand or introductions** (examples 1, 3, 20–21, 24, 47, 55, 70, 77, 95, 98, 100): match verified available talent, buyers, capacity or referrals to a relevant recipient. Confirm existence, availability, representation and permission before sharing identities; never invent overflow, exclusivity or urgency.
- **Bounded useful sample or pilot** (11, 23, 32, 35, 40, 49, 58, 61, 72–73, 76, 91): let the buyer evaluate an actual unit of delivery. Define quantity, scope, usable output, ownership, inputs, costs, duration, end-of-trial terms and owner. A free sample is an option, not the default.
- **Specific finding or diagnostic** (5–7, 9, 25–26, 29–30, 41, 45, 56, 68, 74, 89–90): make the next step a relevant explanation or check. Verify the underlying observation; distinguish symptom, hypothesis and measured loss. A public signal does not establish account-level performance or permission for an intrusive scan.
- **Switching or cost comparison** (10, 12–14, 22, 34, 36–37, 42, 52, 59, 65, 69, 83): compare equivalent scope and full switching burden using authorized actual inputs. Keep estimates conditional; published rates alone do not establish what this prospect pays.
- **Outcome-linked compensation or payment timing** (2, 8, 17–18, 28, 46, 64, 70, 88, 92–94, 99): reduce a particular financial risk only where terms and economics support it. Define the payable event, attribution, qualification, exclusions, timing and dispute/refund rules. “Nothing upfront” is not “free” or “risk-free.” Obtain approval for material terms.
- **Prepared asset or implementation preview** (31, 39, 50, 60, 80, 86, 96): show a genuine artifact or offer a scoped build. Keep already-made vs proposed work distinct. Trials that touch systems require separately authorized access and implementation.

## Apply within the existing offer gate

1. Start with the approved paid service, buyer problem and fulfillment capacity. Choose at most three feasible patterns, not 100 superficial variants.
2. For each, record source example numbers, client-supported advantage, immediate deliverable, exact terms, required evidence/inputs, owner/capacity, paid-service connection and unresolved claims.
3. Reject fake scarcity, unverified diagnosis, fabricated completed work, unapproved guarantees and financial pre-approval claims. Financing, tax, insurance, legal, employment and security examples need appropriate expertise and current evidence; an example grants no action authority.
4. Select one material test lever. Measure qualified progression, fulfillment cost and downstream value alongside replies. Do not import the source’s numbers, deadlines or effectiveness claim as a benchmark.
5. Pass the chosen approved offer and claim limits to Scribe; retain existing review and launch gates. Do not change live campaigns from this reference update.
