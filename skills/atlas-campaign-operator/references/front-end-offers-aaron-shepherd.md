## Fifteen source examples, not approved promises

Retain these only as concept prompts. Quantities, deadlines, fees and guarantees below describe the source, not Dulcy capabilities or recommended terms.

1. Ad agencies: ten UGC creatives, a 72-hour launch, comparison with the current best ad and removal of losers.
2. YouTube agencies: one complete video, free to retain without a subsequent engagement.
3. Amazon agencies: rebuild the weakest ASIN within 48 hours using three competitors as context.
4. DTC email: rebuild and activate an abandoned-cart sequence in 48 hours, with a first-week order claim.
5. Recruiting: three screened candidates in five days, hiring-contingent fees and replacement terms.
6. Freight auditing: invoice-based refund recovery funded by a share of recovered money.
7. Property tax: assessment review with payment conditional on a reduction.
8. Medical billing: denied-claim recovery with collection-contingent compensation.
9. Dormant-lead reactivation: a seven-day CRM campaign with closed-deal-contingent payment.
10. Appointment setting: fees tied to qualified meetings that actually occur.
11. AI quoting: RFQ-to-sent-quote automation within 24 hours and a 30-day trial.
12. Voice AI: missed-call analysis with payment linked to calls recovered.
13. Logistics/3PL: an initial shipment at no charge plus an incumbent-rate comparison guarantee.
14. Lending: a short application and a 24-hour term-sheet offer without a call.
15. Commercial insurance: an incumbent-benefits comparison with a no-change alternative.

## Use selectively through existing contracts

Existing offer-patterns, capability, economics and promise-fidelity guidance already covers bounded samples, conditional fees, usable ownership, genuine inputs and transparent terms. Do not create a second offer hierarchy. The useful refinement in offer-framework.md separates production, transfer, deployment and demonstrated performance; distinguishes delivery timing from result timing; and requires a defined baseline before claiming an improvement. Scribe applies the same state distinctions in copy. These are our operational safeguards, not evidence that the source proved them experimentally.

Do not adopt the categorical claims that only the offer matters, audits/calls are inherently poor, every buyer has zero risk, or more risk reversal necessarily produces more replies. Keep targeting, delivery, copy, buyer state, fulfillment economics and downstream qualified outcomes in the diagnosis. Retain useful diagnostics, direct meetings and paid pilots where appropriate.

Do not promise recoverable refunds, overassessment, missed-call revenue, financing eligibility, insurance superiority or candidate availability without current qualified evidence and approval. Match comparisons on full scope and switching burden. Health, financial, employment and regulated-service examples require the appropriate professional and privacy boundaries. A trial does not authorize system access, sending quotes, contacting old leads, hiring decisions, paid ads, financial execution or accepting agreements. Preserve every current spending, QA, managed-stage, manual-review and human-send gate.
