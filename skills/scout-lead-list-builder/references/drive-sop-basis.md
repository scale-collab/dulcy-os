## Applied methods

1. Start with written ICP, campaign thesis, company constraints and exclusions. Verify company fit before extracting people. Resolve the appropriate decision-maker personas by firm size; do not collapse founders, senior marketers and junior coordinators into one message/segment.
   Sources: [Basic Lead List](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1A7MXx6IwsHKYg-DYtWwOy7x9kv4uVaSogdTZzGhdy0U/edit), [Advanced Lead List](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17q1qqRRMMpfOIgLGWow9Sev2vlNT8LuyWoKbhmUUHcs/edit).
2. Choose data source for the actual criterion: niche directories for specialized business types, StoreLeads for commerce, BuiltWith for tech, funding sources for funding, general B2B sources for broad roles/firmographics, Clay for enrichment. Document source/filter provenance in the campaign record. Vendor priority and database counts in old SOPs are time-bound; current available tools and approved workflow take precedence.
   Source: [Database Selection](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1D0E4vZ5kwiPlX_Bj0vuEkSx9DUVc5g9UNBPbnUeeZ6g/edit).
3. Advanced qualification: explicit criterion, company-level source evidence, separately labeled unknown/estimate, qualified company output, then contact/enrichment waterfall. A guessed revenue/location number or AI yes/no is not verified fit. Do not silently expand requested bounds. Avoid repeated company-level enrichments for multiple contacts from the same company; use stable company IDs/lookups.
   Sources: [Advanced Lead List](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17q1qqRRMMpfOIgLGWow9Sev2vlNT8LuyWoKbhmUUHcs/edit), [Legacy Advanced Lists](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1E-hbGP03qd3a5gtkZJJSHUzl3D4GBvFlFMwAFdriAqY/edit), [Clay SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1ds0vdJYjtEHic1W6tsOzKdgdi3FpQysvwy8466uF8cE/edit).
4. Separate verification layers: current employment and contact relevance, company relevance, email validity, recovery/enrichment and a final independent check where needed. Never send invalids directly; catch-all requires explicit risk treatment. Avoid paying twice for an enrichment already validated. Preserve original result, source and final best-email provenance.
   Source: [Triple Verification](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1sETxooYQGJ0wUWzpcpkbf3x8MlD9bJPt7nQG4N4fz3U/edit).
5. QA before full run: sample real rows across the list, inspect positive and negative classifications, ensure merge fields normalize naturally, check exclusions/suppression and source-specific edge cases. Legacy basic-list tolerance, Clay's 15–20 sample/85% example and the 2-hour score bands are reference workflows, not permission to mark unverified or below-current-standard rows sendable.
6. Investment/hiring/tech signals are relevance evidence, not buying intent. AWS does not establish that a business sells on Amazon. A LinkedIn About Us company-semantic check is supplementary company evidence, not contact verification. The high-accuracy SOP currently contains purpose/scope/tool requirements only, with no complete executable procedure.
   Source: [LinkedIn About Us SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MgpIlTqh1btr9HEAzC6TxhbVN6AQGcirV2lQwHtCJuI/edit).

## Relevant source conflicts

- Verification stack: legacy basic-list SOP uses EmailGuard → LeadMagic → MillionVerifier by credits; newer Triple Verify uses MillionVerifier then selective Clay recovery/LeadMagic and fit; EmailGuard navigation calls its verifier optional. Select the current approved workflow and document deviations.
- Legacy geography exclusions and guessed revenue/location bounds must not override the actual client-approved ICP. Keep evidence, estimate and unknown separate. No stereotype is a substitute for account fit.

## Normalization and derived-enrichment QA

The full available text of the [2-Hour Campaign compilation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pnNUb_SX_aid2fI9gNPX-U_DrnLXdUkcS7BIpYqAhsM/edit) was reviewed in the continued October 9 pass. Treat its formulas and enrichment examples as candidates requiring these checks:

- Preserve raw name, company and address/source evidence. Store cleaned display fields separately with the transformation and provenance. Test single-token and multi-part names, punctuation, non-ASCII text, suffixes and stylized brands; first/last-word formulas, title case and suffix removal must not silently redefine an identity.
- Treat a regex-extracted email as an address candidate, not verified employment, role, ownership, fit, permission or deliverability. Match the intended company/contact and complete independent verification. Label generic versus direct identities; do not globally favor or exclude functional mailboxes because an old formula does.
- Label website-derived keywords as suggestions unless actual current campaign evidence establishes bids. A public ad library shows visible ads, not their best-performing ranking. A page-speed measurement alone does not establish conversion or revenue loss. Pass observation, source/date, derivation, limitations and honest fallback to Scribe.
- A relevant verified business-phone field does not establish caller-ID display on every device or permission to call or text. Inspect raw-to-derived traceability, ambiguous/multiple addresses, missing values and rendered output before import.

## Closers.io outbound batch, October 11, 2026

For a hard-to-identify niche, consider an accessible professional directory, event/speaker list or business community as a seed source when ordinary database filters cannot express the criterion. Record the observed membership/activity and date; it is not proof of need, budget, intent or approved contact permission. Check actual company/person fit, exclusions and the current approved provider workflow before enrichment. Compare usable yield against research effort and approved cost without lowering the fit gate.

Keep alternate addresses attached to one resolved prospect identity. A second email does not create a new person or bypass prior-contact exclusions. Preserve raw source and channel-specific verification states; phone validity cannot make an invalid email sendable. Course advice to retain unknown/unverifiable email statuses is not a send-ready rule. Do not import its automatic extra-contact collection, paid multi-provider sequence, personal-profile matching or verification checkbox as a substitute for current managed review, approved data scope and manual NeedsReview.
