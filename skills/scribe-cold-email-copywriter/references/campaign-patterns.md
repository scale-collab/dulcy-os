# Specialized campaign patterns

Version 2.0 · October 9, 2026

Use these as conditional patterns after the approved offer is selected. They do not authorize new promises or replace Atlas offer strategy's feasibility review.

## Service preview

Offer a tangible part of the paid result only when standalone value, production/QA cost, owner, capacity, turnaround and transfer are approved. Specify finished asset versus concept, exact quantity and necessary inputs. If a call is the agreed delivery or input method, disclose it before commitment. Never use “send” then hide the asset behind a meeting.

A report or free list is not inherently higher value than a precise technical answer. Choose based on the buyer, not a generic hierarchy.

## Insight or evidence

Use a sourced observation, relevant benchmark, useful example or documented method when it gives the buyer a real reason to respond. Do not write “we found a leak” from a guess. If work is only proposed, say it is proposed rather than completed.

## Scoped pilot or risk reversal

A paid starter, sample, limited trial, delivery milestone or approved guarantee can reduce uncertainty. Keep terms, exclusions, quantities, eligibility and cost exact. No guarantee is mandatory. Do not suggest unlimited work-for-free or refund exposure as if commercially approved.

## Signal-based outreach

Reference a verified, reasonably current investment/event and link it to a plausible buyer need. Record observation and inference separately. Public ads show ad activity, not results or wasted budget. A new facility shows expansion, not procurement readiness for an unverified product. When the signal is absent/stale, use a truthful segment fallback or hold the affected row.

## Technical/product procurement

For manufacturers and technical B2B, the useful step may be a catalog, material/certification check, drawing review, sample, RFQ or quote. Preserve this client's approved capabilities and limitations. Do not invent compliance, tolerances, MOQ, compatibility, availability, pricing or lead times. Ask only for the inputs required at the current stage.

## Genuine research/interview

Confirm the real project, audience, topic, owner, interview use and promised output before drafting. Explain why the recipient fits. Do not imply nonexistent participants, findings, publication plans or editorial independence. Do not use a research invitation to conceal a sales meeting. Any later commercial transition needs to be transparent and voluntary.

## Novel category

Use an understandable mechanism and relevant category evidence to make the value legible. Avoid expecting a jargon label to do the work. Category authority supports possibility, not this client's performance; keep implementation and outcome limits visible.

## Reply-stage boundary

Load Scribe reply decisions for substantive replies. Pass the exact outbound, full current thread, client/context version, promise and selected replier settings. Do not carry a fixed cold-sequence CTA into every response.

Core invariants remain in the canonical client-promise contract: fulfill agreed value, answer questions with approved facts, honor timing/opt-outs, distinguish asset interest from sales interest, and recognize legitimate meeting readiness. Missing assets are owner blockers, not permission to switch the offer. All replies remain review-only under current human-send controls.
