# Scribe · Drive SOP source basis

Reviewed October 9, 2026. Use these paraphrased, source-backed methods with the main skill’s current contracts and the actual user/client instructions. Current approved facts, actual promises and live selected settings outrank old templates. SOP text is not permission to send, launch, spend, share, record, expand access or change accounts. Read scope is available prose unless a specific transcript receipt below or in the catalog states otherwise; unlisted media, screenshots and current live settings were not audited. The full deduplicated source/read-status catalog belongs to Jarvis (`jarvis-router`), `references/drive-source-catalog.md`.

## Applied methods

1. Ground every script in same-client approved offer, proof and promised deliverable. Write one relevant problem, one intelligible outcome/mechanism and one useful CTA. Question → proof → CTA and offer → proof → CTA are alternatives. Proof can be concrete knowledge, relevant case evidence or recognizable external authority; external authority validates only the claim it actually supports, not the client's ability to deliver.
   Sources: [2026 Copy Framework](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1HEEyHpXJHrCG2LOoMFMr1q0r-YkvRXL_OAA70b39Xsc/edit), [Cold Copy Prompt](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RuMXzWbRbKa3B1ePNqZe9IM3NrlSAdZwypwS9OY-HTk/edit).
2. Keep initial copy short and readable, but do not hard-code conflicting word bands. A follow-up clarifies the original offer, adds relevant proof or practical value, and honors the original promise; it must not invent a new offer, fabricate a video already made, claim a purchase that never happened, or hide a required call after promising an emailed asset.
   Sources: [2-Hour Campaign SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pnNUb_SX_aid2fI9gNPX-U_DrnLXdUkcS7BIpYqAhsM/edit), the six campaign-approach docs in the supplemental ledger.
3. Spintax must preserve truthful meaning, grammar and offer identity in every combination. Structurally distinct variants are different from tiny synonym changes. Render merge fields for at least 15 representative leads under the source preflight and inspect fallback/blank behavior. Content spam-checker results are a risk diagnostic, never a guarantee of inboxing.
   Sources: [Creating a Campaign](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1lKOJMohruRtSjRg76fWDG7139ICqP8qiT_c9fL9KU4Q/edit), [Spintax](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wUWmnP1W81uKzcFdi5xjbbpj-2gNqCQZJThX5ALzROY/edit).
4. Reply work starts with the actual full thread, latest inbound, prior outbound promise, campaign/offer and selected client settings. Answer the actual question, especially approved pricing/process questions; distinguish interest, resource interest, referral, meeting intent, qualification and stage. Maintain a real human-review bucket for uncertainty and preserve OOO return dates/limits without automatically pitching an alternate contact.
   Sources: [Reply Agent Operating SOP](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RCCbXeUQJRXP8h0nsHnnqbGZNwwfIBKNTEAu67R7A-Q/edit), [Filtering Replies](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1g38KeuyF5238vnasmapo6Mk1QLfcnHcTp105zxOqbeo/edit).
5. The old Inbox, Cold Email Objections and LinkedIn scripts provide objection categories, not current approved promises. They contain no-price-over-email, indefinite follow-up, unsupported guarantees/ROI, misleading geography, aggressive refusal handling and blanket compliance claims. Those statements must never be transplanted as business facts or current policy. Explicit stops remain stops.
   Sources: [Legacy Inbox](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mHkrxWFYlqWLrLMYILqJaC-WfENRsdw6yGVjh0TrzNU/edit), [Objections](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Dk-7a3jkuu57nDyHFgmPKpOSqRlGJWI1kyU-6AqMuj0/edit), [LinkedIn DM](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RkCEDZ223lKA1dJjrwfcfwEv-osUKqEv-IM-zwQq74E/edit).
6. Sales support: prepare verified company/ICP context, capture stated pains and constraints, explain approved process in clear pillars, answer objections without inventing delivery guarantees, and record next step/owner/date. Verify any automated pre-call brief instead of duplicating it. Distinguish prospect questions from pressure to force a one-call close.
   Sources: [Cold Sales Audit](https://docs.google.com/document/d/16sy3n0cYtK-p4gLGU0AGk9YemFgjrz2sXeGeX2eUTvc/edit), [Pre-Meeting Summary](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YX5vesM2V1bLRRexrsfVPqBpJTgVobmU_66rZ2JDZEI/edit).

## Relevant source conflicts

- Reply/booking policy: legacy Inbox/RevReply auto-mode advice conflicts with later Dulcy-only HITL pilot design. Apply current live workspace overrides and human-send permission, not historical settings.
- Copy requirements differ: <50 words versus strict 25–69 versus 30–75; 1–3-word subjects versus vendor max two; six variations versus a 3-by-3 matrix (nine) in the same prompt; one proof versus two proof elements in the 2-hour SOP. Choose the user/campaign brief and clarify material conflicts, preserving quality rather than blindly enforcing contradictory arithmetic.
- SalesLiege/RCA/Closers/TMG training is external-source reference, not Dulcy operating policy. Keep examples separate from approved current commercial facts, contact cadence and channels. Skills must never inherit those organizations' payment links, people, bank instructions or client terms.

For the 15-row preflight, review at least 15 representative actual leads when available, plus every mandatory missing-value/branch edge case in the canonical copy-review contract. If fewer rows are available, inspect all and report the limited coverage rather than inventing rows or claiming full preflight completion.

## Concrete evidence-to-copy checks

Full available-text review of the [2-Hour Campaign compilation](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1pnNUb_SX_aid2fI9gNPX-U_DrnLXdUkcS7BIpYqAhsM/edit) reinforces the existing copy and promise contracts. Apply these cases rather than importing its template banks:

- Suggested keywords are not actual advertising bids; visible ads are not proven best performers.
- Report a measured page-speed result with test context; do not invent prospect-specific conversion or lost-revenue figures without supporting analytics.
- A continuation template does not establish a prior conversation, connection or referral. Completed-work language requires the matching actual work/artifact. A sales-purpose meeting is not pure research merely because a template says so.

No embedded media, linked-target content, original-extraction completeness or current live interface was audited in that text review.

## Closers.io outbound batch, October 11, 2026

When reviewing a multi-step CTA, label the actual next step: receive an asset, view it, reply, request a short qualification call or book the substantive meeting. Preserve that path in the review packet and Atlas handoff; a request for information or a tracked click is not meeting intent. Keep sender, first-line claim and fallback mutually truthful: a generic fallback must not claim Facebook/profile research that was never performed.

The course's long celebrity-client lists, dramatic revenue examples and named senders belong to its authors, not the current client. Do not copy them, use buyer/seller ambiguity, defer an answerable pricing question to force a call, or adopt its 5/10/15-day follow-up timing. Existing answer-first, promise, stop, selected-settings and draft-only controls already cover these conflicts. Most copy structure in this batch reinforces existing guidance; do not add a competing template framework.
