# Sales Diagnostics

## Core funnel

For meeting-led sales, track at minimum. Also track substantive replies, open promises and fulfillment, and the approved client-specific stages:
- Calls booked
- Calls held
- Qualified opportunities
- Offers made
- Closes
- Cash collected

Useful rates:
- Show rate = held / booked
- Qualification rate = qualified / held
- Offer rate = offers / held or offers / qualified, depending on team convention
- Close rate = closes / held and closes / offers
- Cash per held call = cash collected / held

## Benchmarks from Dulcy materials

- Show-rate target: 80%+
- Cold-traffic close-rate review threshold: below 30%
- Qualified cold leads should generally be designed for a 1–2 call close when the decision maker is present and fit is real

Treat these as Dulcy-style agency heuristics, not measured universal benchmarks or promises. For manufacturing, technical, procurement-led, or longer-cycle clients, use their approved stages and relevant baselines.

## Bottleneck tree

### Booked calls are low
First distinguish too few relevant replies from poor conversion after replies. Atlas owns campaign offer/targeting/volume; Scribe sales coaching owns the reply-to-appropriate-next-step strategy with Scribe and Atlas client success. Inspect exact promises, asset readiness, answers, buyer intent, timing, and ownership before prescribing more call asks.

### Show rate is low
Check:
- confirmation speed
- calendar delay
- reminder sequence
- pre-call nurture
- meeting expectations
- prospect quality
- time-zone/calendar errors

### Held calls are high but qualification is low
Check:
- ICP accuracy
- titles/decision-maker quality
- campaign promise vs actual service
- pre-call qualification

### Qualified calls are high but offer rate is low
Check:
- rep is not leading the call
- discovery is incomplete
- rep is avoiding the ask
- qualification criteria are inconsistent
- solution is not being mapped to pain

### Offers are high but close rate is low
Check:
- insufficient pain/urgency established
- weak or irrelevant proof
- wrong stakeholder
- objection discovery happens too late
- price/value mismatch
- poor explanation of delivery
- lack of clear close
- offer itself is weak

### Close rate is acceptable but cash is low
Check:
- payment collection
- financing/payment schedule
- contract workflow
- sales-to-onboarding handoff
- verbal closes not converting to signed/paid

## Review method

When a metric is weak:
1. Compare it to the immediately previous funnel stage.
2. Inspect 3–10 recent examples from that transition.
3. Categorize failures rather than relying on one anecdote.
4. Fix the largest repeated failure first.
5. Re-measure after enough volume to matter.
