  - Spend 3–5x the target cost per call before cutting a test.
  - Creative angles matter more than audiences.
  - Only send qualified calls back to the ad platform.
  - Scale by duplicating campaigns, not by raising live budgets.
  - Ads suit large markets; under about 20k target accounts, cold outbound fits better (a course claim).
