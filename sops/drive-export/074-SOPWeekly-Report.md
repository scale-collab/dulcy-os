📊 SOP 1: Weekly Campaign Report
Title: Creating Weekly Campaign Reports for the Executive
 Role: Executive Assistant
Owner: [EA Name]
Effective Date: [Insert Date]
Last Reviewed: [Insert Date]
🧾 Objective
To provide the Executive with a clear, timely overview of current campaign performance, ensuring visibility into engagement, conversions, and trends on a weekly basis.
🧩 Scope
Applies to all active marketing campaigns (email, lead gen, etc.) tracked in ActiveCampaign or similar platforms.
________________


🔁 Workflow
✅ 1. Gather Weekly Campaign Data
Platforms:
* ActiveCampaign

* Google Analytics (if linked)

* Social platforms (if integrated)

Metrics to pull:
Metric
	Description
	Email Open Rate
	% of recipients who opened emails
	Click-Through Rate (CTR)
	% of clicks on links
	Conversion Rate
	% who completed goal (e.g., sign-up)
	New Leads Generated
	Total number from campaign
	Unsubscribes/Bounces
	Delivery and opt-out performance
	Top Performing Email
	Subject, date, and CTR
	🗂 2. Use Weekly Report Template (Google Docs/Sheets)
Format:
pgsql
CopyEdit
Title: Weekly Campaign Performance Report  
Date Range: [Start Date] – [End Date]  
Prepared for: [Executive Name]

- [Summary in 3–5 bullet points]

- Clean list to reduce bounce rate

📤 3. Share with Executive
   * Send by email every Monday morning
