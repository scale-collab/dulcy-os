How to Audit a Client’s Funnel SOP


Purpose of This SOP
The purpose of the Cold Funnel Audit is to ensure that every new client has the essential foundation needed to convert the meetings we book for them.
Most clients entering outbound have never optimized their funnel:
• Their LinkedIn is not optimized
• Their landing page does not convince
• Their scheduling funnel reduces conversions
• Their sales assets lack clarity
• Their reminders or follow-ups are weak
• Their automations are nonexistent
• Their social proof is poor
This audit identifies every weakness before we begin sending traffic, so their show-up rates and close rates increase — and we don’t lose clients who blame outbound for funnel problems.
________________


Cold Funnel Audit Overview
You will audit the following nine sections:
1. Business & Positioning Check

2. LinkedIn Profile Audit

3. Landing Page & VSL Audit

4. Calendar & Scheduling Funnel Audit

5. Sales Assets & Follow-Up Audit

6. Reminder & Automation Review

7. CRM & Lead Routing Audit

8. Conversion Asset Audit

9. Red Flags + Deliverables to Prepare

Each section below explains exactly what to look for and what standards the client must meet.
________________


1. Business & Positioning Check
You are answering one question:
Is this client easy to understand, credible, and aligned with a specific ICP?
Review the following:
Offer Clarity
   * The core offer must be understood in one sentence.

   * It should include:

• Who they help

• What outcome they provide

• What mechanism they use

ICP Alignment
      * Their messaging must speak to one clear persona.

      * Avoid “we help everyone” positioning.

      * Landing page & LinkedIn should match the audience they target with outbound.

Social Proof
         * Check that they have:

• Client wins

• Case studies

• Interviews

• Portfolio or track record

         * Thin or missing social proof must be called out.

Messaging Match
            * Their copy should match the language, pain points, and outcomes that matter to their ICP.

Value Proposition Speed Test
               * Their value should be understandable within 5 seconds of visiting their site or LinkedIn.

If the client fails any of these, note it and prepare recommendations.
________________


2. LinkedIn Profile Audit
Clients get researched on LinkedIn every time a meeting is booked, so this must be perfect.
Profile Image
                  * Must be a clean, professional, close-up headshot.

                  * No blurry photos, group shots, or distant photos.

Banner
                     * Should signal:

• Their offer

• Their ICP

• Their authority

                     * No generic nature photos, skylines, or random graphics.

Headline
                        * Should read:

“I help [ICP] achieve [Outcome] using [Mechanism]”

About Section
Must be:
                           * Value-driven

                           * Clear

                           * Short paragraphs

                           * Outcome-focused

                           * Written in plain English

                           * Ending with a call-to-action (“Book a call…”)

Experience
                              * Should reinforce credibility with real wins, case studies, or metrics.

Authority Signals
                                 * Recent posts

                                 * Results screenshots

                                 * Interviews

                                 * VSL

                                 * Podcast appearances

                                 * Project section filled out

If LinkedIn is weak → it will directly lower show-ups and close rates. Add it to recommendations.
________________


3. Landing Page & VSL Audit
This is where prospects often go before replying or before showing up.
Landing Page Standards
                                    * Loads fast

                                    * Passes the “5-second clarity test”

                                    * Clear outcome at the top

                                    * Matches the outbound offer

                                    * Shows instant credibility (logos, results, social proof)

                                    * Uses movement/scroll effects only if they support clarity

                                    * No clutter or confusing navigation

                                    * Should scroll to sections instead of opening new pages

                                    * Includes a clear CTA to book a call

                                    * Includes a short VSL

VSL Standards
The VSL should:
                                       * Be 5–12 minutes

                                       * Build trust

                                       * Explain the process simply

                                       * Include pricing overview

                                       * Include social proof

                                       * Pre-handle common objections

                                       * Use simple visuals and plain English

                                       * Drive the viewer toward booking a call

A strong VSL dramatically improves close rates.
________________


4. Calendar & Scheduling Funnel Audit
Your goal: reduce friction and increase the likelihood they book AND show up.
Calendar Setup Should Include:
                                          * Time slots for the next 1–2 weeks only (not months out)

                                          * 6–10 qualifying questions (simple, not overwhelming)

                                          * Questions that give sales context without exhausting the lead

                                          * Clean branding

                                          * Clear description of the call

                                          * Working reschedule link

Confirmation Page
After booking, the user should land on a page with:
                                             * A short video preparing them for the call

                                             * Social proof

                                             * Key expectations

                                             * FAQs

                                             * “What to prepare” instructions

                                             * Authority signals

                                             * Resources they can explore before the call

This page alone increases show-up rates significantly.
________________


5. Sales Assets & Follow-Up Audit
Your job: verify that after someone books, the client is fully prepared to convert.
Check that the client has:
Post-Booking Page
                                                * A video (5–15 minutes) that:

• Reintroduces the founder

• Explains the process

• Shows pricing

• Shows case studies

• Sets expectations

• Pre-handles objections

Email Reminders
Should include:
                                                   * Confirmation email

                                                   * 24-hour reminder

                                                   * 4-hour reminder

                                                   * 15-minute reminder

                                                   * “Meeting starting now” message

Text Reminders
Minimum:
                                                      * Confirmation text

                                                      * 24-hour

                                                      * 15-minute

Optional:
                                                         * AI or team member confirmation call the day before

If these are missing → show-up rates will suffer.
________________


6. Reminder & Automation Review
Verify that the client has:
Essential Automations
                                                            * New lead → CRM stage

                                                            * Booked call → CRM stage

                                                            * No-show → Trigger reschedule workflow

                                                            * Post-call follow-up sequence (6–12 months)

                                                            * AI or human-based no-show recovery

                                                            * Lead routing (no duplicates)

                                                            * Sync between booking tool and CRM

If no CRM exists → this is a critical red flag.
________________


7. CRM & Lead Routing Audit
The CRM must have stages for:
                                                               * New

                                                               * Booked

                                                               * No-show

                                                               * Showed

                                                               * Follow-up

                                                               * Closed won

                                                               * Closed lost

                                                               * Nurture

                                                               * Upsell/downsell opportunities

If stages are missing → sales attribution becomes impossible.
________________


8. Conversion Asset Audit
These assets must be prepared ahead of time:
                                                                  * Proposal template

                                                                  * Offer breakdown

                                                                  * Pricing document

                                                                  * Payment link

                                                                  * Contract template

                                                                  * Case studies (text + video)

                                                                  * Testimonials

                                                                  * FAQ sheet

                                                                  * Objection-handling materials

If these do not exist → the sales process is slow and fragile.
________________


9. Red Flags to Call Out
You must explicitly call out any of these:
                                                                     * Landing page not clear

                                                                     * Offer too vague

                                                                     * Weak or missing social proof

                                                                     * Bad LinkedIn profile

                                                                     * No VSL

                                                                     * Calendar questions too long

                                                                     * Calendar questions too short

                                                                     * No reminders set up

                                                                     * No CRM

                                                                     * No follow-up system

                                                                     * No sales assets

                                                                     * No nurture sequences

                                                                     * Broken links

                                                                     * Confusing branding

                                                                     * No authority content

                                                                     * Non-existent differentiation

                                                                     * Hard payment process

Red flags must be documented and shared with the client.
________________


Deliverables to Prepare After the Audit
Once the audit is complete:
                                                                        1. Write a clear summary of what must be improved.

                                                                        2. Turn each improvement into a client task.

                                                                        3. Organize these tasks by priority.

                                                                        4. Set deadlines and owners.

                                                                        5. Update expectations for launch.

                                                                        6. Get final approval from the client before campaigns start.

This ensures the client knows exactly what to fix and that nothing delays launch.
