Once a sale is closed there are four things you want to do on the call.
* Send xodosign terms
* Send link to Skool group (and accept them)
* Create support group chat on whatsapp
* Advising on coaching call times & getting commitment for a coaching call
Here’s what to say once the payment link is completed and the sale is closed…
“OK, so in terms of next steps we have three things to do. First, I need to send you our terms via xodosign, then we’ll get you in the Skool group, get your support chat sorted and also see if we can get you in for one of the next upcoming group coaching calls. Just to confirm the best email to send that agreement to is ….., right?”
XodoSign
Once the client has paid we need to send terms of business to them via XodoSign. We cannot send Program access until they have signed the XodoSign agreement. You ideally want to do this AFTER they have paid during the call.
1. Log into eversign.com
ASK MAX FOR LOGIN DETAILS
1. Go to templates > SalesLiege Agreement > ‘use’
2. Add the prospects name and best email address (if multiple decision makers you can add multiple, but I personally have never used this feature as we only need one signature and it makes the process easier and smoother)
3. Click send
Download the terms and store them on your computer here (this will be helpful if the prospect wants to see the guarantee in writing before buying on the call)…
SalesLiege Agreement
1. Let the prospect know…
“The guarantee is at section 10. The rest of it is quite long and boring - apologies for that, it’s just the price of selling information online and we have to make sure people don’t resell our stuff. If you have any questions about the terms don’t hesitate to ask, you’ve got my email, right?”
If the prospect has contract related questions be sure to ask us first before answering. Eventually you’ll know the answers to common questions. It’s rare prospects question the agreement (maybe 5-10%).
Skool Group
1. Ask the prospect if they’re happy to join the Skool Group.
2. Send the Skool group link in the Google Meet chat and ask them to request to join.
ASK MAX FOR LINK
1. Accept them into the group and tell them they can introduce themselves to the group later, if they want.
If you’re not a Skool group moderator yet, let us know and we’ll make you one.
Creating WhatsApp Support Group Chats
All of our support goes through WhatsApp business and no other channel. If they want to email, slack or facebook message, we will not reply and they need to know that. If they don’t have WhatsApp, ask them to download it. If they ask why, just say it’s because we are managing multiple different clients and it’s impossible to do that when you have multiple platforms or channels of support (the truth).
1. Tell the prospect we offer support Group chats via whatsapp. This is a group chat between them, Max & Isaiah.
2. If they have WhatsApp, ask them for their MOBILE number (or cell, in USA).
3. If they do not have WhatsApp, ask them for their MOBILE (or cell) number and request they download it right now.
4. Before the next step, open WhatsApp on your phone and confirm there is a WhatsApp account associated with their number. If there isn't, make sure they have created one. It’s essential a real account exists before the next step.
5. Type their number(s) into our Team WhatsApp channel alongside their name(s) and date signed.
E.g. 02/06/24 / +1 616 215 9012 / Max Ross / VERIFIED
Ask if they have any partners of team members they want to be part of the chat too. If they do, ask for the number and name and include this in the same message in the WhatsApp team channel.
E.g. 02/06/24 / +1 616 215 9012 / Max Ross / VERIFIED & +1 615 442 1352 / Isaiah Lebron / VERIFIED
(Verified means you know there is an account associated with their number)
