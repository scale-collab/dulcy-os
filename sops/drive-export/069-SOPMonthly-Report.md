📆 SOP 2: Monthly Report
Title: Creating Monthly Marketing Summary Reports
 Applies To: Executive + Cross-functional visibility
🧾 Objective
To provide a strategic snapshot of overall marketing performance across all campaigns and activities each month, highlighting patterns, ROI, and team contributions.
________________


🔁 Workflow
✅ 1. Consolidate Weekly Reports
* Summarize data from the last 4 weekly reports

* Identify cumulative trends or recurring issues

✅ 2. Add Monthly Campaign Summary
Section
	Content Example
	Campaign Overview
	Campaign names + objectives
	Engagement Summary
	Avg open rates, CTRs, conversion %
	New Leads
	Total leads acquired
	Budget vs. Spend
	% of campaign budget used
	Growth vs. Last Month
	Open rates up 6%, CTR flat, conversions +12%
	✅ 3. Include Charts & Visuals
   * Use Google Sheets to build graphs (bar, line, pie)

   * Show trends over 30 days

   * Highlight standout campaigns or underperformers

✅ 4. Add Executive Summary
At the top of the doc:


Summary:
- Lead generation exceeded target by 15%
- Top campaign: Product Launch Series (54% open rate)
- Key risk: Bounce rate increased on cold list – clean next cycle


✅ 5. Share Report
      * Due by the 5th of each month

      * Email with subject: 📊 Monthly Marketing Report – [Month]

      * Include links to Google Doc + Google Sheet dashboard
