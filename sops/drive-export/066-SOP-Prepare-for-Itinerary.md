🧾 SOP: Preparing the Travel Itinerary
Role: Executive Assistant
Owner: [EA Name]
Effective Date: [Insert Date]
Last Reviewed: [Insert Date]
________________


Objective
To compile and deliver a clear, comprehensive travel itinerary that covers all transportation, accommodation, meetings, and relevant information for the executive’s smooth travel experience.
________________


Workflow
1. Gather All Travel Details
* Flights, hotels, ground transportation

* Meeting times and locations

* Contact details for all bookings and key personnel

2. Create the Itinerary Document
   * Use a clear format (Word, Google Docs, or preferred tool)

   * Include:

      * Flight info (airline, flight number, times)

      * Hotel details (name, address, phone)

      * Transportation arrangements (pickup times, contact info)

      * Meeting schedules with addresses and contacts

      * Important notes (visa, weather, dress code)

3. Review and Share
         * Confirm all info is accurate and up-to-date

         * Send itinerary to executive at least 24 hours before departure

         * Upload itinerary to shared calendar or travel app if applicable

________________


Best Practices
            * Keep itinerary concise but detailed

            * Highlight critical timings and contacts

            * Update promptly if changes occur
