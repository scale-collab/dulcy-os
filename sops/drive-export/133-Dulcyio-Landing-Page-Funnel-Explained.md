Dulcy.io Landing Page Funnel


I’m going to break down exactly how the Dulcy.io funnel works, from the moment someone gets contacted to the minute they either sign or get sent into long-term follow-up.
You’ll see what channels we use, what each prospect sees, and how we handle them depending on what happens after the call.
Let’s get into it.


Pre-Step – How the Funnel is Built
  

The first section is a pattern interrupt. I’ve tested over the years and I’ve been testing this style currently for 2026. The movement  catches their eye and with the rise of AI you can tell there is something different. As soon as they scroll or by pressing the button see how it works it takes them to the VSL.
  

The video and land page is tailored specifically to the pains and vocabulary of marketing agencies. Through the landing page you’ll noticed that every section has at least 1 or more prompts to book a call. So, throughout the page they’ll have the option to book at any time.
  

The next section is trust + social proof through in person case study interviews. Once we hit our clients revenue goal from onboarding we will fly out to them and record an interview and take his/her team out to dinner.
  

We showcase the 6 most important benefits they receive from working with us including our yearly stats. This builds more curiosity into what it is that they do to generate these results. It also builds upon the most important factors that people ask or need to know.
  

Moving down our page goes into our USPs and informs them on how we connect all the systems together. Each section being a scrollable walks through the benefits and the deliverables of working with us. 
  

You continue down the page to the WHY US section and, make it fully transparent and obvious why are aren't even close to our competitors. 
  

Next section adds more social proof to our clients by exposing the strategies we use to generate results for them and can link any case study to the type of agency they run. Building authority and connection.
  

Movement is a really important part for websites to keep people engaged. We showcase the meetings and conversations we are having with our clients prospects and how that conversation is turning into a meeting.
  

We end the page with free resources on cold email (typically gamma’s from our youtube videos) and then link all of our videos here as well. Consistent Youtube videos is a huge point for agencies because it showcases that you are not just doing this as a service but explaining and teaching others daily on the topic. Continuing to build trust and authority. 
Step 1 – Where the Leads Come From (Our Outflows)
Here are all the channels we’re actively using to bring leads into Dulcy:
* Cold Email

* Paid Ads

* LinkedIn Organic

* Instagram Organic

* YouTube Organic

* Twitter Organic

* Cold Calling (SDRs/AI)

* Referral partnerships

All outbound leads go through unique Calendly links for attribution.
All inbound leads book through the main Calendly embedded on Dulcy.io.
  

Step 2 – What Happens After They Book
If it’s an inbound booking, we track everything using UTM parameters so we know which platform or campaign brought them in.
After they book, they get redirected to:
👉 dulcy.io/call-booked
That’s where they’ll see:
   * A detailed post-booking video (about 14 minutes)

   * A breakdown of what we’ll cover on the call

   * Case studies

   * And clear instructions on what to expect next

This helps build pre-call authority and gets people to show up educated and ready. We will cover how to build the pre-call VSL in the sales section however, this is an extremely key point to cold outreach. In this video I expose everything in 100% detail, on the technical set up, strategies, and process. This allows the prospect to ingest all the information super easily quickly and when they hop on the call I can just mention, did you watch the video in your email? 60% of the time they say yes and that makes the engagement and chance to close a lot greater. If not, no harm no foul, and we continue with the sales process as normal.
  

Step 3 – Backend Automations
Behind the scenes, here’s what happens:
      * Their info hits our CRM and Airtable via Make.com

      * Their lead status gets updated automatically

      * They receive a pre-call sequence via email
  
The pre-call email includes:
         * Zoom link

         * Reminder to watch the post-booking video

         * Info on what we’ll cover and how to prep

         * Social proof

         * Reschedule instructions if needed

Then they get automated reminders (Email + SMS):
            * 24 hours before

            * 4 hours before

            * 15 minutes before

            * At the meeting time

The reminder content stays the same,  we just switch the subject line timing.
We’ve developed an AI agent to also call the lead 24 hours prior to the meeting to confirm them showing up. Out of 400+ calls this year I’ve had about 5ish people mention that it was too much. The number weigh the odds for a 98% show up rate.
Step 4 – The Call Itself
At the scheduled time, the Zoom link in their email becomes active.  
Once the call starts, we walk them through their pains, their goals, and whether we’re a fit for each other.
If you have the Strategist plan,  I show real calls in the training portal so you can see how I frame and lead these.
After the call ends, the recording gets transcribed, and they receive a follow-up email with the recording link, kicking off our post-call workflow.
  

Step 5 – Follow-Up Sequences (Post Call)
After the call, what happens next depends on what went down on the Zoom:
✅ If the call was solid and we didn’t close, we will send a proposal:
               * They’re tagged as Proposal Sent

               * PandaDoc tracks when they open it and how long they view it

               * A 6-touch post-call sequence begins (over 16 days)

               * We also follow up manually and by phone every 2 days


  

🔄 If the call was solid but no contract sent yet:
                  * If a follow-up is scheduled, they get a reminder sequence up to the call date

                  * If they were warm but the budget was tight or under a certain circumstance, we’ll down-sell to either consulting, lead lists, build & release system, Agency Ignition or Deal Flux in some circumstances.

🚫 If they no-showed:
                     * They get dropped into a no-show sequence (4 emails + manual follow-ups)

                     * If no reply after that, we mark them as Lost


  

💤 If the call went quiet:
                        * After 16 days of post-call follow-up, they enter Long-Term Nurture

                        * That sequence runs for ~150 days

                        * If there’s still no reply, they get marked Lost (Allows us to retarget in future campaigns)
