Step 1: Determine If You Can Use Apollo for Lead Building
        1.        Check for Database Options: First, look up the lead type in our provided database list to see if it will work for the type of leads we want. If it’s not viable to build on an external database you can make it on Apollo; otherwise, refer to external scrapers when possible.
https://gamma.app/docs/These-databases-have-176B-leads-eu7o92sdsvrem4b
The reason for 95% of the team we build the lists with 3rd party databases is that Apollo doesn’t offer an easy way to filter specific company types.
For example, if you want to target Make-Up & Cosmetics Ecom brands, Apollo has some capabilities with filters, but they won't be perfect.
There’s no straightforward filter or tag to identify whether a company is an eCommerce store. You might try using keywords like “buy now” “add to cart,” or “online store,” but these strategies often result in lists that include companies that don’t fully meet your criteria.
However, with access to a platform like Storeleads, specifically designed for eCommerce leads, you can easily filter for Shopify stores or whatever criteria you’re looking for.

Additional Explanation for Pasting Website URLs in Apollo
When you’re building a list and need to paste website URLs from an external source into Apollo, keep in mind that Apollo can only process up to 6,000 URLs at a time. If you have more than 6,000 URLs, you’ll need to break them down into smaller batches. Here’s how:
        1.        Use CSV Breaker Tool on Lead Academy:
        •        Head over to the Lead Academy Discord and use the “CSV Breaker” command. /break-csv (explained in above in HOW TO USE SCRAPERS SOP)
        •        Upload your CSV file with all the URLs.
        •        This tool will split your list into chunks of 6,000 or less, making it easy to copy and paste each batch into Apollo.
        2.        Paste URLs in Apollo:
        •        Take each column website URLS and paste it into Apollo for a new search.
  

        3.        Complete Separate Searches:
        •        If the full list requires multiple searches, complete each one separately. Build out the exact search you want first for the first batch. Then you can duplicate the tab and just add the new company urls. You can combine the results once all batches have been processed and exported. 
  

Step 2: Build Your Lead List on Apollo
        1.        Set Filters on Apollo:
        •        Account (Company) Location: Set the geographic location based on our target market.
  

        •        # of Employees (to estimate revenue as reference):
        •        1-10 employees = $0-30k MRR
        •        11-20 employees = $30-100k MRR
        •        21-50 employees = $100k-$1M MRR
         •        51-100 employees = $1M+ MRR 
You can use custom range as well to get rid of very small business (solopreneurs) (ex. 2-50 employees)
*This also depends on the industry. Double check with chat gpt to verify. (ex. “If I want to target accounting firms making $5-30M a year, what would the average employee count range be?”)
  

        •        Industry & Keywords: Narrow down by industry and keywords related to the client’s needs.
  

        2.        Switch to ‘People’ for Further Filtering:
  

        •        Email Status: Select  “Verified” unless the list is very small (under 3000-5000).
  

        •        Job Titles: Common titles are CEO, CFO, CMO, COO, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Director, President, Co-Founder, Owner, Chair, Head, Board, Executive. 
*Adjust depending on industry and size.
**Some Clients want to target only marketing positions in a company or only sales so keep this in mind.
  

        •        Employee Location (if applicable): Adjust based on the client’s target region.
  

        3.        Create the Search Link: Copy the people-filtered search link to export later.
  

Step 3: Export Leads with ExportApollo
        1.        Go to ExportApollo: Log in at ExportApollo.  
        2.        Set Up Export:
        •        List Name: Use the client name and campaign to keep it organized. (EX. “Dulcy - Clutch PPC Agencies” *if multiple can add at end: 1/4, 2/4, etc)
        •        Paste Apollo Link: Insert the search link you copied from Apollo.
        •        Contact Count: Note the number of contacts from Apollo’s search result.
        •        Extra Data: Select the “extra data” option.
  
        3.        Scrape and Wait: This should take about 10-15 minutes. You’ll get an email notification when it’s done.
  

        4.        Open and Save the File: The exported list will be under history and you can open it all the way on the right. Then you can save it to Google Sheets for further processing.
  



Step 4: Verify Leads with Email Guard, Lead Magic, Million Verifier




When to use?


We use Email Guard for our verification. We get 50,000 contacts to verify per month. We use this first for contacts with emails. Now if the contact does not have an email (when you leave verified unticked), we will take these leads right to lead magic.


If we run out of 50,000 verification in a month then we will verify the leads with Lead Magic. If we have no credits in Email Guard or Lead Magic, then we will use Million Verifier.


* Check with Email Guard First, and then lead magic and then million verifier




EMAIL GUARD
        1.        Go to Email Guard: Head to Email Guard.
        2.        Drag and Drop File: Upload the file you saved from Google Sheets. Make sure the total emails found matches or is close to the total you exported.

  

3.        Monitor Progress: Wait around 15-30 minutes while Email Guard checks each email. 
        4.        Download Results: Once verified, download the file and keep only those that contain valid.


  





5. Open Sheet: Filter by only the ones that contain valid and delete the others.
  
  



Set the rows starting at 2 to keep row 1. Remove the non-valid emails from the list.






LEAD MAGIC


Lead Magic can be used for 3 things. Verifying emails, finding emails, or finding mobile phone numbers. In this guide I’ll be teaching you how to verify emails and find them.


Verifying Emails:


        1.        Go to Lead Magic: Head to Lead Magic.
        2.        Drag and Drop File: Upload the file you saved from Google Sheets. Make sure the total emails found matches or is close to the total you exported.

  

3.        Monitor Progress: Wait around 15-30 minutes while Lead Magic checks each email. 
        4.        Download Results: Once verified, download the “Good Emails Only” list from the results page.


  



  



Follow the same steps as above to filter only valid emails.


Finding Emails:


        1.        Go to Lead Magic: Head to Lead Magic.
        2.        Drag and Drop File: Upload the file you saved from Google Sheets. Make sure it is the sheet with no emails found.
  

Map out First Name, Last Name, Company Name, and Company Domain


Repeat steps as above for the filtering.
