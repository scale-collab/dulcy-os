🧾 Objective
To ensure all flight bookings for the executive are handled with efficiency, accuracy, and attention to preferences, while maintaining flexibility, cost control, and executive satisfaction.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP covers the end-to-end process for booking domestic and international flights for the executive, including research, selection, confirmation, itinerary management, and changes.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
* Gathering and confirming travel details with the executive

* Researching and selecting optimal flight options

* Booking flights according to preferences and policies

* Confirming all details and sending travel itineraries

* Monitoring for changes, delays, or cancellations

* Updating calendar and travel tools accordingly

________________

🔍 2. Search & Compare Flights
Use the following platforms:
   * Google Flights (comparison)

   * Airline websites (for best fares and upgrades)

   * Travel platform (e.g., Navan, Amex GBT, Concur if used)

   * SeatGuru (for seat selection insights)

Criteria to prioritize:
      * Nonstop flights > 1-stop

      * Early morning or mid-day over red-eyes (unless preferred)

      * Strategic airport for ground transport ease

      * Time buffer for meetings or event check-ins

      * Minimum 90-minute layovers (domestic), 2+ hours (international)

Create a shortlist (2–3 options) and share with the executive if needed for approval:

🛫 3. Book the Flight
Steps:
         1. Log in to airline or travel management account

         2. Use frequent flyer number (stored in travel profile)

         3. Select seat based on preferences:

            * Aisle/window, front of cabin, extra legroom

               4. Add baggage if required

               5. Use corporate card or pre-approved payment method

               6. Enter correct traveler info:

                  * Full name as per ID

                  * Known Traveler Number / TSA PreCheck

                  * Contact info (phone + email)

✅ Double-check all details before submitting.
________________

📆 5. Add to Calendar & Tools
                     * Add all flights to the executive’s calendar (Motion/Google/Outlook):

                        * Use correct time zones

                        * Title: Flight – SFO to JFK (United 1234)

                        * Include booking ref, seat number, check-in link

                        * Add time buffer before and after (e.g., 90 mins before departure)

                           * Add to travel tracker (e.g., TripIt, TravelPerk, spreadsheet)

                           * Share flight info with:

                              * Internal team (if needed for coordination)

                              * EA backup (in case of emergencies)

                              * Driver/transportation if scheduled

________________


🔁 6. Monitor & Manage Changes
                                 * Enable flight alerts via airline app or TripIt

                                 * Monitor for:

                                    * Delays, cancellations

                                    * Gate changes

                                    * Auto-upgrades or rebookings

If flight is canceled/delayed:
                                       1. Immediately notify executive with options

                                       2. Rebook as necessary

                                       3. Update all relevant tools and calendars

________________


🛑 What NOT to Do
                                          * Don’t book nonrefundable tickets unless approved

                                          * Never assume travel preferences — always confirm if unsure

                                          * Don’t send itinerary with missing info (seat, time, confirmation)

                                          * Avoid tight connections or short notice departures unless cleared

                                          * Don’t forward airline confirmation without context or calendar update
