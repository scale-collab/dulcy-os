🗓️ Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Setting Tasks for the Executive in Motion
* Role: Executive Assistant

________________


🧾 Objective
To ensure that all tasks, priorities, and deadlines for the executive are accurately created, categorized, and maintained in Motion, supporting optimal time management and productivity.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all professional and approved personal tasks entered into Motion on behalf of the executive, including daily action items, recurring responsibilities, project deadlines, and meeting preparation.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
   * Translating executive’s goals, calendar events, and communications into actionable tasks

   * Prioritizing and scheduling tasks appropriately in Motion

   * Monitoring task status and completion

   * Communicating changes, overdue items, or blockers to the executive

   * Updating or archiving completed, canceled, or outdated tasks

________________


🔁 Workflow: Setting Tasks in Motion
✅ 1. Identify Tasks
Sources of tasks include:
      * Emails (direct requests or follow-ups)

      * Executive meetings and calendar events

      * Slack, Teams, or internal tools

      * Verbal directives or daily stand-ups

      * Weekly or monthly planning sessions

Organize the task input as:
Task Type
	Examples
	Immediate
	“Review investor memo by EOD”
	Scheduled
	“Board meeting prep – due Friday”
	Recurring
	“Weekly team check-in agenda – every Monday”
	Delegated/Tracking
	“Follow up with legal on contract edits”
	________________


🧠 2. Clarify Task Parameters
Before creating in Motion, confirm:
         * Deadline or due date

         * Estimated time to complete

         * Priority level (High, Medium, Low)

         * Dependencies or blockers

         * Project or category (if using labels/folders in Motion)

________________


🏗 3. Create Task in Motion
In the Motion web or desktop app:
            1. Click “+ Task” or use shortcut (e.g., Cmd/Ctrl + K, then "Add Task")

            2. Enter:

               * ✅ Task Title: Clear, concise (e.g., “Review Q3 Forecast Slides”)

               * 📅 Due Date & Time

               * ⏱ Estimated Time (e.g., 30 min, 1 hr)

               * 🚦 Priority Level

               * 📂 Project or Category Tag (e.g., "Finance", "Board Prep")

               * 🔁 Recurring Settings (if applicable)

               * 📎 Notes/Details: Add links, context, attachments, or subtasks

                  3. Set to “Auto-schedule” or manually drag into open time slots

                  4. Click Save

________________


🧑‍💼 4. Assign & Monitor
                     * Assign task to the executive

                     * Add collaborators or watchers if others are involved

                     * Use comments to track updates or clarify deliverables

                     * Monitor the Motion dashboard to ensure tasks are scheduled

                     * Alert the executive if any task is unscheduled, overdue, or conflicting

________________


📤 5. Communicate Daily/Weekly Tasks
Share a daily or weekly digest of scheduled tasks.
📧 Daily Brief Template:
Subject: ✅ Daily Tasks – [Date]
Hi [Executive Name],
Here are your tasks for today, as scheduled in Motion:
                        * 🕒 9:30 AM – Review QBR Deck (30 min)

                        * 📞 11:00 AM – Call with [Client Name] (prep slides)

                        * 📂 1:00 PM – Approve Budget Reforecast

Let me know if you'd like to reprioritize or shift anything.
________________


🔁 6. Update or Reschedule as Needed
                           * Adjust time estimates or deadlines if tasks shift

                           * Cancel or archive completed items

                           * Move overdue tasks to new time slots or reprioritize

                           * Flag tasks that require escalation, input, or resourcing

________________


❌ What NOT to Do
                              * Don’t overload the calendar with back-to-back tasks

                              * Avoid vague task names like “misc admin”

                              * Don’t assign tasks without confirmed context or timing

                              * Don’t leave overdue tasks unmonitored or unaddressed

                              * Never auto-schedule tasks marked “High Priority” without confirming alignment
