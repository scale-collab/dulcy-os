Quick Summary of Us: Dulcy is a lead generation agency helping B2B service based businesses get more sales opportunities through cold outreach. We run cold email campaigns for ourselves and our clients as our service. The order is we reach out to a prospect and sign them as our client, then set up campaigns to send emails on their behalf to their ideal prospect. We communicate with their ideal prospect and book in a meeting with the prospect for our client.


The primary responsibility of this role involves following detailed instructions and performing the tasks in a timely manner.
