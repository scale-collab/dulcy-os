﻿Next Steps for Your Campaigns
Phase 1: Onboarding & Setup (Days 1-2)
        •        Onboarding Call Completed – Ensure all necessary details are collected.
        •        Provisioning of Domains & Email Accounts
        •        Purchase and set up 50 domains and 100 Email Accounts for sending.
        •        Configure domain and email account for outreach.
        •        Credentials & System Access
        •        Ensure access to Slack, Dulcy, and any necessary platforms/documents.
Phase 2: Research & Lead List Development (Days 3-7)
        •        Target Market Research
        •        Analyze ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) and industry data.
        •        Identify pain points and key outreach angles.
        •        Conduct in-depth market research to understand the mindset, pains, desires, and decision-making process of potential customers.
        •        Use targeted questions to refine messaging based on real ideal customer experiences.
        •        Lead List Compilation
        •        Use SmartScout, Store Leads, and other databases.
        •        Validate and enrich data for accuracy.
        •        Identify high-intent leads that match the ICP.
Phase 3: Cold Email Campaign Creation & Infrastructure Setup (Days 7-14)
        •        Setup Email Sending Infrastructure
        •        Configure warm-up sequences for all accounts.
        •        Implement SPF, DKIM, and DMARC for deliverability.
        •        Test inbox placement and monitor email health.
        •        Develop Cold Email Campaigns
        •        Build structured outreach sequences based on market research insights.
        •        Craft hyper-personalized messaging to align with customer pain points and motivations.
        •        Segment outreach based on lead characteristics for tailored engagement.
        •        Start sending 5 emails per account per day and gradually scale to maintain high deliverability.
Phase 4: Campaign Optimization & Testing (Days 14-20)
        •        Optimize Email Copy & Messaging
        •        A/B test subject lines, email content, and CTAs to determine what resonates best.
        •        Analyze engagement metrics (open rates, response rates, meeting bookings).
        •        Adjust messaging based on initial results to improve conversion rates.
        •        Testing Audience Segments
        •        Experiment with different personalization approaches (SKU mentions, competitor references, product pain points).
        •        Identify the best-performing outreach angles and refine targeting.
        •        Iterate & Improve
        •        Optimize sequences based on real-time campaign feedback.
        •        Implement automation tweaks to enhance engagement and response rates.
        •        Ensure ongoing alignment with client goals and ICP adjustments.
Phase 5: Full Outreach Launch (Days 21-35)
        •        Gradually Increase Email Volume
        •        Increase from 5 → 10 → 15 emails per account per day.
        •        Monitor Campaign Performance
        •        Weekly reporting on open rates, replies, and booked meetings.
        •        Adjust messaging and targeting based on initial responses.
        •        Test Additional Personalization Angles
        •        Experiment with different outreach styles and subject lines.
Phase 6: Optimization & Scaling (Days 35-60+)
        •        Analyze Campaign Data
        •        Identify high-performing subject lines, copy, and audiences.
        •        Scale Sending to Maximum Capacity
        •        Adjust email volume based on campaign performance.
        •        Refine Targeting & Expand Lead Lists
        •        Continue prospecting and testing new lead sources.
Ongoing Support & Reporting
        •        Slack Notifications & CRM Integration
        •        Real-time updates on campaign performance.
        •        Weekly Reporting
        •        Comprehensive reports sent every Friday.
        •        Continuous Optimization
        •        Adjusting strategy based on real-time insights.
        •        Bi-Weekly Client Check In Calls
        •        Anytime the client wants to discuss current outreach performance or new ideas


Dulcy Multi-Channel Outreach Flow
Email Outreach Sequence
        1.        Dulcy Email Sending Provider → Positive Reply → Your CRM (If no inbox management)
        2.        Email Reply Handling → Respond within 5-10 minutes
        3.        If Meeting Request is Ghosted → Begin 3-Day Email Follow-Up Sequence
AI Employee (Call & SMS Outreach)
        1.        AI SMS → Check if within 9-5 business hours
        2.        DND Verification & Tagging
        3.        Test 3 Different AI Voices for Performance
        4.        Call Attempt → Answer or No Answer → Catagorize Accordingly
        4.        Call Sequence → <30m Call → 24H Call → 19H Call → 44H Call
LinkedIn Outreach
        1.        View Profile
        2.        Follow Prospect
        3.        Engage with Post (Like Posts)
        4.        Send Connection Request
        5.        Send Message Sequence After Accepted: Message 1 → (2D Later) → Message 2 → (3D Later) → Message 3
        6.        Action Step Sequence If Not Accepted: Like Post → View Profile → Like Post → View Profile → Like Post → View Profile 
RB2B
        1.        Capture Active Website Visitor
        2.        Export Data to Clay
        3.        Verify ICP + Competitor Research
        4.        Sendspark Integration
        5.        Dulcy Website Visitor Campaign
