Why Do This Task?
When someone purchases a lead list on our website, it’s essential to make sure they receive exactly what they ordered. Handling these tasks promptly ensures that the client gets their leads quickly and accurately, boosting customer satisfaction and efficiency.

Tasks for Handling Lead List Orders
        1.        Responding to Questions
        •        If a question comes in from the “Question Form” on the website, reply to it as soon as possible. A Slack notification will be sent.
        •        If you don’t know the answer, ping the team in Slack and let them know about the question.
***Talk With Our Team! (LEADS REQUEST)***  
        2.        Follow-Up on Order Form Completion
        •        Once an order is placed, the client is directed to an order form. A task will also be created in Motion to begin a lead list project. Please start the project promptly.
        •        If the client does not complete the order form within 24 hours, send a follow-up reminder email.
        •        Continue following up every 2-3 days until they complete the form. Remember, completing the form is necessary to start building their lead list.
        3.        Review the Completed Order Form
        •        When the order form is filled out, check for any special instructions or details.


***Lead List Creation Form (ORDERS)***  
        •        Take the information from the order form and apply it to the Creating Lead Lists and Verifying Leads SOP and (which covers filtering, formatting, and exporting the lead list).

        4.        Format Job Titles
        •        As part of the lead list building process, use the “format job titles” command in the Lead Academy Discord server.
        •        Instructions for using this tool can be found in the Creating Lead Lists and Verifying Leads SOP or HOW TO USE SCRAPERS SOP.
        5.        Notify the Team
        •        Once the lead list is complete, notify the team in Slack. Include the company name and the client’s name so everyone knows which order has been fulfilled.


Additional Notes
        •        Be sure to check the Creating Lead Lists and Verifying Leads SOP for step-by-step instructions on filtering, exporting, verifying, and formatting lead lists.
        •        Keep communication prompt and clear with the client to ensure their satisfaction.
