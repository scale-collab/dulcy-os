﻿Google Calendar Configuration
Introduction
You will be given an email account associated with Google calendar. Once you have received this these are the steps to configure your calendar and set everything up.
Prospects will book open slots you have in your calendar, so if you can’t make a certain date or time make sure you block it out. For example, let’s say you are due to have an afternoon to drive up and see a family member. Make sure it’s blocked out in the calendar, otherwise prospects will book that time.
