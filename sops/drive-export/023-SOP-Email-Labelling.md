🧾 Objective
To implement a clear, standardized labeling system for managing and categorizing emails in the executive’s inbox to ensure visibility, actionability, and prioritization of critical communication.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all incoming and outgoing emails in the executive’s business email account, handled by the Executive Assistant (EA) for sorting, labeling, responding, or escalation.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
* Monitoring the inbox throughout the day

* Categorizing each email with the appropriate label

* Applying relevant sub-tags (e.g. Invoice, Receipts, Post Call Notes)

* Ensuring urgent and important matters are surfaced to the executive promptly

________________


🗂️ Labeling & Flagging System
The following color-coded labels must be applied to each email as per priority and action type.
________________


🔴 Urgent – Needs Executive Input ASAP
Criteria:
   * Requires executive decision, approval, or reply

   * Time-sensitive client/vendor issues

   * Legal, financial, or high-stakes communication

Action:
      * Flag and immediately notify the executive via Slack/SMS

      * Draft response (optional) and place in “Review” if time permits

Sub-Tags (Use in subject line or folder structure):
         * 🔴 Urgent - Invoice

         * 🔴 Urgent - Post Call Notes

         * 🔴 Urgent - Minutes of Meeting

________________


🟡 Review – Requires Executive Review Before Sending
Criteria:
            * Emails EA can draft but needs executive review

            * Non-urgent approvals

            * Strategic decisions or offers that require exec tone

Action:
               * Draft a response and label “🟡 Review”

               * Slack executive with summary and location in inbox

               * Follow up if no action within 24 hours

Sub-Tags:
                  * 🟡 Review - Invoice

                  * 🟡 Review - Receipts

                  * 🟡 Review - Meeting Notes

________________


🟢 Actioned by EA – Fully Manageable by Assistant
Criteria:
                     * Routine confirmations, scheduling, admin follow-ups

                     * Travel, expense submissions, form completions

                     * Responses within EA’s scope

Action:
                        * Reply, complete, or archive as needed

                        * Copy executive only if informative

Sub-Tags:
                           * 🟢 EA Actioned - Invoice

                           * 🟢 EA Actioned - Receipt

                           * 🟢 EA Actioned - Post Call

________________


🔵 For Information Only – FYI for Executive
Criteria:
                              * Executive should be aware but no action is needed

                              * Newsletters, board updates, CC’d convos, company updates

Action:
                                 * Label and file to relevant folder (e.g., “Industry Updates”)

                                 * No reply needed unless requested

Sub-Tags:
                                    * 🔵 FYI - Minutes of Meeting

                                    * 🔵 FYI - Internal Memo

                                    * 🔵 FYI - Industry Article

________________


⚫ Spam/Unimportant – Low/No Value
Criteria:
                                       * Promotional or cold emails with no strategic relevance

                                       * Duplicate threads

                                       * Irrelevant sales/outreach

Action:
                                          * Unsubscribe if safe

                                          * Mark as spam or archive

Sub-Tags:
                                             * No sub-tags needed unless consistent senders need to be blocked

🧠 Tips & Best Practices
                                                                              * Use rules/filters to auto-label common senders (e.g., Receipts, Calendars)

                                                                              * Use Slack or text for 🔴 Urgent follow-ups if no reply in 1 hour

                                                                              * Avoid duplicate labeling; only one priority label per email

                                                                              * Always read full thread before labeling

                                                                              * Confirm labels are applied before archiving any conversation
