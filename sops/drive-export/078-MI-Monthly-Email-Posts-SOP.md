﻿MI Monthly Email & Posts SOP


Objective:
Send out monthly email campaigns for birthdays and anniversaries for one of our old SMMA clients. Each month, add new customers from the Google Sheet who have signed up through the client’s website and whose date/time is not grayed out. Every week create 5 regular social media posts for the Metuchen Inn Restaurant.

1. Steps for Completing the Monthly Email Campaign


1. Log in to GoDaddy
        •        Go to GoDaddy and log into the account.
  

        •        In the top right, click on Account Settings and choose Delegate Access.
  

        •        Select Metuchen Inn and click Access Now.
  

        •        Under Your Business, click Metuchen Inn again.
  

2. Add New Customers to the Contact List
        •        On the left panel, go to Customers > All.
  

        •        Click on Add Contact.
  

3. Filter and Gather Customer Info from Google Sheet


Email Sign Up (Responses)
        •        Open the Google Sheet provided for this task.
        •        Apply a filter for the next month based on the birthday/anniversary column.
        •        Select customers whose date/time is not grayed out and add them to the list manually, one by one.
        •        Gather their name, email, and birthday/anniversary date. (If there is a duplicate you can fill the whole line red)
  

4. Enter Customer Information in GoDaddy
        •        Add each customer’s name, email, and birthday/anniversary date.
        •        Make sure to mark them as subscribed.
        •        Press Add to save each contact.
  



* Go back into the Google sheet and gray their name out. 


Set Up the Monthly Email Campaign


5. Navigate to Email Campaigns
        •        In the left panel, go to Marketing > Email Marketing.
  

6. Select the Right Campaign Draft
        •        Under Drafts, you’ll find two email templates: Birthday and Anniversary.
        •        Choose the correct template for the campaign you’re setting up (Birthday or Anniversary).
  

7. Update Campaign Details
        •        Scroll down and update the “Valid for Month of” field to the upcoming month.
        •        Click Save & Continue.
  

8. Schedule the Campaign
        •        On the Send To page, select Custom List and choose the list you just updated for birthdays or anniversaries.
  

        •        Click Next.
        •        Select Schedule for Later.
        •        Set the date to the 1st of the next month and time to 10:00 AM EST.
        •        Press Schedule to finalize.
  





Prepare for Next Month
9. Clone the Campaign for the Following Month
        •        Under Recent Campaigns, find the campaign you just scheduled.
        •        Click Clone Campaign.
  

        •        Update the name to reflect the next month’s validity (e.g., “Valid for December”).
  

        •        Go to Step 3 of the campaign setup, then exit with the X in the top right to save it as a draft.
10. Repeat for the Other Campaign Type
        •        Go back to Drafts and repeat the process for the other campaign (e.g., if you did Birthday first, do Anniversary next).
        •        Follow the same steps to schedule and clone it.


Important Notes
        •        Manual Entry Only: Enter each contact individually. Do not bulk import.
        •        Check with Team: If any issues arise ping the team in Slack for support.
        •        Campaign Timing: Make sure all campaigns are scheduled by the last day of the month for the following month’s birthdays/anniversaries on the 1st at 10 AM. No sooner than 2 days before the end of the month.
