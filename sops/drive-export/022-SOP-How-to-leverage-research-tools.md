🔍 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
How to Leverage Research Tools as an Executive Assistant
* Role: Executive Assistant

* Owner: [EA Name]

* Effective Date: [Insert Date]

* Last Reviewed: [Insert Date]

________________


🧾 Objective
To equip the Executive Assistant with a consistent and efficient process for using online and internal research tools to support the Executive’s decision-making, meeting preparation, project planning, and strategic insights.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all types of research conducted by the EA on behalf of the Executive, including but not limited to:
   * Market and industry trends

   * Competitive analysis

   * Event and speaking opportunity scouting

   * Lead or contact background checks

   * Vendor/service research

   * Travel and logistics

________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
      * Identifying the research goal and scope

      * Choosing the appropriate research tools

      * Gathering and verifying information

      * Organizing and summarizing findings in a clear format

      * Delivering actionable insights to the Executive

      * Citing sources and storing research in a central drive or Notion database

________________


🔁 Workflow for Conducting Research
✅ 1. Define the Research Goal
Ask yourself or the executive:
         * What specific question(s) are we trying to answer?

         * What is the purpose of the research (e.g., decision-making, briefing, planning)?

         * What is the deadline?

         * Who will use this research and how?

Document the scope in a brief:
vbnet
CopyEdit
Goal: Identify top 5 CRM tools for startup sales teams  
Deadline: Friday, June 21  
Context: Executive considering a new tool for Q3  
Output: 1-page comparison chart + summary with recommendation

________________


📥 3. Collect and Evaluate Information
Use the P.A.S.S. method:
            * Pertinent: Only capture relevant details tied to the research goal

            * Authoritative: Use credible, up-to-date sources

            * Summarized: Capture key data points and insights concisely

            * Sourced: Note the origin (URL, author, publication date)

Organize findings in a table, chart, or document format.

📤 5. Deliver the Research
Choose format based on the executive’s preference:
Format
	Use When
	1-page PDF Brief
	Fast review before a meeting
	Google Doc
	Longer summaries with links or context
	Table/Sheet
	Comparisons, pricing, contact tracking
	Notion Page
	Living document, ongoing reference
	✅ Always include:
               * A brief summary at the top

               * Hyperlinks to sources

               * Timestamp or “Last updated” note

               * Any unanswered questions or assumptions

________________


🧠 Best Practices
                     * Block focused time for research in your calendar

                     * Always verify data from at least two reliable sources

                     * Don’t overwhelm with too much detail—filter for what matters

                     * Use bullet points, tables, or highlights to increase scanability

                     * Keep a running list of trustworthy newsletters and research hubs

                     * Stay subscribed to industry alerts or trends (Google Alerts, Feedly, Substack)

________________


❌ What NOT to Do
                        * Don’t copy-paste chunks of content without context or summary

                        * Avoid relying solely on AI-generated research without validation

                        * Don’t store findings only in email or personal folders—centralize in Drive or Notion

                        * Don’t include outdated or unverified statistics

                        * Never share confidential research externally

________________


🔁 Review & Maintenance
This SOP should be reviewed every 6–12 months, or when:
                           * New research tools or platforms are introduced

                           * Executive feedback requires changes in formatting or depth

                           * Workflow efficiency needs to improve
