1. 5 minutes before the call, join the Google Meet. Copy and paste the Google Meet link, and send it to the prospect via email (from you@salesliege.com). If you have the prospect on Linkedin, Facebook or any other platform, send it to them there as well.


Hey name, here’s a link for our call due to start in 5 min.


(MEETING LINK)


Speak soon!
[yourname]


2. 5 minutes before the call, Open Skype and load in their number, ready to dial. If you’re dialling to the USA, make sure you have a USA number ready and loaded. If calling to UK/Europe, make sure you have a UK number loaded. Anywhere else, just use the USA number. You can change the number you’re calling from by going to dial pad > settings cog > caller ID > selecting number.


Bear in mind that area codes may change depending on where you’re calling, so you will have to select the country in skype. If you’re not sure which area code the country is, just put a + before copying the number in and it’ll probably auto populate.


3. If they haven’t joined the meeting by the minute the clock strikes the time you’re due to talk, call them. If they don’t answer, call them again. If they still don’t answer, text them saying...


Hey name, [yourname] with SalesLiege here. I’ve just emailed you a link to join me on Google Meet. Speak shortly.


If they still haven’t joined after 5 minutes, call them again twice and text them…


Hey name, you delayed? Can you make it?


If they still haven’t joined after 10 minutes, call them again twice and then text them…


I’ll wait in the meeting for 5 more mins, you still coming?








4. If they don’t show up after 15 minutes, close the meeting and email them by replying to your initial email with the google meet link…


Hey name, looks like you couldn’t make this one. Assuming you’re still up for a chat and want to grow your Agency, here’s a reschedule link…


GET LINK FROM MAX




5. Add the prospect to our no-show sheet:


SalesLiege No Show Prospects


You don’t need to send any follow-ups to get the prospect to reschedule, a VA will do this for you. 


EXTREMELY IMPORTANT: If a prospect reschedules, be sure to colour them in Green in the no show sheet. If you don’t do this, the VA will continue to follow-up and try to get them to rebook, pissing the prospect off heavily and reducing the chance of a show.


This applies for ALL prospects that reschedule as a result of not showing to the call. 


  





If the prospect does not show for the reschedule, leave it. Highlight them in red on the sheet and move on to the next prospect. We give prospects a single chance to show up for their reschedule. If they don’t, we won’t allow a second chance (unless understandable circumstances apply). We only allow for one reschedule.










6. Here’s how we prepare for the call. Over the next 45 minutes you could completely change someone's life and be the person who helps them take action towards extreme positive change and prosperity. Feels good, right?




Pre-Call Routine & Mindset
* This is a 2-3 minute routine you do before every call
   * Think of it as “re-priming” 
* The reason these our important is it allows us to “reset” after every call
   * Your biggest risk is letting one bad call turn into two bad calls…
   * Each call is it’s own “universe” 
* Triad (Tony Robbins)
   * Physiology / Language / Focus
* Our Pre-Call Routine
   * 6 Deep Breaths (if needed, take a quick walk outside // breathwork)
   * Become aware of how your body feels and your current self talk (surrender to it) 
   * Find something to be grateful for or appreciate
   * Set intentions: 
      * Ruthless detachment from the outcome
      * Commitment Of Getting To The TRUTH (transformation, pain, actions+words)
      * Have fun
