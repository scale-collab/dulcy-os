How to Manage Client Expectations Pre-Launch SOP
Purpose
To ensure clients fully understand the onboarding, setup, and pre-launch timeline so they feel confident, updated, and taken care of during the 14-day warm-up period before campaigns begin. This prevents client anxiety, lack of clarity, and unmet expectations.
________________


1. Overview of the Pre-Launch Phase
Once a client pays and completes onboarding, Dulcy has a 14-day setup and warm-up window before campaigns launch.
The purpose of this phase is to:
* Complete all backend setup

* Warm up domains & email accounts

* Build campaign theses

* Conduct research

* Configure tech & client workspace

* Maintain daily proactive communication

* Reduce churn risk through expectation management

Clients often assume “nothing is happening” during warm-up. This SOP ensures we eliminate that perception.
________________


2. Pre-Launch Timeline Breakdown (14 Days)
Day 0 — Payment + Onboarding
   * Client pays

   * Client receives onboarding form + portal access

   * You explain the setup window and expected launch date

   * Set tone: we are not a miracle switch — every part of the funnel matters

Day 1 — Introductions
      * Introduce yourself in the private Slack channel

      * Restate timelines, next steps, and expectations

      * Set the warm-up start date

Day 2 — Technical Setup Begins
Inform client of:
         * Domain ordering

         * Email account creation

         * Workspace creation

         * IP warm-up prep

         * Portal being populated with their folders, tasks, and resources

Day 3–7 — Warm-Up Begins + Portal Organization
Daily updates should cover:
            * “Email accounts added → warm-up begins today”

            * “Warm-up day count” (e.g., Day 3/14)

            * “Your client dashboard is set up”

            * “Your login credentials are uploaded”

            * “We are reviewing your assets and industry positioning”

Day 8–14 — Research + Campaign Thesis Development
Each day, provide updates such as:
               * “Completed industry research”

               * “Working on your first campaign thesis”

               * “Lead list segmentation underway”

               * “Scripts in draft”

               * “Awaiting your review on X asset”

On or before Day 14:
                  * Share campaign thesis in the portal

                  * Share scripts

                  * Share lead lists

                  * Align on launch timing

                  * Confirm everything is approved

________________


3. Warm-Up Date Management
Warm-up is usually exactly 14 days.
Example:
Warm-up start: Nov 5
Warm-up ready: Nov 19
Always calculate the exact date. Always tell the client the exact date.
Clients feel anxious if they think “nothing is happening.”
Warm-up dates provide certainty and structure.
________________


4. Slack Communication Expectations
Daily messaging is mandatory.
Even if no major progress occurs that day, send a brief update.
Example daily update types:
                     * Confirming warm-up day count

                     * Confirming asset review

                     * Letting them know something is “in progress”

                     * Informing them what to expect tomorrow

                     * Asking questions to move the project forward

Clients feel abandoned if they go 2–3 days without hearing from us.
Daily communication eliminates churn risk.
________________


5. Using the Client Portal During Pre-Launch
5.1 Tasks
Add tasks through:
Project Management → Client Tasks → Add New Task
Every request must become a task so the client can track progress.
5.2 Documents & Info
Everything a client sends must be uploaded here:
                        * Pitch decks

                        * PDFs

                        * Brand assets

                        * Offers

                        * Sales materials

                        * Lead lists

                        * Loom videos

Nothing should live only inside DMs.
If leadership needs to review something, it must be findable in under 30 seconds.
5.3 Onboarding Form
Review the form daily during setup:
                           * Offer

                           * ICP

                           * Logins

                           * Calendars

                           * Examples

                           * Past campaigns

                           * Assets

                           * Tools

5.4 Logins
All client logins must be entered in the “Logins” section:
                              * Email accounts

                              * Passwords

                              * CRM access

                              * Any relevant platforms

Transparency reduces friction and avoids back-and-forth messaging.
________________


6. Preparing the First Campaigns
What must be ready before launch:
                                 * Campaign thesis

                                 * Lead lists

                                 * Scripts

                                 * Loom walkthrough

                                 * Approval from the client

The thesis includes:
                                    * Why you’re using this angle

                                    * Why this ICP

                                    * Why this pain point

                                    * Expected positive reply patterns

                                    * Example emails

This should appear inside the client portal automatically once created.
________________


7. Key Principle: No “Silent Days” Allowed
The number one cause of early churn is silence.
Even if the update is small, you MUST message the client daily with something like:
“Quick update: warm-up day 6/14. Working on research for your campaign thesis. Tomorrow I’ll have your first ICP segment drafted for review.”
Small updates avoid big problems.
________________


8. Summary
During the 14-day setup period, your job is to:
                                       * Keep the client informed daily

                                       * Make the warm-up period feel active

                                       * Organize assets in the client portal

                                       * Build the campaign thesis

                                       * Prepare lead lists

                                       * Avoid uncertainty

                                       * Set the tone for the entire relationship

                                       * Ensure the client feels progress every single day

This SOP ensures the client never feels forgotten and shows them that the setup period is structured, intentional, and productive.
