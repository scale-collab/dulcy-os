1. Goal
When you start a new campaign, your first data task is to build a clean, basic lead list using our core databases:
* Apollo (or ALEADS – same logic)

* Clay

This SOP shows you how to build a solid, usable list fast without overthinking, and how to avoid obviously-bad leads before we ever send a single email.
________________


2. Prerequisites
Before you start lead list building, you must already have:
   1. ICP defined

      * Based on the Lowest Hanging Fruit criteria:

         * Massive pain

         * Purchasing power

         * Easy to target (TAM ≥ 10,000)

         * Growing market

            2. Campaign thesis done or roughly outlined

               * You should already know:

                  * Country/region

                  * Industry

                  * Company size

                  * Who in the org you want to contact (titles)

If you don’t have those, stop and go back to those SOPs.
________________


3. Core Principles (for any basic list)
Keep these in your head while building:
                     1. Start at the company level, then go to people.

                        * First: make sure the companies fit.

                        * Then: pull the right contacts from those companies.

                           2. Don’t spend 5–6 hours on one list.

                              * If it’s taking that long, you’re over-engineering.

                              * Get it to ~80–90% good, not 100% “perfect.”

                                 3. Aim for “9 good companies, 1 bad” ratio.

                                    * It’s okay if 1–2 out of 10 are off.

                                    * It’s not okay if every other record is garbage.

                                       4. Use exclusions smartly.

                                          * Don’t get trigger-happy and nuke half the market with one bad keyword exclusion.

________________


A. Building a Basic Lead List in Apollo / ALEADS
Note: ALEADS works basically the same way conceptually, so this logic applies to both.
We’ll use this example scenario as the pattern:
“We want marketing agencies that do PPC in US/UK/CA, with 5–50 employees, who can afford a ~$4K/month service.”
________________


A.1 Company-Level Filters
Step 1 – Set locations
                                             * Account / company location:

                                                * Include:

                                                   * United States

                                                   * Canada

                                                   * United Kingdom

(We often get the best performance from these three, but adjust per client.)
________________


Step 2 – Set industries
                                                      * Pick the closest industry tag(s). Example:

                                                         * Marketing and Advertising

                                                            * This will usually bring a big number (e.g. 300k+ companies).

If an industry is clearly wrong (e.g. “Market Research” or “Recruiting”), you can exclude it.
________________


Step 3 – Set employee count (as a proxy for revenue)
Example:
                                                               * Employee count: 5–50

Rule of thumb:
If a client wants, say, $4K/month deals, you want businesses that likely do $40K+/month in revenue. Employee size is your quick filter.
If a client says “10–50 employees,” you might widen slightly to 5–60 to account for out-of-date data.
________________


Step 4 – Add keyword filters
Now refine with include and exclude keywords.
                                                                  * Example include keywords:

                                                                     * PPC

                                                                     * “pay per click”

                                                                     * “paid ads”

                                                                     * “Google Ads”

                                                                        * Example exclude keywords:

                                                                           * “recruiting”

                                                                           * “staffing”

                                                                           * “job board”

                                                                           * “retail”

                                                                           * “online shopping”

                                                                           * etc.

🔑 Important:
                                                                              * Include keywords → company must have any of those keywords.

                                                                              * Include all → company must have all of them.

                                                                                 * Use “include all” only when you’re very sure (e.g. needs both “content” and “PPC”).

________________


A.2 Cleaning the Company List
Once the base filters are set, now you spot-check and clean:
                                                                                    1. Go through page 1–5 of the company results.

                                                                                    2. For each weird / totally off company:

                                                                                       * Open their website.

                                                                                       * Ask: “Is this actually our ICP or is it mislabeled?”

                                                                                          3. If it’s clearly wrong:

                                                                                             * Look for unique keywords that are causing this:

                                                                                                * e.g. “online shopping,” “jewelry,” “toys,” “job board,” etc.

                                                                                                   * Add those to your exclude keywords.

                                                                                                      4. Repeat until:

                                                                                                         * For every ~10 companies:

                                                                                                            * 8–9 are correct

                                                                                                            * 1–2 might be off → this is acceptable

Do NOT:
                                                                                                               * Exclude broad words like software, platform, etc., unless you’ve checked that it doesn’t blow up your valid ICP.

________________


A.3 People-Level Filters (Contacts)
Once companies look good, switch to the People tab (or “Contacts”).
Step 1 – Region logic
We usually do NOT hard-limit people by country because many execs live elsewhere (remote work).
Instead:
                                                                                                                  * Keep company filter for regions (US/UK/CA or whatever).

                                                                                                                  * For people, we mainly use exclusions, not strict inclusions:

                                                                                                                     * Exclude regions:

                                                                                                                        * India

                                                                                                                        * Pakistan

                                                                                                                        * Africa

Reason: email quality & time zone + generic offshore roles.
But if the company is based in your target country, the exact person location can be flexible.
________________


Step 2 – Job titles
We target relevant decision-makers. Examples for agencies:
                                                                                                                           * “Owner”

                                                                                                                           * “Founder”

                                                                                                                           * “CEO”

                                                                                                                           * “President”

                                                                                                                           * “Managing Director”

                                                                                                                           * “Head of Marketing”

                                                                                                                           * “VP of Marketing”

                                                                                                                           * “Director of Marketing”

                                                                                                                           * “CMO”

                                                                                                                           * Sometimes: “Head of Growth”, “Head of Demand Gen”

Best practice:
                                                                                                                              * Use “similar to” or “contains” rather than “exact title” to catch variations:

                                                                                                                                 * e.g. Head of Marketing, Head, Marketing, Head – Marketing

Separate lists by seniority if needed:
                                                                                                                                    * For bigger companies (100+ employees), make two lists:

                                                                                                                                       * List A: Owners / C-level / Founders

                                                                                                                                       * List B: Heads/Directors of Marketing, Growth, Demand Gen

                                                                                                                                          * Messaging will be different, so keep those segments separate.

________________


Step 3 – Verified emails toggle
There’s usually an option like “Only show verified emails”.
                                                                                                                                             * Default: ON

                                                                                                                                                * Use this 90–99% of the time.

                                                                                                                                                * Cleaner lists, less bounce risk.

                                                                                                                                                   * Turn it OFF only when:

                                                                                                                                                      * Niche ICP with very limited volume

                                                                                                                                                      * You plan to use the Triple Verification process to hunt missing emails

Remember: just because a contact is “unverified” doesn’t mean they don’t exist—it means that database didn’t find the email yet.
________________


Step 4 – Exclusion lists
If the client has:
                                                                                                                                                         * Past leads

                                                                                                                                                         * Current customers

                                                                                                                                                         * “Do-not-contact” list

Then:
                                                                                                                                                            * Use include/exclude list feature:

                                                                                                                                                               * Exclude those domains or contacts from your final list.

________________


B. Building a Basic Lead List in Clay
Clay can do two things for basic lists:
                                                                                                                                                                  1. Build lists directly (Find People / Find Companies)

                                                                                                                                                                  2. Enrich companies/people from other sources (GMB, Clutch, etc.) – more advanced

Here we focus on the direct “basic list” approach.
________________


B.1 Using “Find People” in Clay
Step 1 – Find People block
                                                                                                                                                                     * In Clay, create a new table.

                                                                                                                                                                     * Add a step: Find People.

Set filters similar to Apollo:
                                                                                                                                                                        * Company Industry: e.g. “Marketing Services / Marketing and Advertising”

                                                                                                                                                                        * Employee Count: e.g. 2–50 or 5–50

                                                                                                                                                                        * Location (company level):

                                                                                                                                                                           * Include:

                                                                                                                                                                              * United States

                                                                                                                                                                              * United Kingdom

                                                                                                                                                                              * Canada

If Clay groups people & company location in one filter, just set the same logic.
________________


Step 2 – Exclude regions (IPA)
If available, use region exclude:
                                                                                                                                                                                 * Exclude:

                                                                                                                                                                                    * India

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Pakistan

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Africa

Same reasoning as above.
________________


Step 3 – Job titles in Clay
Within the Find People config:
                                                                                                                                                                                       * Job title “similar to” or “contains”:

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Owner

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Founder

                                                                                                                                                                                          * CEO

                                                                                                                                                                                          * President

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Head of Marketing

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Director of Marketing

                                                                                                                                                                                          * CMO

                                                                                                                                                                                          * etc.

Use similar to where possible to catch variations.
________________


Step 4 – Save search & reuse
Clay lets you Save Search.
                                                                                                                                                                                             * Once you dial in a good search (e.g. “Senior leaders in Marketing & Advertising, US/UK/CA”), save it.

                                                                                                                                                                                             * You can reload it later instead of re-doing filters every time.

________________


B.2 Enriching Emails in Clay
Once Clay gives you a list of people:
                                                                                                                                                                                                1. Add a “Find Work Email” step:

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * We typically use a provider like LeadMagic (or whatever you have wired).

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Input: person’s full name + company domain.

                                                                                                                                                                                                      2. Run on sample rows first (e.g. first 10) to confirm:

                                                                                                                                                                                                         * You’re getting valid-looking emails (name@company.com).

                                                                                                                                                                                                            3. Once confirmed:

                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Run on the whole list (respecting credit usage).

Optional:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Add an additional email verification step if you want that extra safety.

________________


C. Quality Standards & Common Mistakes
C.1 Quality Standards
A basic lead list is “good enough” when:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * The ICP matches the campaign thesis

                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Most rows are:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Correct type of company

                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Correct region

                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Correct seniority/title

                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Error rate is acceptable:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Roughly 10–20% of leads can be a bit off and that’s fine at scale

We are not trying to build a perfect dataset. We are trying to build a high-probability, fast-to-deploy dataset.
________________


C.2 Common Mistakes to Avoid
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 1. Over-excluding keywords

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Example: excluding “software” when targeting SaaS-enabled agencies.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * You might accidentally remove your best ICP.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       2. Not checking the first 3–5 pages

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * If page 1 is trash and you never checked, you’ll send a whole campaign to the wrong market.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             3. Relying only on person location

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Executives live everywhere; the company location + industry matters more.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   4. Lumping all titles together in big companies

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Don’t target Owner, CMO, and Marketing Coordinator with the same copy in a 500-person company.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         5. Spending 5+ hours on one perfect list

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * If it takes that long, zoom out and simplify your filters.

________________


D. Quick Checklist (for the team)
Before you say “Lead list done,” make sure you can answer YES to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               1. ✅ ICP matches Lowest Hanging Fruit criteria

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               2. ✅ Company filters:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Correct countries/regions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Correct industries

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Reasonable employee size

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     3. ✅ Company list spot-checked (first 3–5 pages)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * No obvious junk (job boards, retail shops, etc.) dominating

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           4. ✅ People filters:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Titles match decision-makers

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * IPA regions excluded

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 5. ✅ Emails:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * “Verified only” ON (unless niche case)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Exclusions (client lists) applied

If yes to all → you’ve built a basic lead list correctly.
