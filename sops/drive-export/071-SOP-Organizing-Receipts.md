Objective
To efficiently manage and organize receipts by saving them securely and recording them accurately in Xero for expense tracking and reimbursement.
________________


Workflow
1. Monitor Inbox Daily
* Check email inbox and shared accounts for new receipts

* Download receipt images or PDFs immediately

* Verify receipt details: vendor, date, amount, and purpose

________________


2. Upload to Google Drive
   * Save receipts in the folder:
/Finance/Receipts/YYYY/MM/

   * Use clear file naming format:
YYYY-MM-DD_VendorName_ExpenseType.pdf (e.g., 2025-06-30_Starbucks_Travel.pdf)

   * Keep folder organized by year and month for easy access

________________


3. Record Receipt in Xero
      * Log in to Xero

      * Go to: Business → Expense Claims → New Claim

      * Enter details: date, amount, vendor, expense category, and description

      * Attach receipt file (upload from Google Drive if needed)

      * Submit for approval or mark as reimbursed as per process

________________


4. Confirm Completion
         * Update internal tracking sheet or notify relevant parties when receipt entry is done

         * Flag missing or unclear information promptly

________________


Best Practices
            * Check inbox daily to avoid backlog

            * Maintain consistent naming and filing habits

            * Verify all receipt details before uploading

            * Communicate any discrepancies immediately

________________


What NOT to Do
               * Don’t leave receipts unfiled in inbox

               * Don’t skip verification of receipt details

               * Don’t forget to attach receipts when recording expenses in Xero
