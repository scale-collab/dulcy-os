﻿No Close Follow-up SOP




video instructions: No Close Workflow.mp4


If you can’t overcome the prospect's objections, you need to schedule a followup with them for the next meeting.




1. Use your reschedule link to find a time in your calendar that suits the prospect (be sure to do this on the call) (you will need to request your own followup event - message Max for this).


2. Use the prospects info to fill out the scheduler, and press schedule. This means they will receive reminders. Getting their mobile / cell number is very important.


3. Double check the time with the prospect and say something like ‘is there any reason you can’t make 4PM on Wednesday, except from maybe an alien invasion?’.


4. Let the prospect know you’ve scheduled it.


5. After the call, draft up a new email for the prospect (from you@salesliege.com).


CC max@salesliege.com on all of these emails.
