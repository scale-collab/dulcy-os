﻿Email Campaign Reporting SOP


Why Do This Task?
Creating email campaign reports every Friday helps us monitor performance and adjust strategies to improve client results.


Video Walkthrough: HERE


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Overview of Reporting Process:
        1.        Generate reports for each workspace (client).
        2.        Provide a breakdown per campaign for each variant.
        3.        Analyze campaign performance over the last 10 days.


----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Step-by-Step Instructions


1. Access the Dashboard
        •        Log into the Dulcy platform.
        •        Navigate to the Dashboard section.
  

2. Select the Workspace (Client)
        •        Choose the specific workspace (client) in the top right you need to create a report for.
        •        Ensure you’re viewing stats for the correct client.
  

3. Set the Date Range
        •        In the top-right date selector, choose Last 10 Days (should be set by default) to filter recent campaign data. *Last 30 days for monthly reports
        •        This will help capture only the relevant metrics.
  

4. Check Campaign Stats
        •        Click on the Campaigns tab and select the active campaigns. You then press Stats to view an overview of campaign performance. The individual results of each variant will be at the bottom for the whole campaign duration. 
        •        Note down key metrics:
        •        Emails Sent
        •        People Contacted
        •        Replies
        •        Bounced
        •        Interested
        •        Meetings Booked
  

5. Record Individual Email Stats
        •        Scroll to view Individual Email Stats for each campaign variant.
        •        Write down the performance of each email variant, including (with percentages):
        •        Total Sent
        •        Contacts
        •        Replies (replies/contact)
        •        Bounced (replies/contact)
        •        Interested (interested/replies)
        •        Meetings Booked (booked meetings/interested)
  

6. Document Your Findings
        •        Organize the data for each client workspace in a simple report format:
        •        Client Name
        •        Date Range: Last 10 Days
        •        Overall Campaign Stats
        •        Individual Campaign Stats (by Variant)


7. Share the Report
        •        Export the data if needed or take screenshots of key metrics.
        •        Send the report to the relevant team members for review.

Key Notes:
        •        Consistency: Check stats on Fridays and use the same format each time.
        •        Accuracy: Double-check metrics to ensure accurate reporting.
        •        Completion: Submit reports by end-of-day Friday.
