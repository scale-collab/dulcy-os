📉 SOP 3: Quarterly Marketing Report
Title: Creating Quarterly Marketing Reports for Strategic Review
 Applies To: Executive + Leadership Team
🧾 Objective
To provide executive-level insight into marketing outcomes over the quarter, including KPIs, progress vs. goals, challenges, and recommendations for Q-next.
________________


🔁 Workflow
✅ 1. Gather Monthly Reports (3 months)
Use the data compiled in monthly reports for the quarter (e.g., Q1 = Jan–Mar).
✅ 2. Analyze by Campaign Type
Campaign Type
	Metrics to Include
	Lead Generation
	New leads, CTR, conversion rate
	Product Launches
	Signup rates, page visits, campaign ROI
	Nurture Sequences
	Engagement trends, top content pieces
	Paid Campaigns (if any)
	Cost per lead, spend vs. return
	✅ 3. Create Executive Slide Deck
Use Google Slides with:
* Q Overview

* Key Wins

* Strategic Goals Met/Unmet

* Graphs showing trend lines

* Action Items for Next Quarter

✅ 4. Add Recommendations
Bullet key improvements or proposals:
   * Shift budget to top-performing list segments

   * Pause underperforming campaigns

   * Plan Q-next content based on engagement history

✅ 5. Deliver & Present
      * Due by the 10th of the first month of new quarter

      * Include link to Google Slide deck + supporting Sheets

      * Prepare 10–15 min summary for executive sync
