How to Conduct a Client Onboarding Call SOP
Purpose
To ensure every new client is welcomed, guided, and aligned with how Dulcy operates, while clearly setting expectations, establishing authority, and preparing them for a successful outbound launch.
________________


1. Pre-Call Requirements
1.1 Confirm Payment + Contract
Before the onboarding call is booked:
* Contract must be signed

* Payment must be completed

* Slack channel created via automation

* Client invited to:

   * Dulcy Email Bison Workspace

   * HeyReach Workspace

   * Dulcy Client Portal Workspace

1.2 The Onboarding Form MUST Be Completed
You do not take the onboarding call unless the client has filled out the Onboarding Form.
If they haven’t:
      * Follow up every 2–3 days to remind them

      * If they fill it out an hour before the call → reschedule

      * You must have time to review their answers and prepare

________________


2. Opening the Call (First 2 Minutes)
2.1 Introduce Yourself Clearly
Example:
“Hey John, great to meet you. I’m Juan, your Client Success Manager here at Dulcy. My role is to manage your campaign strategy, oversee performance, and make sure we get you to your goal of consistent booked calls.”
Your goal:
✔ Remove confusion
✔ Establish authority
✔ Show you’re their main point of contact
2.2 Align Expectations
Clients speak to:
         * The Salesperson

         * Possibly Alex

         * Now you

So clarify the handoff:
“From here forward, I’ll be the person guiding your campaign day-to-day. Anything you need related to performance, strategy, or changes—you’ll come to me.”
________________


3. Goals of the Onboarding Call
There are four main objectives:
3.1 Make the client feel supported & confident
They just paid → they’re excited but skeptical.
Your job:
            * Reduce uncertainty

            * Show them the roadmap

            * Make them feel cared for

3.2 Re-explain the process (even if they “already know”)
Clients forget 80% of what sales told them.
Re-align them on:
               * Setup timeline

               * 14-day warmup

               * Offer testing strategy

               * What Dulcy handles vs what they handle

3.3 Review their onboarding form
You must demonstrate:
                  * You understand their business

                  * You’ve reviewed their inputs

                  * You have early strategic thoughts

                  * You’re prepared

3.4 Set clear expectations
You must cover:
                     * What happens today

                     * What happens this week

                     * What happens in 14 days

                     * What early results may look like

________________


4. Walk Through the Roadmap
Use the roadmap document on the call.
Explain:
4.1 Today:
                        * Confirm domains + email accounts

                        * Begin warmup

                        * Confirm client portal access

                        * Ensure Slack is working

                        * Set up reporting automations

4.2 Next 14 Days:
                           * Domain + inbox warmup

                           * Copywriting

                           * Market research

                           * Lead list creation

                           * Offer development

                           * Script writing

                           * Testing 2–3 industries with 10–20k leads

                           * AB testing CTAs & angles

4.3 Goal of First 30 Days:
                              * Validate ONE winning offer

                              * Validate ONE winning angle

                              * Validate ONE niche

                              * Produce the first flow of booked calls

________________


5. Deliver the Offer Validation Framework
Explain this exactly:
Phase 1 — Test Broad (10–20k per segment)
Run:
                                 * Direct offer

                                 * Education-based offer

                                 * Industry specific offer

Across:
                                    * 2–3 industries

                                    * Ten unique messaging angles

Purpose:
Quickly identify what resonates and what produces the fastest booked meetings.
Phase 2 — Identify the Winner
Once you see:
                                       * Best open rate

                                       * Best positive reply rate

                                       * Best booking rates

You use that to build the campaign thesis.
Phase 3 — Scale & Deepen the Messaging
Now:
                                          * Add A/B/C testing

                                          * Personalize

                                          * Build multi-step sequences

                                          * Expand lead buckets

                                          * Focus on consistency

________________


6. Review Systems Access With the Client
Make sure they have:
                                             * Access to their Dulcy client portal

                                             * Access to the HeyReach workspace

                                             * Access to the Email Bison workspace

                                             * Access to Slack

If multiple people need access:
                                                * Manually invite them to HeyReach

                                                * Manually invite them to Email Bison

                                                * Manually add them to portal users and assign their Client ID

________________


7. Explain the Reply Funnel & Cold Email Logic
Clients must understand:
                                                   * What happens to sent emails

                                                   * What happens when emails bounce

                                                   * What domain masking is

                                                   * How inbox warmup works

                                                   * What “cooked inboxes” mean

                                                   * How Dulcy automatically handles replies

                                                   * Why messaging is structured the way it is

You also show:
                                                      * Where replies go

                                                      * How we respond

                                                      * How to set tone, length, and structure in RevReply

________________


8. Ending the Call
You MUST end the call by booking:
✔ Step 2: Cold Sales Funnel Audit Call
This will:
                                                         * Review their offer

                                                         * Review sales materials

                                                         * Review follow-up systems

                                                         * Prepare for maximum conversion of booked calls

And that’s the final step before launching.
________________


9. Post-Call Responsibilities
Immediately after the call you should:
9.1 Confirm items in Slack
                                                            * Domains purchased

                                                            * Inboxes purchased

                                                            * Warmup started

                                                            * Lead research underway

9.2 Update your internal tasks
                                                               * Thesis creation

                                                               * Script writing

                                                               * Lead list verification

                                                               * Reporting setup

                                                               * Offer development

9.3 Keep them warm
Send updates every 1–2 days during setup.
Never let a client feel like nothing is happening.
________________


Final Notes
Your job is to:
                                                                  * Set expectations

                                                                  * Establish trust

                                                                  * Educate the client

                                                                  * Give them clarity

                                                                  * Show them the path

                                                                  * Explain timelines honestly

                                                                  * Prepare them for performance variability

A good onboarding call is:
Clear, confident, educational, and structured.
A bad one is:
Confusing, rushed, and vague.
