1. 
Purpose
When we launch a campaign for a client, they usually have multiple industries or segments they could target.
This SOP shows you how to pick the lowest hanging fruit – the market segment that is:
* Easiest to convert

* Most likely to respond

* Worth the effort (and fee)

We do this using four criteria.
________________


2. Core Principle: We Do 
Not
 Create Demand
Before anything else:
We are not trying to convince people to want something they don’t care about.
We are not in the “sell me this pen” game.
If the market doesn’t need it, we don’t force it.
Our job is to:
   * Find people who already have the pain

   * Match them with a strong offer

   * Communicate it clearly through cold email, LinkedIn, Voice AI, etc.

If you try to create demand from scratch → you’ve already lost.
________________


3. The Four Criteria
When evaluating a potential market / industry / segment, you must check all four:
      1. Massive Pain

      2. Purchasing Power

      3. Easy to Target

      4. Growing Market

If it fails any of these badly, it’s probably not lowest hanging fruit.
________________


3.1 Massive Pain (Need vs Want)
Question:
Does this market need the solution… or would it just be “nice to have”?
         * We want:

            * People who desperately need the outcome

            * Issues that hurt daily (lost revenue, broken systems, no leads, errors, slowness, etc.)

               * We want to avoid:

                  * “Cute” offers

                  * Luxury / vanity wants

                  * Stuff that feels optional or “someday”

Example – Good (Need):
                     * Lead generation for agencies that are stuck at $20k/mo

                     * Fixing broken systems that waste hours daily

                     * Replacing slow manual processes that block growth

Example – Weak (Want):
                        * “Nice new website just in case you ever need it”

                        * Niche “insurance photo archive in case your house burns down one day”

                        * Anything that’s preventative but not felt as painful right now

Rule of thumb:
The pain is the pitch.
If they don’t feel the pain, the pitch will never land.
________________


3.2 Purchasing Power
Question:
Can this market realistically afford the service at the price that makes it worth our time?
                           * If the ideal price point is $3k–$10k+ and the ICP:

                              * Struggles with cashflow

                              * Operates on tiny margins

                              * Historically resists paying for help

                                 * → 🚩 Bad fit.

Real example from your transcript:
                                    * Voice AI for Realtors:

                                       * Pain: makes sense

                                       * Concept: useful

                                       * Reality: when you start talking $5–10k, most simply won’t pay

                                       * So even if the idea is good, purchasing power isn’t there → we skip it.

Don’t build a campaign around people who like the idea but will never pay.
________________


3.3 Easy to Target
Question:
Can we easily find these people in bulk and reach them via channels we use?
We prefer markets where:
                                          * There’s a clear data source:

                                             * Apollo

                                             * Google Maps / GMB

                                             * Industry directories

                                             * LinkedIn

                                             * Associations

                                             * Review sites, etc.

                                                * They have:

                                                   * Email addresses

                                                   * LinkedIn profiles

                                                   * Websites / GMB

                                                   * Some digital footprint

Minimum requirement:
Total Addressable Market (TAM) ≥ 10,000 contacts.
Why?
                                                      * We need enough volume to:

                                                         * Run tests

                                                         * Iterate

                                                         * Scale if it works

                                                            * Tiny markets = fast saturation, poor test data, no real scalability.

Bad example:
                                                               * Extremely niche, offline-only categories

                                                               * Hard-to-find owners (no email, no LinkedIn, no directory listings)

Good example:
                                                                  * Dentists

                                                                  * HVAC/roofing

                                                                  * eCom brands

                                                                  * SaaS companies

                                                                  * Multi-location services

All have structured data and are easy to scrape.
________________


3.4 Growing Market
Question:
Is this market growing, stable, or dying?
We want:
                                                                     * Industries with tailwinds

                                                                     * Markets where demand is increasing or at least stable

We want to avoid:
                                                                        * Declining or obviously dying segments:

                                                                           * Newspapers

                                                                           * Traditional billboards

                                                                           * Legacy media on its way out

Because:
                                                                              * Business is already hard enough

                                                                              * A shrinking market = more resistance, more price pressure, more fear

Three evergreen “big markets”:
                                                                                 1. Health

                                                                                 2. Wealth (where Dulcy lives)

                                                                                 3. Relationships

Most strong offers plug into one or more of these.
________________


4. How to Use This With a Client (Step-by-Step)
When a client has multiple industries they can target (e.g., plumbers, HVAC, medspas, ecommerce, etc.), do this:
Step 1 – List All Possible Industries
For that client, list every ICP they could serve:
                                                                                    * Example:

                                                                                       * Dentists

                                                                                       * HVAC companies

                                                                                       * Home remodelers

                                                                                       * SAAS tools, etc.

________________


Step 2 – Score Each Industry on the 4 Criteria (1–5)
For every industry, quickly rate:
                                                                                          1. Massive Pain – how urgent and intense is the primary problem?

                                                                                          2. Purchasing Power – can they afford the offer?

                                                                                          3. Easy to Target – can we easily build a list at scale (10k+ TAM)?

                                                                                          4. Growing Market – does the market have momentum or is it shrinking?

Step 3 – Align With Offer Strength
Once you’ve picked 1–2 “lowest hanging fruit” markets:
                                                                                             * Confirm:

                                                                                                * Where does the client already see wins?

                                                                                                * Where do they have case studies or proof?

                                                                                                * Where can their delivery actually match the pain level?

You are aiming for:
Strong pain
                                                                                                   * Clear purchasing power

                                                                                                   * Easy targeting

                                                                                                   * Growing market

                                                                                                   * Strong offer delivery

 = ideal campaign starting point.

________________


5. Saturation: How to Think About It
Clients will say:
“Isn’t that market saturated?”
Your internal rule:
                                                                                                      * Saturation isn’t the real problem if:

                                                                                                         * The offer is actually good

                                                                                                         * The positioning is differentiated

                                                                                                         * The messaging is clear and pain-driven

You’re not trying to be “just another agency.”
You’re targeting starving crowds with something clearly better.
________________


6. Final Mental Model for the Team
When picking markets, always think in this order:
                                                                                                            1. Find the starving crowd

                                                                                                            2. Check the four criteria

                                                                                                               * Massive pain

                                                                                                               * Purchasing power

                                                                                                               * Easy to target

                                                                                                               * Growing market

                                                                                                                  3. Match offer strength

                                                                                                                  4. Use our cold email / LinkedIn / voice AI / SMS to deliver the message

If any one of the four criteria is seriously missing → it’s not lowest hanging fruit.
