﻿A. COMPANY SNAPSHOT
- Company Name: Shop Innovator
- What the company does: Provides a full-stack marketing strategy + execution service for US-based brick-and-mortar retailers (“bricks & clicks”) that sell premium, high-ticket physical goods online via Shopify.
- Core outcomes the work supports (no guarantees): improved on-site conversion, stronger trust/credibility on the website, better-performing lifecycle email marketing, clearer brand messaging, and more consistent revenue performance without relying on discounts.
- Primary channels/surfaces worked on:
  - Shopify website (UX, layout, copy, page structure)
  - Klaviyo (email audits, flows, segmentation, templates, campaigns support)
  - Supporting analytics/attribution (Shopify, Klaviyo, GA4; and optional heatmap/session tools)
- Ideal product category focus: Home & Garden (premium physical goods)
- Typical product price point in ICP: $2,000 to $20,000+
- Geography focus: United States (not Canada)
- Engagement style:
  - Done-for-you delivery for buildouts/implementation
  - Done-with-you collaboration option during optimization/acceleration (e.g., working with an internal team or existing partners)


B. DETAILED OFFERINGS
Shop Innovator’s work is organized under a 3-phase system: Audit → Build → Accelerate. Deliverables are tailored based on what’s found in the audit and what the brand needs most.


1) PHASE 1: AUDIT (Diagnosis and roadmap creation)
- Website Experience Audit (Shopify)
  - UX/UI review of homepage, product pages, collection pages
  - Trust-building elements (reviews, badges where appropriate, customer stories, credibility cues)
  - Brand messaging clarity and premium positioning alignment
  - Mobile responsiveness, accessibility considerations, and friction in purchase flow
  - Speed/load-time considerations and “conversion friction” identification
- Email Marketing Audit (Klaviyo)
  - Review of existing flows (welcome, browse abandonment, cart abandonment, post-purchase, winback)
  - List growth and segmentation evaluation
  - Voice/tone, timing, and offer structure review (with high-ticket consideration windows in mind)
- Brand Voice & Positioning Check
  - Brand story clarity and consistency across core site pages and email
  - Whether the site communicates value and reduces high-ticket hesitation
- Analytics & Attribution Review (high level)
  - What appears to be driving revenue (e.g., direct, organic, paid—reviewed at a high level)
  - Where conversion gaps likely exist in the funnel
  - Optional: incorporate insights from heatmap/session recording tools if available


2) PHASE 2: BUILD (Implementation of priority fixes and foundational assets)
- Website Optimization (Shopify)
  - Homepage redesign or refinement (within existing theme when possible; theme recommendations when needed)
  - Product page layouts designed for high-ticket decision-making (trust cues, lifestyle visuals, specs presented clearly, objection-handling copy)
  - Collection page clarity (navigation, hierarchy, merchandising logic)
  - Copywriting aligned with premium positioning and brand personality
  - Visual hierarchy improvements to support confident purchasing
- Email Flow Buildout (Klaviyo)
  - Strategy, copy, design, and build of key flows, typically including:
    - Welcome series (nurture-forward)
    - Browse & cart abandonment (aligned with premium positioning)
    - Post-purchase (expectation setting, care/use guidance, satisfaction support, repeat purchase)
    - Winback
    - Review request and/or “customer story” prompts
  - Flow approach designed for longer consideration cycles common with high-ticket items
- Brand Messaging & Trust Layering
  - About page writing/refinement
  - Customer story section creation/refinement
  - Homepage messaging and product storytelling updates
  - Goal: reduce bounce, increase trust, and close the gap between interest and purchase
- Promo & Campaign Infrastructure (without discount dependency)
  - Promo playbooks and repeatable campaign structures
  - Email templates and campaign frameworks
  - Landing page support for campaigns (as needed)
  - Seasonal content calendar framework (planning structure, not rigid mandates)


3) PHASE 3: ACCELERATE (Ongoing optimization, testing, and support)
- Campaign Execution Support
  - Guidance and execution support for launches, seasonal promos, or content series
  - Email creative (copy + structure), landing page support, and visual strategy direction
  - Quarterly promotional rhythm and holiday planning
- Conversion Optimization (CRO)
  - Iterative testing/review of headlines, layout, CTA placements, product page structure, and checkout friction
  - Use of visual analytics tools (e.g., Hotjar or Lucky Orange) when available/appropriate
- Attribution Clarity & Growth Coaching
  - Dashboard setup guidance and interpretation support (Shopify, Klaviyo, GA4)
  - Recommendations on what to stop/start/scale based on observed performance patterns

F. TYPICAL BUYING TRIGGERS
Common situations that make this ICP more likely to engage:
- Traffic is stable or growing, but conversion rate feels “stuck” or inconsistent.
- Owner suspects the site looks “fine,” yet doesn’t match the price point or in-store experience.
- Email list exists (often in Klaviyo), but flows are underperforming, outdated, or generic.
- The owner is overwhelmed being the de facto marketer/project manager and wants a cohesive team.
- The business is hitting a plateau and needs a clear prioritization roadmap.
- Brand has constraints around discounting (e.g., MAP policies), increasing the need for trust-building and better messaging.
- A change event triggers reevaluation (new product line, new season, new goals, frustration with current execution quality, or desire to refresh the brand story).

H. OBJECTIONS AND FRIENDLY REBUTTALS
(Always acknowledge, clarify, and stay factual. Avoid pressure.)


1) “We’ve tried improving the site before and it didn’t help.”
- It’s common to see cosmetic changes without a conversion strategy. Shop Innovator starts with an audit to identify specific friction points and trust gaps, then implements prioritized changes tied to buyer behavior for high-ticket purchases.


2) “Our products are expensive—people just won’t buy online.”
- High-ticket items can sell online when the site provides the guidance and credibility buyers need. The work focuses on trust-building, product storytelling, and consideration-stage nurture, so buyers feel informed and confident. (Do not claim certainty; frame as increasing likelihood.)


3) “We already get traffic; we need more traffic, not website changes.”
- If traffic is already steady, the biggest lever can be improving what happens after the click: product page clarity, credibility cues, and email nurture. Shop Innovator’s audit helps confirm whether conversion is the bottleneck before recommending solutions.


4) “Email doesn’t work for us.”
- Often the issue is that flows are generic, outdated, or not aligned with the longer decision cycle of high-ticket products. Shop Innovator’s approach is to tailor flows, segmentation, and messaging to how premium buyers evaluate options.


5) “I’m worried this will cheapen our brand.”
- The work is specifically designed for premium positioning. Messaging and promo planning aim to preserve brand perception and avoid relying on aggressive discounting as the default lever.


6) “We don’t have time to manage another project.”
- The service is structured to reduce owner burden with a unified team and a phased approach. The audit clarifies priorities so the business isn’t spinning on endless low-impact tasks.


7) “We’ve been burned by marketing help before.”
- Shop Innovator’s process begins with a diagnosis and specific recommendations. The goal is clarity: what’s happening now, what to fix first, and what changes will be made (site, email, messaging). Encourage them to review the results page for examples.

  - Reality: flows require iteration, segmentation improvements, and messaging updates as products and customer behavior evolve.
