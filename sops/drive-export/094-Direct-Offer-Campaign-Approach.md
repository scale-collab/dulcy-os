﻿Direct Offer Campaign
What it is
A straightforward campaign that clearly states the problem and the outcome. No storytelling, no long setup.
Why we run it
When the pain is obvious and the solution is easy to understand, directness outperforms creativity.
When we run it
* When the prospect already understands the problem

* When speed matters more than nuance

* When clarity beats persuasion

How we run it for success
   * One clear problem

   * One clear outcome

   * One clear CTA

Example scripts
Short Direct Ask
Hey {FIRST_NAME},
If we could get you {BENEFIT}, would that be worth a chat?
Problem → Outcome
Hey {FIRST_NAME},
We help {niche} {offer} in {timeframe}.
Just helped {case study}.
Worth exploring?
Why Isn’t? Case Study
Hey {FIRST_NAME}, just curious why {COMPANY} isn’t running outbound right now?
With case studies like {CASE}, this could be a quick win.
Want me to walk you through it?
