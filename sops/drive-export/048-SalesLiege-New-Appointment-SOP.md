1. Draft and send a personal email to the prospect using the below template… (you@salesliege.com)


Hey [name], [yourname] here. I handle all the new calls that we book with SalesLiege. I wanted to personally let you know we’ve received your booking for [booking date/time] and it’s locked in. 


Excited to chat!

In the meantime, check out Max & Isaiah on youtube www.youtube.com/@maxrossofficial & www.youtube.com/@isaiahlebronnn if you haven’t already :)


Chat on [DATE E.G. NEXT WEDNESDAY].


If you have any questions, don’t hesitate to reach out to me on this email.


Thanks,
[yourname]


1.5 CC max@salesliege.com on all of these emails.


2. Find the prospect on LinkedIn and send them a connection request. Send a note that says...


Hey name, I saw you booked a call with SalesLiege. I’ll be handling your call, so I thought it would be good to connect.


3. Find the prospect on Facebook and send them a friend request. Send them a DM (doesn’t matter whether they’ve accepted or not) voice note saying…


“Hey name, [yourname] here. Just letting you know I got your booking for SalesLiege. I’ll be taking care of you through these next steps, so if you need anything don’t hesitate to let me know” Sound energetic, upbeat and excited. 


NOTE: Batch this to happen at the end of the day for efficiency (unless you have a same day
schedule).
