At the end of each day, send a message to the sales daily reporting WhatsApp channel summarising your day.
It’s really simple and won’t take more than 2-3 mins. All you have to do is answer the following...
1. How many calls did you do today?
2. How many new calls booked into your calendar today?
3. How many deals did you close today?
4. Have you added deals to your commission tracker, added all calls to your tracking sheet, uploaded call recordings, and completed the new appointment SOP for all of your new calls today?
5. How are you feeling? (quick 2-3 sentences describing your overall feeling about the day is fine)
6. What could you do tomorrow to make it better than today?
7. Feedback on anything you need help with?
   * do this is voicenote form
