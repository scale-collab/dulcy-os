﻿COLD CALLING SOP


Why Do This Task?: Cold calling helps us talk to new people who might want to work with us. By calling them, we can introduce ourselves and see if they need our help. The goal is to set up another meeting or a call if they’re interested.

Cold Calling Strategy: To improve our chances, we follow a specific strategy before making the call. This helps the prospect recognize us and be more open to a conversation.


1.        Send Email 1-2 Days Before the Call:
        •        Start by sending an introductory email 1 or 2 days before the call.
        •        The email is brief, introducing who you are and how we can help.
2.        Send LinkedIn Connection Request with a Note:
        •        Send a LinkedIn request with a short, friendly note to create a personal connection.
        •        This helps the prospect see a face, name, and message associated with you, creating familiarity.
3.        Prioritize High-Value Prospects:
        •        Each week per day, choose around 10 High-Value Prospects. These are people you think are a great fit for our services.
        •        Reach out to these high-value prospects more regularly and consistently, focusing on them during blocked time frames.
4.        Cold Call Only When Email Engagement is High:
        •        For other leads, monitor if they’ve opened your email (especially if they’ve opened it several times).
        •        Only call these leads when you see high email engagement, moving them into the high-value category.


Goal: By following this strategy, we increase the chances that at least 50% of prospects recognize your name when you call, making it easier to start the conversation.


Best Times to Make Cold Calls


        •        10 A.M. - 2 P.M. EST


---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Cold Calling Rules


✅ MUST DO:
        •        Be friendly and introduce yourself.
        •        Check that you have the right person by asking their position.
        •        Mention an offer to get their attention.
        •        Suggest a time for another meeting if they’re interested.
        •        Always check that you wrote down any information correctly.
❌ MUST NOT:
        •        Don’t push for a sale immediately.
        •        Don’t argue if they say they’re not interested.
        •        Don’t talk too long – keep it short and to the point.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


📌Key Notes
⏰Time to Call: Calls should be about 5-10 minutes.
🥅Goal: Introduce what we do and see if they’re interested in learning more.
🏆End Result: Set up a second call or meeting.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Understanding the Cold Call Structure


In cold calls, we follow a specific structure to keep things organized:
        1.        Introduce Yourself:
        •        Start by saying hello and asking how their day is going.
        •        Confirm that you’re talking to the right person.
        2.        Make an Offer:
        •        Tell them why you’re calling and mention an offer, like a free list of potential clients.
        •        Explain that it’s no-cost and only takes a few minutes to set up.
        3.        Propose a Meeting:
        •        Ask if they’d like to set up a short call to go over things.
        •        Offer a specific time and date.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Handling Objections


Sometimes people might say they’re not interested or have questions. Here’s what to do:
        •        If they say they’re not interested: Politely ask if they’ve tried cold email outreach before.
        •        If they want to know why it’s free: Explain that we’re confident they’ll find value in what we offer and want them to try it to demonstrate our expertise.
        •        If they say they have it in house: Kindly say that we triple verify the leads and provide accurate contact information for decision makers in any niche they target. Or Politely ask if they’ve tried cold email outreach before.
        •        If they question how are they interested leads: Say that we take your favorite clients and build a list based on their criteria. How you solved the problems with your clients, we target the exact same pain points in the leads and offer you as the solution. 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Call Outcomes


After each call, decide what happened and mark it down:
        1.        Interested: They want a follow-up call or more information.
        2.        Not Interested: They said no or didn’t want to hear more.
        3.        Follow-Up Needed: They need more time or information, so you’ll call back.
        4.        No Answer/Voicemail: They didn’t pick up; leave a short message if possible.


Sample Responses for Each Scenario
        •        Interested Lead: “That sounds great! I know you're probably busy now so does [specific time] or [alternate time] work for you?”
        •        Not Interested Lead: “Thanks for your time, and if anything changes, we’d love to connect in the future. Would it be okay if I follow up in a couple weeks?”
        •        Follow-Up Needed: “I’ll send over some info, and we can touch base again next week if that works with you.”
        •        Voicemail: “Hey LEAD, NAME from Dulcy... Looking to connect this week on sharing a few resources that might be useful. We help marketing agencies scale their business on autopilot through outbound outreach. We’d love to provide you with a list of 3,000 leads to demonstrate our expertise. I sent you an email with more details, I look forward to hearing from you.”
Then email immediately with a subject line similar to "VM from NAME (Dulcy)”

Objections:

Q: I appreciate the offer, but I'm not really interested in that right now. We already have our lead generation strategies in place.

A: Good to hear you already have some strategies in place. Just out of curiosity, does (company name) have a way to contact them all at scale?


–


Q: We haven't really considered it. We prefer keeping things in-house to maintain control over our processes.

A: What are the biggest challenges you face with your current lead generation strategy? We specialize in helping marketing agencies like yours overcome common hurdles, and even if you don’t need our services now, we might have some insights that could be useful so you can do it yourself.


–


Q: Yes, we handle it all internally. It's been working well for us. (If they do cold email)

A: I’m glad to hear it's going well for you, NAME. If you don’t mind me asking, (how many emails are you sending/meetings are you currently booking) on average per month?


Q: Why is it free with no strings attached? Hard to believe.


A: Great question! We offer the free leads because we believe in the quality of our service and want to give you a risk-free way to see the results for yourself. We're confident that you'll find value in what we provide.
