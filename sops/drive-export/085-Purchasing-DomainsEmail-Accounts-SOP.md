Domains

2. Log in to your pork bun account

Purchasing The Domains 
1. We now want to purchase our domains 
a. Head to the home screen of Porkbun and search for your domains, you can use the bulk search feature for ease.
  

b. The domains you purchase should end in only “.com”, but at times when there is a good sale “.co” (Our team will let you know). This is more for trust because “.com” is looked at as trustworthy compared to the cheaper LTDs like “.xyz” or “.info”.
  



*If the domains are for a client who is being onboarded, a Google Doc will automatically be created with all 50 domains so you may just copy and paste it into the bulk search. Before you buy these just confirm with the team and we’ll give you a yes or no to proceed.


Also, make sure there are no things like weird symbols (-, $, #, etc.) or numbers. 


Use a prefix or suffix to your domain to make it unique (try, get, demo, the, etc.)


1. trydulcy.com 
2. getdulcy.com 
3. heydulcy.com


(In the case that a domain is taken we trust you can come up with a different variant to reach the amount of domains needed.)
2. Once purchased, we want to tag the domains to the client's name
a. Head to ACCOUNT and then Domain Management 
  



b. You will now see your domains in your account. For the outreach domains, tick the box on the left of the name, select all of the recently purchased domains.


  



c. Then head to Bulk Actions and Edit Labels
  





d. Select the label of the client. If it is a new client you can add their company name in the title and pick a fun color. Then press save.

Email Accounts
1. Head to https://cheapinboxes.com/
  

2. Press Order Now (Purchase the amount of inboxes according the the package: dulcy.io/pricing)


https://buy.cheapinboxes.com/
  

3. Make sure everything is correct then Click Checkout 
  



4. You MUST apply the discount code in the box before you do anything else. The code is “LEADACADEMY” then press apply and the total should always be $195.00 for 100 inboxes. Enter your card number that was provided to you. And submit.
  



5. You will receive an order form you have to fill out. Instructions below. Cheap Inboxes Order Form - Alexander.xlsx
  



There is a sample info sheet that you can replicate but below is an example on how we would fill it out for DULCY.
  

* In the Inbox Manager, there is a form clients fill out when they get onboarded. Under data you’ll be able to see the responses. A Slack notification will go out, alerting when the form is complete. Information for clients accounts will be in the response table. 
* Everything red and blue will stay the same. Black just change my name to clients name and their websites.
* For domains just paste what you purchased.
* Password can be there company name followed up 99$$ (Ex: Company99$$)
* 2 Users per domain
* Enter the names for emails from onboarding form
* User name can be: firstname@company.com or firstname.lastname@company.com or firstletterlastname@company.com
* Enter the full name associated with the account from the form
* Enter profile picture link from the form
Make sure to mention to mask the domains and active warm up in Email Bison.


6. Open up Email Guard, and go into their chat and ask to have 50 domain masking credits added to the account. The total should be $37.50. Confirm with them.


  



Finally, notify Cheapinboxes support in the Whatsapp channel with the link to the Sheet and mention you already paid on the site.
