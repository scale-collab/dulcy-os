Purpose of the Task:
Before Alexander or the team meets a prospect, we need a complete summary of the client’s company. This summary helps prepare us for the call by giving us essential background information, so we’re ready to connect with the prospect on a deeper level.

Steps to Complete the Client Summary
        1.        When is the Client Summary task needed
        •        A Client Summary is needed for an upcoming meeting. You may complete this a day or couple days before a scheduled call if it is the first time we are meeting. The URL to the company’s website will be in the CRM under the prospects name.
  

        •        If unsure, ask the team if a summary is required for a specific meeting.


        2.        Use GPT Link for Company Overview (Optional)
        •        If you need assistance or are unsure where to start, use the provided GPT link: ChatGPT ICP Analysis Tool. 
        •        Prompt to Use in GPT:
        •        “I want you to give me a full report on this company using their website url: “URL HERE”

        •        If you use this, you MUST double check the information it gave was correct.


        3.        Gather Key Information on the Company
        •        Company Website: Start with the company’s official website. Look at the “About” page and other relevant sections.
        •        LinkedIn: Visit the company’s LinkedIn profile and the prospects for additional insights and recent activities. (You can search “FIRST & LAST NAME - COMPANY NAME” on google to see if linkedIn pops up. If not, try adding “LinkedIn” after.
        •        News and Updates: Check company website and all news sections for any recent updates or announcements. Google their name for news, and look on LinkedIn.


        4.        Create a Summary Note in Close CRM
        •        Open Close CRM and locate the lead page for the client.
        •        Add a new note labeled “Client Summary.”
        •        Use the following structure to create a clear and organized summary:


Client Summary Structure for Close CRM
        1.        Company Overview
        •        Description: Provide a short summary of what the company does and their services.
        •        Example: “CAR PR USA specializes in PR, advertising, and events for the collector car community, helping clients enhance brand visibility within this niche market.”
        2.        Target Audience / Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
        •        Industries: Describe the industry or industries they target (e.g., automotive, real estate).
        •        Location*: Mention the geographic focus if they have one (e.g., North America).
        •        Company Size*: Include the estimated company size (e.g., small to medium).
*If possible
                3.        Market Position & Competitors
        •        Industry*: Briefly state the industry in which they operate. (Marketing Agency, Saas, etc.)
        •        Competitive Advantages*: Mention anything unique about their position in the market, such as expertise, customer relationships, or niche focus.


        4.        Potential Talking Points
        •        List a few conversation starters or specific topics our team could mention during the call to demonstrate understanding and preparation from either their website or linkedin.
        •        Example: “I saw your recent press release about your event expansion—what has the response been like?”
        •        Example: “Attended Brighton SEO event 4 months ago”


Final Steps
        1.        Notify In Slack
        •        Once you complete the Client Summary, message the team in Slack.
        •        Example message:


Hi Everyone! The Client Summary for [Client’s Company Name] is ready in Close CRM under the notes section. Let me know if you need more details.


        2.        Review and Update if Necessary
        •        Before each meeting, double-check that information is current.
        •        Update any last-minute details if you find relevant news or developments.
