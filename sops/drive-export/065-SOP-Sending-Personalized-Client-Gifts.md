Objective
To ensure personalized client gifts are thoughtfully selected, timely delivered, and contribute to a positive client experience.
________________


Workflow
1. Identify Occasion and Client Preferences
* Confirm the reason for the gift (e.g., milestone, holiday, thank you)

* Check client preferences, allergies, or restrictions

* Review past gifts to avoid repetition

2. Select and Personalize Gift
   * Choose a gift aligned with client’s tastes and company brand

   * Add a personalized note or card with a sincere message

   * Verify gift quality and packaging

3. Arrange Delivery
      * Use reliable delivery service with tracking options

      * Confirm recipient’s preferred shipping address

      * Schedule delivery to arrive on or just before the occasion

4. Confirm and Follow Up
         * Track delivery status and confirm receipt if possible

         * Notify relevant internal stakeholders of gift sent

         * Follow up with client (via email or call) to ensure satisfaction

________________


Best Practices
            * Keep gift budget and company policy in mind

            * Maintain a gift log for tracking and future reference

            * Always add a handwritten or personalized message

________________


What NOT to Do
               * Don’t send generic or impersonal gifts

               * Don’t miss delivery deadlines

               * Don’t forget to check client preferences or restrictions
