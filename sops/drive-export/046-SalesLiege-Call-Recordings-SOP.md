﻿Call Recordings SOP




video instructions: Call Recordings SOP.mp4


1. Open your Google Drive folder and create a sub-folder called ‘Call Recordings’
2. Create a folder named after the year
3. In this sub-folder, create 12 sub-folders, one for each month in the year, start each folder name with the corresponding number of the month
4. In each monthly folder, create 4 sub-folders called ‘WK1, WK2, WK3, WK4’


Call recordings are vital. You must record, upload and properly file every call you do.


Steps: Fathom Configuration


1. Go to Fathom.video in your desktop


  



2. Create an account via google using your workspace google account (name@salesliege.com)


3. Go to the settings and follow the steps until google meet is ‘fully enabled’ as shown below.
  





4. Find the recording settings in the settings and turn on ‘Auto Record External Meetings’.


  



This will allow the Fathom Notetaker to join your calls automatically without you inviting it. You just have to click accept once it enters the waiting room. 




Steps: Logging and uploading




1. Once the call is finished, leave the meeting. The recording will automatically populate in fathom in the ‘My Calls’ section.


2. After the call has populated in the ‘my calls’ section, click on the call recording and select the three grey dots to download the call. 


  



3. Go to your call recording folder (bookmark for easy access as you’re going to be using it 3-8 times a day), find the month and week relevant to the call and drag the call for it to upload. 


4. Name the call recording in this format before uploading to the corresponding column: FULL NAME | DATE


5. Head into the sales tracker and copy and paste the link for the recording into the relevant column.
