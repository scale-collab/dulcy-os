🧾 Objective
To provide a clear, repeatable method for prioritizing the executive’s tasks, decisions, and commitments using the Eisenhower Matrix, ensuring critical work is addressed efficiently and distractions are minimized.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all task intake and prioritization activities performed by the EA for the executive — including email triage, scheduling, project support, and time management.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
   * Categorizing executive tasks using the Eisenhower Matrix

   * Advising the executive on priority level and scheduling

   * Delegating or deferring low-priority tasks as appropriate

   * Regularly reviewing and updating the task list

________________


📊 Eisenhower Matrix Overview
The Eisenhower Matrix helps determine what needs immediate attention vs. what can be scheduled, delegated, or removed.


	Urgent
	Not Urgent
	Important
	DO now
	DECIDE when to do (schedule)
	Not Important
	DELEGATE to others
	DELETE (eliminate, ignore)
	________________


🔁 Workflow: Prioritizing with the Eisenhower Matrix
✅ 1. Gather Incoming Tasks
Collect all executive-related tasks from:
      * Email

      * Calendar invites and meeting actions

      * Verbal instructions

      * Slack/Teams/Notion

      * Project updates or dashboards

Create a master list (daily or weekly).
________________


🧠 2. Evaluate Each Task
Ask these questions for every task:
         * Urgency: Does this require immediate attention or have a deadline within 24–48 hours?

         * Importance: Does this contribute directly to the executive’s goals, performance, or responsibilities?

Then classify into one of four categories:
Quadrant
	Definition
	Action
	Q1: Do
	Urgent + Important
	Schedule immediately or today
	Q2: Decide
	Not Urgent + Important
	Block time in calendar
	Q3: Delegate
	Urgent + Not Important (for executive to do)
	Assign to EA or appropriate team
	Q4: Delete
	Not Urgent + Not Important
	Remove or deprioritize
	________________


🗂 3. Assign Tasks Accordingly
Category
	EA Action
	Do
	Add directly to executive’s Motion/Calendar; alert them if not already aware
	Decide
	Propose available time blocks to schedule task or follow up later in week
	Delegate
	Reassign via email/Slack with clear context; track for follow-up
	Delete
	Archive or remove from backlog; confirm with executive if unsure
	________________

	Email / Slack

🧑‍💼 5. Weekly Review with Executive
Hold a Weekly Prioritization Sync (15–30 min):
            * Present categorized tasks

            * Confirm upcoming deadlines

            * Review what's been completed or needs escalation

            * Adjust priorities based on new input or changes

Tip: Use a color-coded visual matrix (Notion table, Google Sheet, or printed) to simplify discussion.

✅ Best Practices
               * Always prioritize impact over volume

               * If unsure, ask: “Does this support the executive’s top 3 goals this month?”

               * Don’t hesitate to reclassify tasks during weekly reviews

               * Use calendar time blocking to reserve energy for Q2 work

               * Avoid clogging the calendar with low-value Q3 or Q4 items

________________


❌ What NOT to Do
                  * Don’t schedule Q4 (Delete) tasks

                  * Don’t auto-delegate without confirming clarity/context

                  * Avoid labeling tasks “Urgent” without deadline verification

                  * Don’t let Q2 (important but not urgent) items pile up — they often become Q1 emergencies
