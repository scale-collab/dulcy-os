﻿Client Reporting & Check In Meetings SOP


Purpose:
This SOP outlines the structured process for client engagement, reporting, and check-ins to ensure client satisfaction, retention, and ongoing campaign success.


Key Responsibilities:
✅ Engage with clients twice weekly (Monday & Friday) for updates and planning
✅ Weekly performance insights email every Friday—focus on trends, takeaways, and strategy, not just numbers (since stats are already in the Google Sheet)
✅ Record a 4-5 minute video walkthrough summarizing campaign insights and recommendations
✅ Bi-weekly 1-on-1 check-in calls to maintain strong relationships and gather deeper feedback
✅ Immediately update the team after check-ins to keep alignment across campaigns

Step 1: Weekly Client Engagement (Monday & Friday)
Monday Check-In: Setting the Plan for the Week
A quick check-in via Slack to:
        •        Recap the previous week’s key trends
        •        Outline upcoming adjustments and tests
        •        Ask if they have any concerns or input
✅ Example Monday Message:
        Subject: Weekly Focus for {{Client Name}}
        Hi {{Client Name}},
        Hope you had a great weekend! Here’s what we’re focusing on this week:
        •        [Brief highlight of last week’s insights]
        •        [Planned adjustments or tests]
        •        [Any shifts based on replies/lead feedback]
        Let me know if there’s anything specific you’d like us to tweak or focus on. Looking forward to a strong week ahead!


Friday Check-In: Insights, Strategy & Video Recap
Every Friday, instead of sending just a report (since that’s automated), focus on providing strategic insights and action points.
What to include in your Friday Check-In:
        •        Key Takeaways from the campaign this week (reply trends, quality of leads, etc.)
        •        Patterns in responses—Are we seeing objections? Interest? Ignoring?
        •        Adjustments we’re making—New angles, scripts, targeting changes
        •        Next steps for optimization
        •        Loom video (4-5 minutes) walking through everything
✅ Example Friday Message:
        Subject: Weekly Campaign Insights & Next Steps for {{Client Name}}
        Hi {{Client Name}},
        🔍 Key Takeaways from this Week:
        •        [Any notable trends in replies]
        •        [Any positive patterns or objections that stand out]
        🎯 Next Steps & Strategy Adjustments:
        •        [Messaging adjustments]
        •        [Changes in targeting]
        •        [New A/B tests or campaign ideas]
        📹 Here’s a quick video going over everything: [Insert Loom Link]
        Let me know if you have any thoughts or if you’d like to discuss anything in more detail.


Step 2: Weekly/Bi-Weekly/Monthly 1-on-1 Check-In Calls
Every two weeks, offer a scheduled 1-on-1 strategy call with each client (recurring to make it easier). This call is their opportunity to discuss feedback, explore new strategies, and ensure they’re happy with the campaign.
Check-In Call Format:
✅ Intro: Quick recap of performance & trends
✅ Client Feedback: Their thoughts on lead quality, messaging, and objections
✅ Campaign Strategy: What’s working, what needs adjusting
✅ New Opportunities: Fresh campaign ideas or optimizations
✅ Action Plan: Confirm next steps and any takeaways
✅ Example Meeting Agenda:
        Performance Review (15 mins)
        •        Go over campaign insights (not just stats, but trends & responses)
        •        Discuss positive replies and objections
        Client Feedback (10 mins)
        •        What are they happy with? Any concerns?
        •        Are leads aligning with their expectations?
        Next Steps (5 mins)
        •        What optimizations are we making?
        •        Any new tests or shifts in messaging?
After the call, send a quick recap summarizing key points and next steps.
✅ Example Post-Call Recap:
        Subject: Recap of Today’s Check-In Call
        Hi {{Client Name}},
        Great catching up today! Here’s a quick recap of what we discussed:
        •        Performance Highlights: [Key insights]
        •        Your Feedback: [Any client concerns/input]
        •        Next Steps: [Adjustments, tests, or ideas moving forward]
        Let me know if I missed anything. Looking forward to keeping the momentum going!


Step 3: Keep Clients Updated in Slack
Clients should always feel informed about the progress of their campaigns. Use Slack to provide real-time updates on everything that is happening.
What to Update Clients On in Slack:
✅ Onboarding & Setup
        •        Domains purchased & set up
        •        Email accounts created & warmed up        
•        Automations + LinkedIn creation
        •        Initial campaign strategy + timeline
✅ Campaign Launch Updates
        •        First batch of emails sent
        •        Early results and feedback
        •        Adjustments being made
✅ Ongoing Performance Updates
        •        Any major positive trends in responses
        •        Any objections/issues we are noticing
        •        Planned optimizations & A/B testing
✅ Technical Issues or Adjustments
        •        Email deliverability concerns
        •        Any delays or unexpected challenges
        •        Fixes & solutions we are implementing
✅ Campaign Scaling & New Strategies
        •        When we are increasing volume
        •        When we are testing new messaging
        •        When we are launching new campaigns
✅ Example Slack Message:
        🚀 Campaign Update - {{Client Name}}
        ✅ Domains & email accounts set up!
✅ First email batch went out today
🔍 Early responses: Seeing interest from {{industry segment}}
🛠 Adjustments: We’re tweaking subject lines to improve open rates
🎯 Next steps: Scaling up volume & refining messaging based on replies
        Let us know if you have any questions!
Step 4: Internal Team Updates
After every Friday report or client call, update the internal team to keep everyone aligned. This ensures that messaging, targeting, and campaign strategy remain on point.
What to update the team on:
        •        Client feedback on lead quality
        •        Any objections from leads that need addressing
        •        Adjustments to messaging or targeting
        •        New campaign tests being launched
Final Notes
🔹 Clients are high-ticket. Treat them like first-class passengers. Be proactive, over-communicate, and always offer insights.
🔹 Be strategic. Don’t just report numbers—analyze trends, anticipate challenges, and offer solutions.
🔹 Build strong relationships. The better the client experience, the longer they stay with us.
