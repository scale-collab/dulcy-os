﻿Aloware Lead Flow SOP


1. New Leads
   1. Classified as leads that opted in, within the last 24 hours. 
      1. Speed to lead is king. The faster initial outreach is completed, the higher the likelihood of a successful connection
      2. Leads contacted within 10 minutes are 400% more likely to result in a meaningful conversation. 
      3. This is your biggest lever


2. Old Leads
   1. Classified as leads that opted in longer than 24 hours prior. 
      1. Leads have an 11% chance of being connected with, on the first call, but that number increases to 90% by the 6th touch point. 
      2. These prospects are less likely to remember the ad that they clicked on, and will need to be nurtured, to ensure quality and show rate


We MUST prioritize new, incoming leads. 

When a lead opts in
* Text message within 5 mins.
   * Hey NAME, it’s just SETTER with Remote Closing Academy. Saw you just clicked on one of our ads about becoming a remote closer, and wanted to touch base to see if I might be able to help. 


   * Were you able to access the video?


   * PS. not a robot despite my lightning fast message lol


If no response by minute 10
* Call lead x2




Daily Work Flow
* Load two instances of Aloware in separate windows.
   * One instance should be looking at new incoming leads as well as leads who respond to messages. 
      * When these leads come in, they should take priority.
      * Follow Lead opt in process above 


   * When no New Leads are available
      * In the second instance of Aloware, load the power dialer with leads ranging from the day before, back 4 days (example, if today is Thursday, include leads from Sunday through Wednesday)


      * Call through each of these leads, and include a ‘bumping this up’ type message, for those who do not answer
      * Have this process running all day, if you get through all the leads, re-start this section


   * Once per day, you must allocate an hour to power dial every new lead that came in earlier that day.
      * Calls made after 4pm are 164% more likely to result in meaningful conversations, this would be a good time to leverage your new leads.
      * This is a great way to end your day. Last 1-1.5 hrs.
