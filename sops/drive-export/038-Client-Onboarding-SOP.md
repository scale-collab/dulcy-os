Steps for Client Onboarding


1. Monitor Notifications and Tasks After Payment
        •        After a client pays, you’ll receive a Slack notification and a task in Motion to start the project.
        •        The automation will mark the deal as “won” and create a Slack channel with the client. Workspaces will be created automatically inviting you and the client to the channels
        •        Your Task: Confirm the Slack channel is active and the Motion project is created. Also confirm that the client was able to join.
  

2. Send Onboarding Form Reminders
        •        First Reminder: Wait 3 days after the initial onboarding email/date. If the client hasn’t filled out the form by then, please kindly send a reminder.
        •        Follow-Up Reminders: Every 3 days, send a reminder until the form is completed.
        •        Book a Call: If the client hasn’t booked a call, remind them to schedule one to go over the onboarding details as found in their onboarding email.


3. Organize Client Information in Google Docs and Airtable
        •        Once the form is completed, a Google Doc with the client’s details will be generated.
        •        This information will automatically be added to an Airtable table with details about their email accounts and domains.
        •        Your Task: Be familiar with the information in Airtable from the onboarding form, especially the email accounts and domain names.
  



4. Purchase Client Domains and Email Accounts
        •        Refer to Purchasing Domains/Email Accounts and the created document from onboarding with the necessary domain information for purchase. Same for the set up of the required email accounts.

        •        Always Check for Instructions: Each client’s needs may vary, so ensure you’re following specific directions from our team.
