📧 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
 Rescheduling Meetings via Calendly for the Executive
Role: Executive Assistant
Owner: [EA Name]
Effective Date: [Insert Date]

________________


🧾 Objective
To ensure all meeting reschedules are handled professionally and efficiently through Calendly only, maintaining a consistent process that respects the executive’s time and availability.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to any meeting involving the executive that requires rescheduling, whether initiated internally (e.g., from Alex) or externally (e.g., via email or calendar conflict), using Calendly exclusively as the rescheduling tool.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
* Monitoring all reschedule requests

* Executing reschedules only via Calendly

* Adjusting Calendly settings if availability does not support the new request

* Ensuring no reschedules are made through Motion, Google Calendar, or any other tool

________________


🔁 Rescheduling Workflow via Calendly
✅ Step 1: Identify the Need to Reschedule
   * Trigger 1: Alex directly requests a reschedule (verbally, via Slack, email, etc.)

   * Trigger 2: You receive an email from a participant asking to move a meeting

   * Trigger 3: You proactively identify a conflict or issue in the calendar

🛑 Do not take any rescheduling action outside of Calendly.
________________


🗓️ Step 2: Reschedule in Calendly Only
      * Go to the Calendly dashboard

      * Locate the specific event type (e.g., "30-Min Intro Call with Alex")

      * Use the Reschedule option within Calendly to send a new link or manually rebook the time

      * Select a new time slot that works based on Alex’s availability

✅ Make sure the new time honors existing Motion blocks or deep work periods
________________


⚙️ Step 3: Adjust Calendly Settings if Needed
If there are no available time slots in Calendly:
         * Go to the event type settings

         * Check the following and adjust if appropriate (with discretion or executive confirmation):

            * Availability windows (e.g., extend hours or add additional days temporarily)

            * Buffer times between meetings

            * Maximum daily meetings

            * Linked calendar conflicts (ensure Motion or Google is not falsely blocking time)

⚠️ Never reschedule outside Calendly due to availability issues—always adjust the settings first.
________________


📩 Step 4: Confirm the Reschedule
               * Once rescheduled through Calendly:

                  * Ensure the participant receives the new invite

                  * Confirm that the new meeting appears on Alex’s calendar

                  * Double-check that Zoom/Google Meet links are included

                  * Notify Alex of the updated schedule if it impacts their day

________________


🔐 Notes & Best Practices
                     * Always keep the Calendly reschedule link handy in Slack/email templates

                     * Do not cancel or reschedule directly in Motion or Google Calendar—this creates sync errors

                     * If reschedule requires same-day action, flag it as urgent with Alex for confirmation

                     * Maintain consistency in event naming and invite formatting (title, link, agenda)

________________


✅ Review & Maintenance
This SOP should be reviewed quarterly or whenever:
                        * Calendly event types or availability windows are updated

                        * Alex’s calendar strategy (focus time, availability preferences) changes

                        * There is a pattern of frequent rescheduling or calendar conflicts
