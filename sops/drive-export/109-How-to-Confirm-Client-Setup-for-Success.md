How to Confirm Client Setup for Success SOP


Purpose:
To ensure every new client is properly configured inside all systems, has access to the full onboarding environment, and understands the tools and workflows needed before campaign setup begins. This SOP ensures a smooth post-sale handoff, prevents delays, and eliminates early-stage confusion.
________________


1. Objective
Immediately after a client pays, Dulcy staff must verify that:
* All systems are set up correctly

* All access is granted

* All automations fire properly

* The client knows exactly where to upload information

* No bottlenecks will delay the first two weeks of onboarding and setup

This SOP covers the full “client setup confirmation” process that typically happens during or immediately after the first onboarding call.
________________


2. Steps to Confirm Client Setup
Step 1 — Confirm Payment + Trigger Internal Setup
   * Verify that the payment was successfully processed.

   * Ensure the contract is signed and stored properly.

   * Confirm that the “New Client Setup” automations have run (Slack channel creation, Airtable entries, portal workspace creation).

________________


Step 2 — Send the Client Their Onboarding Form
      * Immediately provide the onboarding form link.

      * Confirm they received it and can access it.

      * Explain that this form is essential for:

         * Understanding their business

         * Getting logins

         * Accessing assets needed for the campaign

         * Ensuring nothing is missing during setup

Goal: The client begins filling out the onboarding form same day, so no time is lost in the 14-day warm-up window.
________________


Step 3 — Purchase Email Accounts + Domains
            * Once payment hits, purchase all email accounts and domains required for their plan.

            * Inform the client in Slack:

               * That the accounts have been purchased

               * That warm-up will begin shortly

               * That this is a key part of the 14-day window

This communication creates transparency and prevents clients from feeling like “nothing is happening.”
________________


Step 4 — Confirm Slack Channel Setup
                  * Make sure the automated Slack channel was created correctly.

                  * Confirm that:

                     * The client was invited

                     * The client joined

                     * The intro message was sent

                     * Internal team members are inside

Slack is the only communication channel. No texting, WhatsApp, email, etc.
Reiterate in the call:
“Slack is where all communication lives — this is for your protection and ours. Everything stays centralized.”
________________


Step 5 — Confirm the Client Portal Login Works
Ensure the client can log in to their personal portal (Notion/Portal system).
Verify they can see:
                        * Tasks

                        * Documents & Info section

                        * Logins tab

                        * Onboarding form responses

                        * Campaign assets once built

                        * Passwords & credentials (email account logins, domain access, etc.)

During onboarding, instruct them:
“Whenever you send us anything — assets, documents, scripts, lists, logins — add it to the portal. Do not DM files. Everything must live in the portal so the whole team can access it at any time.”
________________


Step 6 — Confirm Their Workspace Is Active
Inside Dulcy’s backend:
                           * Go to Settings → Workspace → Manage Members

                           * Confirm the client is correctly assigned the Client Role

                           * Confirm their workspace exists and is mapped properly

                           * Confirm system permissions match their plan

This prevents future “I don’t see X page” issues.
________________


Step 7 — Make Sure They Understand Every Tool
On the onboarding call, walk them through:
                              * Slack (for communication)

                              * The Client Portal (for files, tasks, uploads, onboarding form, and campaign visibility)

                              * Where to upload logins

                              * Where to see tasks

                              * Where their campaigns will appear

                              * Where to expect updates

This is brief but extremely important.
A confused client = a future cancellation.
________________


Step 8 — Confirm Internal Email Guard Setup
The client does not need access to Email Guard, but you must confirm:
                                 * Emails were added

                                 * Warm-up is enabled

                                 * The warm-up date is correct

                                 * No errors appear

                                 * Sending limits match the client’s plan

This is critical because the success of the entire system depends on email health.
________________


3. Final State = Successfully Set Up Client
A client is considered fully confirmed and ready when all the following are true:
                                    * Payment is received

                                    * Contract signed

                                    * Slack created + client is active in it

                                    * Onboarding form received

                                    * Domains + email accounts purchased

                                    * Warm-up started

                                    * Client portal access confirmed

                                    * Workspace permissions correct

                                    * Logins tab completed

                                    * All early-stage tasks created

                                    * Client understands the flow and next steps

________________


4. Outcome
By following this SOP, you eliminate:
                                       * Confusion

                                       * Missed communication

                                       * Setup delays

                                       * Lost days in the warm-up window

                                       * Early buyer’s remorse

                                       * Back-and-forth DM chaos

                                       * Misaligned expectations

And instead create:
                                          * A smooth, professional onboarding experience

                                          * High client confidence

                                          * Faster setup

                                          * Faster launch

                                          * Strong preparation for the campaign thesis & research phase
