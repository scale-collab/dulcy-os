Objective
To accurately record liquidation transactions in Xero with all relevant documentation attached for clear financial tracking and audit purposes.
________________


Workflow
1. Gather Liquidation Documents
* Collect all related liquidation documents:

   * Settlement statements

   * Receipts

   * Invoices

   * Correspondence related to liquidation

________________


2. Save Documents to Google Drive
      * Upload all liquidation documents to:
/Finance/Liquidations/YYYY/MM/

      * Use clear file names:
YYYY-MM-DD_Liquidation_[DocumentType]_CompanyName.pdf

      * Ensure all documents for each liquidation are in one subfolder for easy reference

________________


3. Record Liquidation in Xero
         * Log in to Xero

         * Navigate to the appropriate module (e.g., Business → Bills to Pay or Bank Reconciliation depending on liquidation type)

         * Enter transaction details accurately:

            * Date

            * Amount

            * Description (“Liquidation payment” or specific details)

               * Attach all relevant liquidation documents by uploading from Google Drive or directly attaching PDFs

               * Save and reconcile the transaction

________________


4. Confirm and Notify
                  * Verify the liquidation is properly recorded and documents attached

                  * Update any internal liquidation tracker

                  * Notify Executive or Finance team as required

________________


Best Practices
                     * Always attach scanned PDFs of all liquidation paperwork

                     * Maintain organized folders by date and company name

                     * Double-check transaction amounts and dates before saving

                     * Keep communication open for any discrepancies

________________


What NOT to Do
                        * Don’t record liquidation without supporting documents attached

                        * Don’t misclassify liquidation payments in Xero

                        * Don’t delay filing liquidation transactions beyond the reporting period
