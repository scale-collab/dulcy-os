﻿Case Study Campaign
What it is
A campaign that leads with a specific, real result achieved for another company and offers to share how it was done. The focus is not selling the service directly, but exposing a strategy that worked.
Why we run it
People are far more interested in how something worked than being pitched. A strong case study reframes the outreach as learning instead of selling, which lowers resistance and increases replies.
When we run it
* When there is a clear result worth sharing

* When the outcome aligns closely with what the prospect wants

* When credibility and proof matter more than novelty

How we run it for success
   * The case study must be specific (numbers, timeframe, mechanism)

   * The CTA is framed as sharing the strategy, not booking a sales call

   * Follow-ups reinforce proof, not pressure

Example scripts
Standard Case Study Opener
Hey {FIRST_NAME},
{AI generated first line that is very relevant to the prospect reading this email + also noting what you did for the competitor company}
{go more in-depth on the case study}
{CTA}
Short Case Study Ask
Hey {FIRST_NAME} — {relevant first line}
I recently just helped {case study company} accomplish {outcome} and I think we could do something similar for you.
Could I show you how it works?
Soft CTA Case Study
Hi {FirstName}, not sure if this is for you but figured I’d ask.
We’ve been helping companies like {case study} achieve {result} in {timeframe}.
Could I send over a video explaining how I did it?
