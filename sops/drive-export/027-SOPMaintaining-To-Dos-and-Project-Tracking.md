✅ Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Maintaining To-Dos and Project Tracking in Notion
 For the Executive, EA, and Team Members
* Role: Executive Assistant

* Owner: [EA Name]

* Effective Date: [Insert Date]

* Last Reviewed: [Insert Date]

________________


🧾 Objective
To establish a clear, centralized process using Notion to manage to-do lists and project tracking across three key roles: the Executive, the EA, and Team Members—ensuring visibility, alignment, and accountability.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all task and project tracking managed by the Executive Assistant across:
   1. Executive’s action items and goals

   2. EA’s own task management

   3. Cross-functional team projects requiring executive oversight or EA coordination

________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
      * Capturing and updating tasks in Notion for all three roles

      * Categorizing, prioritizing, and assigning ownership and deadlines

      * Ensuring all project dashboards are current and actionable

      * Leading or supporting weekly reviews and follow-ups

________________

🔁 Workflow for the Executive
✅ 1. Add Tasks to Executive Dashboard
Create or update tasks in Notion > Executive Dashboard with the following fields:
Field
	Description
	Task
	Clear, actionable name (e.g., “Review Q3 deck”)
	Priority
	High / Medium / Low
	Status
	To Do / In Progress / Blocked / Done
	Due Date
	Deadline or target review date
	Owner
	Executive or delegated team member
	Related Project
	Link to project (if applicable)
	Notes/Links
	Attach any relevant files or context
	🧠 2. Weekly Executive Review
During weekly syncs:
         * Review overdue, upcoming, and blocked tasks

         * Reprioritize as needed

         * Confirm new or deleted items

         * Discuss any delegation opportunities

📅 3. Calendar Sync
For high-priority items, block time in the executive’s calendar and link the task in Notion.
________________


🧍 Workflow for the EA
✅ 1. Maintain EA Dashboard
Use the EA Task Board in Notion organized by:
Section
	Examples
	Daily Admin
	Emails, scheduling, follow-ups
	Recurring Tasks
	Weekly reports, travel planning
	Project Support
	Board prep, event logistics
	Personal Exec Support
	Gifts, reservations, errands
	Each task should include:
            * Status

            * Deadline

            * Notes

            * Priority

⏰ 2. Daily Review Routine
               * Review EA dashboard every morning

               * Move tasks to “Today” section or view

               * Tag urgent blockers or follow-ups

               * Update status (Done, Waiting, Moved)

________________


👥 Workflow for Team Members & Projects
✅ 1. Use Team Projects Tracker

Field

	🔄 2. Weekly Project Sync or Check-In
                  * Host or assist with a quick team project review (15–30 min)

                  * Update statuses live or shortly after

                  * Highlight overdue or stuck items

                  * Record decisions or task updates directly in Notion

📢 3. Follow-Up & Accountability
                     * Set Notion reminders or Slack notifications

                     * Ping task owners for updates before meetings

                     * Escalate any risk items to the executive when necessary

📌 Tips & Best Practices
                        * Use views in Notion (e.g., Calendar, Board, Table) to filter by owner, status, or project

                        * Tag tasks with “Executive Only” or “EA Support” to clarify visibility

                        * Archive completed projects monthly

                        * Use linked databases to embed executive tasks inside project pages

________________


❌ What NOT to Do
                           * Don’t leave tasks without clear ownership or deadlines

                           * Don’t allow multiple task lists to live in email, Slack, and docs—centralize in Notion

                           * Avoid assigning tasks without confirming priority and due date

                           * Don’t delete past tasks without archiving (for reference and accountability)

________________


🧠 Review & Maintenance
This SOP and associated Notion dashboards should be reviewed and updated every 3–6 months, or when:
                              * Executive’s priorities or working style changes

                              * New team members or project tools are added

                              * Cross-functional tracking becomes disorganized
