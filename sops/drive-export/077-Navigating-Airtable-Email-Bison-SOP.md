What is a Sequence?
A sequence is where you write and manage your cold email scripts. This is also where you:
✅ Set up email sequences (multiple follow-ups)
✅ Write spintax (alternative words/phrases for variation)
✅ Create and test subject lines
✅ Add unique variables (e.g., {FIRST_NAME}, {COMPANY})
Why is This Important?
This is the main section you’ll be working in. To get comfortable, take some time to review active campaigns and scroll through the interface to see how it works.
Setting Up a Sequence
Each email needs a subject line. When you’re in the parent email, you’ll see the selected subject line below it.
You can:
✔️ Add new steps (follow-ups)
✔️ Adjust the delay between steps (Typically, we use 2 days between Email 1 & 2, and 3 days between Email 2 & 3.)
✔️ A/B test different call-to-actions (CTAs)—we usually test 3-5 CTAs in a one-step sequence to see what resonates best.
Example of a 3-Step Sequence
In the screenshot below, you’ll see a 3-step sequence for a new client. The email script is written on the right side, and all prospects in the sequence receive that version.
On the right-hand side, you’ll also find the “Add Subject Variable” button.
How Variables Work
We use variables to personalize emails automatically. Example variables:
        •        {FIRST_NAME}
        •        {COMPANY}
        •        {INDUSTRY}
        •        {CITY}
When contacts are uploaded, these placeholders will automatically populate with real data.
Understanding Spintax
Spintax allows us to add randomized variations in emails. Example:
💡 “Would it be {helpful | valuable | useful}?”
When the email is sent, one of the three words will be randomly chosen, keeping emails unique and improving deliverability.
Signature Format
Our standard signature includes:
✔ Best wishes, [Your Name]
✔ Company Name
✔ Position (if applicable)
✔ P.S. Line (if it fits the script)




  

The Schedule section determines when emails are sent out in a campaign.
Sending Timeframe
📅 Monday – Friday
⏰ 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST
Why This Schedule?
✅ Covers East Coast (EST) mornings when inboxes are most active.
✅ Extends until 6 PM EST to reach prospects in Pacific Time (PST) afternoons.
✅ Avoids weekends when response rates are typically lower.




  





The Settings section controls key sending limits and email configurations to maximize deliverability.
Email Sending Limits
The maximum number of emails per day depends on the number of email accounts in use.
        •        Example: If you have 100 email accounts sending 15-25 emails each, the total max per day is 2,500 emails.
        •        New Sequence Starts: Should be set to 80% of the daily limit.
        •        Example: If 2,500 emails/day are being sent, then 2,000 new sequence starts/day is the max.
Open Rate Tracking – OFF by Default
🚫 Why? Open tracking relies on a hidden 1x1 pixel in emails, which is classified as HTML code.
📌 At scale, this can hurt deliverability, so we keep it off.
Plain Text Emails – ALWAYS ON
✅ Why? Keeps emails looking natural and prevents issues with spam filters.
✅ No HTML, no formatting, no unnecessary code.
No Unsubscribe Links
🚫 Why? Unsubscribe links count as clickable links, which can lower deliverability.
✅ Instead, we use natural opt-out language to encourage engagement.
Example opt-out lines:
📌 P.S. Or tell me to pound sand if you’d like…
📌 P.S. If you’ve already got your lead system set up, tell me to file my motion to dismiss…

The Inbox Placement Test allows you to check deliverability for all email accounts in a campaign.
How It Works
📌 Manually activate a test for selected email accounts.
📌 The system sends test emails to 8 inboxes from the Email Bison platform:
        •        4 to Google (Gmail)
        •        4 to Outlook
📌 Results show whether emails:
✅ Inboxed (Good to send from)
❌ Went to Spam (Needs to be quarantined)
❓ Never received (Potential issue)
Why This Matters
💡 This test helps identify which email accounts are safe to send from and which should be quarantined & rested to improve deliverability.

The Master Inbox is where you track all incoming and outgoing emails across campaigns.

1. What You Can Do Here
✅ See all received emails, including those that landed in spam or bounced

1️⃣ Tag it as “Quarantine”
2️⃣ Set daily limit to 1
3️⃣ Test the account again in 2-3 days before reactivating


  





7. Warm Up


The Private Warm-Up Pool is a newer feature in Email Bison that helps improve deliverability by gradually warming up email accounts before full-scale sending.
1. What You Can Monitor Here
✅ Total Warm-Up Emails Sent – Number of warm-up emails being sent daily
✅ Replies Received – How many warm-up emails got a response
✅ Saved from Spam – Emails that were rescued from the spam folder
✅ Warm-Up Score – Overall health rating of the email account
✅ Warm-Up Status – Whether warm-up is on or off for each account
2. Adjusting Warm-Up Settings
✔ Bulk select email accounts to adjust the daily warm-up email limit
✔ Adjust warm-up volume based on client timeline & campaign performance
(🚀 A dashboard is being built to help track stats & optimize settings easily!)
3. New Accounts & Warm-Up Timeline
📌 New email accounts require a 14-day warm-up before they are ready for full sending.


  





8. Settings


The Settings section allows you to customize workspace details, email filters, and integrations.
1. Key Settings & What They Do
✅ Profile – Edit your personal settings.
✅ Workspace – Change the workspace name or invite members (e.g., a client’s co-founder).
✅ Contact Verification – Not in use at the moment.
✅ Email Blacklist – Prevent emails from being sent to specific addresses in a campaign.
✅ Email Host Lookup – Check which email provider a contact is using.
✅ Domain Blacklist – Blocks emails from being sent to all addresses on a specific domain.
✅ Custom Tags – Create custom tags for campaigns, contacts, and email accounts.
✅ Custom Tracking Domains – Used for tracking open rates (but we typically keep this off).
✅ Master Inbox –
        •        Change email sync settings
        •        Add warm-up word blockers (filters out warm-up emails from appearing in your inbox)
✅ Billing – No action needed here.
✅ Developer API – View or create API tokens for integrations.
✅ Webhooks –
        •        Used for contacts, emails, or campaigns
        •        A new webhook is created for every new client to capture email replies.
✅ Integrations – EmailGuard API key must be added for every new client to run inbox placement tests.
✅ Warm-Up Settings – Warm-up tags are automatically filtered out from inboxes.
