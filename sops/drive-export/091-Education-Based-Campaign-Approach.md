﻿Education-Based Campaign (Whitepaper)
What it is
An education-first campaign framed around a whitepaper, report, or industry breakdown. Instead of pitching a service, the outreach positions the conversation as research, insight-sharing, or peer learning.
Why we run it
Education campaigns lower defenses by removing the feeling of being sold to. Prospects engage because they want to:
* See how others in their space are operating

* Learn what’s working before committing to change

* Validate their current strategy against peers

This naturally qualifies interest and leads into a sales conversation after trust is established.
When we run it
   * When positioning as a thought leader matters

   * When selling a more complex or strategic service

   * When the market is skeptical of direct pitches

   * When the goal is higher-quality conversations over raw volume

How we run it for success
      * The topic must be something the market already cares about

      * The framing is “research” or “education,” not “marketing”

      * Participation feels optional and low-pressure

      * Insights are shared back with the prospect (early access, summary, or examples)

The transition into what we do happens after value is delivered.
Example scripts
Interview Whitepaper Outreach
Hi {{firstName}},
We’re putting together a whitepaper on {HEADLINE}.
We’ve already spoken with {company}, {company}, and {company}.
Would you be open to a short conversation where we can cover some of the challenges and insights we’re seeing?
Happy to share early access to the report once it’s done.
________________


Education-First, Soft CTA
Hi {{firstName}},
I’m working on a short report around {how agencies are using outbound systems to consistently bring in new opportunities} = headline.
Not sure if this is relevant for you, but figured I’d ask.
If it is, I’m happy to share what we’ve learned so far.
________________


Sharing Completed Whitepaper
Hi {firstName},
I recently wrapped up a whitepaper on {HEADLINE}.
Not sure if this would be useful, but happy to send it over if you’d like.
________________


Education → Conversation Bridge
Hey {{firstName}},
We’re finishing up a research piece on how agencies are building predictable outbound without relying on ads or referrals.
After speaking with a few teams, some clear patterns have emerged.
Would you be open to a quick conversation so I can share what we’re seeing?
