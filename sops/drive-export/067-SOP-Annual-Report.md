📅 SOP 4: Annual Report
Title: Creating the Annual Marketing Performance Report
 Audience: Executive, Board, Stakeholders
________________


🧾 Objective
To summarize the full year’s marketing performance and strategic effectiveness, tracking growth, ROI, and brand engagement metrics.
________________


🔁 Workflow
✅ 1. Aggregate Quarterly Reports
Pull Q1–Q4 campaign summaries into a central Google Doc or Slide deck.
✅ 2. Key Performance Indicators
Area
	Metrics
	Campaign Reach
	Total sends, opens, engagement
	Lead Generation
	Annual total + % increase YoY
	Conversion Performance
	By funnel stage or CTA type
	List Growth
	Total subscribers added/lost
	ROI Estimate
	Cost per lead vs. revenue contributed
	✅ 3. Year-in-Review Highlights
Create a “Wins & Milestones” slide:
* Product launch success metrics

* List growth benchmarks

* Highest performing campaign of the year

* Testimonials or quotes

✅ 4. Executive Summary Page
Provide a concise briefing:
diff
CopyEdit
   * 2025 Marketing Review Summary:
   * - Lead acquisition grew 37%
   * - 12 email campaigns launched
   * - 3 major product launches supported
   * - Most successful campaign: Fall Webinar Series (57% CTR)


✅ 5. Delivery
   * Due by January 15

   * Send with subject: 📈 2025 Marketing Year in Review

   * Include:

      * Slide Deck (Google Slides)

      * Full Data Appendix (Google Sheet)

      * Summary Report (Google Doc or 1-pager PDF)
