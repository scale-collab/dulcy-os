🧾 SOP: Doing Reservations on Behalf of the Executive
Role: Executive Assistant
Owner: [EA Name]
Effective Date: [Insert Date]
Last Reviewed: [Insert Date]
________________


Objective
To efficiently handle all types of reservations (hotels, restaurants, transportation) ensuring the executive’s preferences, schedule, and budget are respected.
________________


Workflow
1. Confirm Reservation Details
* Clarify date, time, location, and purpose of reservation

* Confirm any preferences (dietary, seating, room type, vehicle type)

* Note budget limits and approval requirements

2. Research and Book
   * Use preferred platforms or direct contacts (e.g., hotel websites, OpenTable, car services)

   * Choose options matching executive’s preferences

   * Book refundable or flexible options when possible

3. Confirm and Document
      * Save confirmation emails or receipts

      * Add reservation details to the executive’s calendar

      * Send a summary message/email to the executive with reservation info

________________


Best Practices
         * Double-check all details before finalizing

         * Communicate any changes promptly

         * Keep backup options ready for last-minute changes
