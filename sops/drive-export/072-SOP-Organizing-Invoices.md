________________


Objective
To ensure all incoming invoices are promptly organized, stored securely, and entered accurately into Xero for bookkeeping and payment processing.
________________


Workflow
1. Check Inbox Daily
* Review email inbox and shared accounts for new invoices

* Download invoice PDFs or attachments immediately

* Verify invoice details (vendor name, date, amount, PO number if applicable)

________________


2. Save to Google Drive
   * Upload invoices to the shared folder:
/Finance/Invoices/YYYY/MM/

   * Use standardized naming convention:
YYYY-MM-DD_VendorName_InvoiceNumber.pdf

   * Ensure clear folder structure for easy retrieval by month and year

________________


3. Enter Invoice in Xero
      * Log in to Xero

      * Navigate to: Business → Bills to Pay → New Bill

      * Fill in invoice details:

         * Vendor name

         * Invoice date and due date

         * Invoice number

         * Amount and currency

            * Attach the invoice PDF (upload from Google Drive if needed)

            * Assign appropriate account codes and tax settings

            * Save and submit for approval or payment as per workflow

________________


4. Confirm and Communicate
               * Confirm entry completion via internal tracker or email to relevant parties

               * Flag any discrepancies or missing details to the Executive or Finance team promptly

________________


Best Practices
                  * Check inbox at least once daily to avoid delays

                  * Maintain consistent naming and folder structure

                  * Verify all invoice details before entry to minimize errors

                  * Keep communication clear for any invoice issues

________________


What NOT to Do
                     * Don’t leave invoices unattended in inbox

                     * Don’t enter invoices without proper documentation

                     * Don’t skip verification steps before uploading or entering in Xero
