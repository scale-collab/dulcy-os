You will be given a commission tracker. You can find it in your Google Drive folder.


Commission is paid on the 3rd of every month. Your commission will have been negotiated prior to you coming onboard. If you aren’t certain or unsure of what % you are being paid, ask Max.


It looks like this: SalesLiege Sales Commission Tracker


You are responsible for keeping track of your closed deals and commission on a daily basis. If you sign up prospects on payment plans, be sure to log them in for the following months, and don’t forget!


We will verify your commission on the 2nd of every month, 1 day before payouts are due. 




Steps


1. Once you’ve closed a deal open your commission tracker provided in your Google drive folder.


2. Fill the row for a new sale.


3. If the customer is on a payment plan, put them in the sheet for the following months. E.g. if you know they paid 12/11/21 at $1930 with a 3 month plan, put them in 01/11/22 for $1930 too, and also 02/11/22.


IMPORTANT…


If you sign a prospect on a payment plan and they cancel future payments, you will NOT be paid commission on their future payments. We can only pay sales commissions on payments we receive. In the event of a cancellation (which is very rare), we will notify you.


If we have to refund a prospect your commission is safe and cannot be clawed back.




















Payouts


Your commission payouts will be on the 3rd of each month, paid via Wire transfer directly to your bank. We will need your bank details. 


Note that we employ all reps as freelancers, so will require an invoice from you. If you run an agency, you can just invoice us through your company and we will pay your company. 


Please provide Max with your bank details. We’ll need your name, account number, sort code (UK) or SWIFT (USA), and your address. 

If possible setup a Wise bank account. This is the business bank account we use ourselves and will allow us to transfer you your money instantly and without any fees.


Below is an invoice template for your monthly invoicing to us. 


Commission cannot be paid until an invoice is provided.


1. Create a folder in your Google drive folder called ‘Invoices’


2. Once we’ve verified your payout on the 1st of each month, use the below template to create an invoice. Upload this invoice to your Google drive (name them 1. Jan 22, 2. Feb 22, 3. Mar 22, etc) and then copy and paste that link into your commission tracker cell (see screenshot)


3. GET PAID
