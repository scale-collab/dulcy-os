﻿Risk Reversal Campaign
What it is
A direct offer paired with a clear guarantee or downside protection. The goal is to remove fear around trying something new.
Why we run it
Most prospects hesitate not because they don’t want results, but because they’ve been burned before. Risk reversal shifts the mental equation from “what if this fails?” to “what if this works?”
When we run it
* When skepticism is high

* When the outcome can be clearly defined

* When confidence in delivery is strong

How we run it for success
   * The guarantee must be simple and believable

   * The outcome must be measurable

   * The email stays short and confident, not defensive

Example scripts
Guarantee With Proof
Hi {FirstName},
If we leveraged what we did for {case study} I’m confident we could {offer}.
Just did something similar where they saw {result} in {timeframe}.
Would you be open to a quick walkthrough?
P.S. If we don’t hit {result} in {timeframe}, {guarantee}
Plan + Guarantee
Hi {firstName},
We help {niche} {offer}.
Here’s the plan we’d run:
{plan}
P.S. If we don’t hit {result} in {timeframe}, {guarantee}
