1. Purpose
This SOP teaches the team how to extract deep, practical market insights before building any campaign.
The goal is to understand:
* What the client does

* Why their service matters

* Who their market truly is

* What pains, language, and triggers drive buying behavior

The better the research → the stronger the messaging → the higher the campaign performance.
________________


2. Why This Matters
Most clients do NOT know how to speak about their offer in an outbound context.
They may be experts in their craft, but they often:
   * Give vague descriptions

   * Use outdated marketing terminology

   * Have unclear ICP definitions

   * Struggle to articulate pain points

Your leverage comes from understanding their market better than they do, so you can translate their value into messaging that resonates.
________________


3. The Data Hierarchy (Best → Worst)
This is the framework we use to determine the quality of research sources.
Tier 4 – Worst Quality: AI, Google, & Generic Articles
      * High-level, surface-level info

      * Echoed opinions, not real pain

      * Not suitable for forming messaging

      * Only useful for getting context or definitions

Tier 3 – Good: Communities (Reddit, Niche Forums, FB Groups)
         * Real people sharing real frustrations

         * Gives raw language directly from the market

         * Great for finding hidden pains and emotional drivers

         * Useful for extracting patterns and common objections

Example: Searching “manufacturing form automation” on Reddit yields direct complaints like:
“We’re tired of spreadsheets”
“This process is slow and manual”
These insights are better than anything AI will generate.
Tier 2 – Better: Network Interviews
            * Speak with industry-adjacent people

            * Other agencies, partners, or vendors

            * Great for understanding:

               * Buying behaviors

               * Industry norms

               * Common blockers

               * Messaging that already works

This is where proactive team members gain massive advantages.
Tier 1 – Best: Prospect Interviews
This is the gold standard.
You call people in the ICP and ask them questions directly, using the “UCF student” angle if needed.
Example (real from your transcript):
Calling local Realtors to test Voice AI demand before launching a campaign.
This provides:
                  * Real responses

                  * Real pains

                  * Real buying objections

                  * Real decision-making logic

1 prospect interview > 100 Google searches.
Tier 0 – Absolute Best: Customer Interviews (Provided by Client)
If available, this is the most accurate data possible.
These recordings show:
                     * What their customers hated before buying

                     * What emotion pushed them to buy

                     * What result they valued most

                     * What language they used to describe the problem

This should always be reviewed if the client provides it.
________________


4. What You’re Trying to Learn (Core Questions)
Every piece of research should help answer these:
Mindset Before Buying
                        * What were they thinking?

                        * What did they believe?

                        * What misconceptions did they have?

Pains Before Buying
                           * What were they struggling with daily?

                           * What consequences did that create?

Desires Before Buying
                              * What outcome did they want but couldn’t get?

Path to Discovering the Solution
                                 * How did they learn solutions existed?

Moment They Decided to Buy
                                    * What flipped the switch?

                                    * Why now?

                                    * Why this vendor?

Operational Questions to Dig Deeper
Ask things like:
                                       * “How were you solving this before?”

                                       * “How much time/money was wasted?”

                                       * “What felt frustrating about the old way?”

                                       * “How did you track progress before?”

                                       * “What would have happened if you didn’t fix this?”

You will NOT use every question — but you must understand the spirit behind them.

5. How to Execute Market Research
________________


Step 1 — Review the Onboarding Form
Clients often fill these out poorly.
If anything is unclear, incomplete, or vague → schedule a clarification call.
Never assume the form is accurate.
________________


Step 2 — Review Their Website
Look for:
                                          * What they think their core value is

                                          * What services they highlight

                                          * Case studies (very important)

                                          * Jargon or repeated language

                                          * Obvious ICP clues

But do not rely on the website for messaging.
________________


Step 3 — Research Their Market via Communities
Examples:
                                             * Reddit

                                             * Facebook groups

                                             * Discord groups

                                             * Industry forums

                                             * LinkedIn comment sections

Search:
“{industry} problem”
“{industry} automation”
“{industry} common mistakes”
“why doesn’t {industry} do X”
Extract real language and real frustrations.
________________


Step 4 — Conduct Network Interviews
Find:
                                                * Competitors

                                                * Service providers

                                                * Similar agencies

                                                * People who sell into the same ICP

Ask what they’re seeing in the market.
________________


Step 5 — Conduct Prospect Interviews (The Gold Standard)
Use the “student project” framing:
“Hi, my name is ____. I’m doing a research project for school on {industry} tools. Could I ask you two quick questions? It would help a lot.”
You can do this with:
                                                   * Realtors

                                                   * Manufacturers

                                                   * IT directors

                                                   * Marketers

                                                   * Owners

                                                   * Any ICP

Goal: gather real objections, pains, and desires BEFORE launching the campaign.
________________


Step 6 — Review Customer Interviews (If Provided)
These are often the best insight source because the customer already bought the solution.
Study:
                                                      * What convinced them

                                                      * What they struggled with

                                                      * What emotional triggers were mentioned

                                                      * How they described the problem

________________


6. How to Use the Research
All insights must translate into:
1. Stronger Pain Points
Specific → Emotional → Real wording
2. Better Angles
Based on what the market actually cares about, not guesses.
3. More Accurate ICP Segmentation
Adjust targeting based on real demand signals.
4. Improved Offers
Match offer strength to buyer readiness.
5. Cleaner Messaging Across Channels
Cold email, LinkedIn, Voice AI scripts — all become sharper.
________________


7. Practical Example (From Transcript)
You called ~20 Realtors to evaluate demand for Voice AI.
Finding:
“Realtors see the value, but most believe they personally wouldn’t use it.”
Insight:
                                                         * The market is solution-aware

                                                         * But low commitment

                                                         * High skepticism

                                                         * Perceives Voice AI as “nice-to-have,” not “must-have”

Action:
                                                            * You scrapped the campaign

OR

                                                            * You adjust messaging to attack the skepticism head-on

This is exactly how research protects the client AND improves performance.
________________


8. Summary (What the Team Must Remember)
Do NOT rely on AI, Google, or the onboarding form alone.
Best → worst order:
                                                               1. Customer Interviews

                                                               2. Prospect Interviews

                                                               3. Network Interviews

                                                               4. Communities

                                                               5. Articles / AI

The goal is simple:
Understand the industry so deeply that your messaging feels like it was written from inside the market.
