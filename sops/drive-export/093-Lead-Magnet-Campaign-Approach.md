﻿Lead Magnet Campaign
What it is
A campaign that offers something tangible and valuable upfront, with no commitment. The free asset directly connects to the core service. The free asset should be good enough, someone is willing to pay for it. It also should solve a problem, that then creates a new problem that is solved by your offer.
Why we run it
Giving value first builds trust faster than claims. A good lead magnet also pre-qualifies interest because only people with the underlying problem engage.
When we run it
* When trust needs to be built quickly

* When the market ignores direct pitches

* When the free asset naturally leads to a bigger conversation

How we run it for success
   * The lead magnet must be something people would normally pay for

   * It must solve a real sub-problem, not be generic

   * The CTA asks permission, not commitment

Example scripts
Straight Lead Magnet
Could I send over 1,000 ideal customer leads similar to {CASE STUDY} (full name, email, company name, LinkedIn, etc.) for you to use in outbound?
Free Resource – Expect to Believe
Hey {FIRSTNAME} — I saw {SOMETHING RELEVANT}.
We help companies achieve {results}.
But I’d never expect you to believe me without giving you something first.
I made you a {resource}. Want me to send it over?
Lead Magnet Subject Line
Subject: {LEADMAG}
{FIRST_NAME} — I just finished a report on {LEADMAG}.
Would you like to see it?
