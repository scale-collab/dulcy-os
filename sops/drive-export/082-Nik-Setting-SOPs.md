3. Program / Service Design Model
This section is where we look closer at the 4 Pillars (Content, Coaching, Community, Culture) and help you better understand & design your program.
MODEL 1: Evergreen course
The evergreen course model is simple. It’s typically a lot of content with a sprinkle of community, and a dash of coaching/support. These programs are typically “cheaper” in investment, their success rate is low (<25%).
Who This Is Best For:
                  * B2C Markets
                  * Biz Opp / beginners, Dating, Fitness, Themepages, Reselling, Dropshipping, SMMA courses etc
                  * B2B Markets
                  * Front end course to generate customers & them ultimately turn them into clients. Not a good idea typically until beyond 7-figures.
How does it look:
                  * Very In-Depth Course Material
                  * 1-2x Group Coaching Calls Per Week
                  * Discord/Skool Group - For asking Q’s & sharing wins.
                  * Email/Community Manager Support
                  * Price Points / Timelines
                  * $500 → $3000
                  * Lifetime Access or 6/12 months
                  * Key Distinctions:
                  * As you scale, you’ll want to add more group coaching calls (up to 5-6, then multiple per day if needed)
                  * The key to making this model work, always, is having VERY good course material. (Sam Ovens Consulting Accelerator is a great reference for this. Really good material.)
                  * With enough students/clients you’ll likely hire a community manager. This role is more of your “help desk” person. They are reactive, as opposed to proactive.
                  * Group Onboarding Call (Optional - But Preferred At Scale)
                  * Can be 30 min, 1-2x weekly that helps clients understand the path to success, set expectations, mitigate refund/cancels, etc.
💡 For most coaches, this is not the right model. While it provides scalability because it is so “hands off”, it really doesn’t produce great clients results.


MODEL 2: 8-16 Week Coaching Intensive
The goal here is to still have a robust training curriculum (Similar to the evergreen course) AND then add in coaching & support.
This model is much more hands-on, and because the coaching culture is more proactive the community will typically be more alive & vibrant.
The key here is giving the clients exactly what they need, strategically leveraging each of the 4 pillars - So we can find that sweet spot of great clients results with as little time & energy from you & your team as possible.
Who This Is Best For:
                  * B2C Markets (higher investment markets)
                  * Biz Opp / Beginners, Dating, Fitness
                  * Small B2B Markets:
                  * Coaches, consultants, agencies, for example…
                  * Current e-commerce biz owners
                  * Real estate agents, insurance agents, financial advisers, etc
                  * Bigger B2B Companies
                  * 1M/yr+
                  * SaaS companies
How does it look:
                  * In-Depth Course Material
                  * Skool Group - Questions, Coaching & Support.
                  * Community Manager Support
                  * Dedicated success coaches or Account Managers
                  * 1:1 or group Onboarding Calls (Optional, but preferred)
                  * Periodic 1 on 1 Coaching (Optional, but preferred)
                  * The key difference with here vs. evergreen course is asking yourself, “what’s the timeline to success for my client?” and how can we create speed to go from A → B asap.
If the goal is measurable & they can succeed in a short timeframe, go with this model
                  * Price Points / Timelines
                  * $3500-$15.000 (sometimes as high as 18-24k)
                  * 8wk → 16wk coaching
                  * Lifetime access to content
Key Distinctions
                  * Group Calls:
                  * Scaling is the same as I mentioned above
                  * Great course material is still obviously important. But 1o1 support can help fill in the gaps.
                  * You’ll either want to have a dedicated success coach or Account Managers.
                  * These are 1-1 coaches / inside sales guys who do onboarding calls and (on average) one 1 on 1 call every 1-2 weeks
                  * They’ll also coordinate with the rest of the team on what clients needs are, and map out clients implementation pathway
                  * They’ll do 1-1 onboarding, 1 on 1 coaching, and slack/discord
                  * You’ll want better client management systems here
                  * Asana, Monday.com, or even a CRM like close.com or hubspot.
                  * You’ll want noting on every client by the success coach.
                  * And a weekly report and recap on every clients progress each week.
                  * Identifying and deciding on your channels of support
                  * Messenger, Slack, Voxer, Email. The point is finding something that works for you and your clients.
Summary:
                  * 8-16 weeks
                  * Training curriculum that is the foundation
                  * Group calls every week
                  * 1:1 call with a coach every 1-2 weeks
                  * Discord/Skool/Slack chat access
                  * Culture is PROACTIVELY looking for ways to support the clients, as opposed to reactively waiting for clients to complain or ask for help.
MODEL 3: DFY/DWY Hybrid
While this model still has content, coaching & community and the client is still responsible for a lot of the learning & implementation… This model also assumes more of the risk, as we are now responsible for delivering certain aspects of clients results.
Most clients that you onboard with a DFY structure most of the time have a lot of expectations.
Done well, This model can be a great way to charge more AND ensure clients get even better results. But executed poorly and you’ll end up with a lot of disappointed clients & a ruined reputation.
The lesson with this model is while it’s harder to deliver DFY services, it can be both better for you (more $) and better for the client (better results).
Who This Is Best For:
                  * Small B2B Markets (Premium Priced)
                  * Coaches, consultants, agency’s, for example…
                  * Current e-commerce biz owners
                  * Real estate agents, insurance agents, financial advisers, etc
                  * Bigger B2B Companies
                  * 1M/yr+
                  * SaaS companies
                  * Agency-type business models.
How does it look:
                  * Group Coaching Calls Per Week (Optional - But Preferred)
                  * Course Material (Optional - But Preferred)
                  * Dedicated Account Manager
                  * 1 on 1 Onboarding Calls
                  * 1 on 1 Support
                  * Skool / Slack / Discord
                  * DFY Service Departments
                  * Recruiting Team
                  * Ads Teams
                  * Design Team
                  * ...all really depends on what you’re doing. These teams coordinate with the acct manager. The success coach/account manager is the dedicated point of contact.
                  * Price Points / Timelines
                  * $10k-$50k+
                  * Retainers ($1000-$2500/m+)
