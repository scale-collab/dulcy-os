Writing Style & Tone


        1.        Keep it Conversational:
        •        Write like you’re talking to a friend. Avoid formal language and make it sound relatable.
        •        Use simple, clear language to get your point across.


        2.        Add Emphasis with Short Sentences or Breaks:
        •        Break up long thoughts with short sentences. (THE Dash to copy and paste: “—”)
        •        Use caps sparingly to emphasize certain words, e.g., “The biggest mistake? TALKING about yourself.”


        3.        Make it Valuable:
        •        Every tweet should offer something useful or interesting. Think of it as giving a small piece of advice, insight, or lesson.

Quick Tips for Success:
        •        Personalize when possible: If Alexander has a personal experience to share, use that in the tweets. Don’t be afraid to ask!!
        •        Check for Spelling & Grammar: Always double-check for any errors before posting.
        •        Stick to the Schedule: Follow the Monday-Sunday schedule to keep tweets balanced and interesting.


By following these guidelines, you’ll be able to create tweets that align with Alexander’s style and offer valuable insights to the audience. If you’re unsure about a tweet, ask for feedback!
