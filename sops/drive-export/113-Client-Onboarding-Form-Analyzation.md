Client Onboarding Form Analyzation SOP


Applies to: All new Dulcy clients after contract is signed and payment is received
Goal: Leave the client with total clarity on next steps, timelines, expectations, and who’s doing what, so they feel confident and taken care of.
________________


1. Purpose of This Call
* Introduce yourself as their main point of contact.

* Clarify roles (who’s who on the Dulcy side).

* Make sure the onboarding form has been completed and reviewed.

* Walk them through:

   * Timeline from today → first campaigns live.

   * What happens during warmup.

   * How we’ll test offers and segments.

   * What they should expect in terms of meetings and results.

      * Align on communication and next steps.

________________


2. Prerequisites (Before You Book or Take the Call)
You should not take the onboarding call until:
         1. Contract is signed in PandaDoc.

         2. Initial payment is made (or approved if your process allows the call between signed & paid).

         3. Onboarding form is completed by the client.

         4. You’ve:

            * Opened and read their onboarding form in Airtable.

            * Understood:

               * Who they sell to (ICPs, industries, locations, company size).

               * Their offer(s), case studies, results, and goals.

               * Any exclusions or “do not contact” lists.

                  * Watched any example onboarding call recordings if you’re new.

If the form is filled out less than ~1 hour before the call and you haven’t reviewed it, reschedule. You must come into this call prepared.

                                          * AI note generated with pain points & context.

                                             * Email is sent to client:

                                                * “Let’s begin onboarding” + onboarding form link + onboarding call booking link.

                                                   * Slack:

                                                      * Private client channel is auto-created.

                                                      * Core Dulcy team + client are invited.

                                                         * Workspaces:

                                                            * Dulcy/email bison workspace is created.

                                                            * Email Guard workspace is set up.

                                                            * HeyReach workspace is created & invites sent.

You don’t need to run these manually, but you do need to:
                                                               * Join the new workspaces (Email Guard, Email Bison/Dulcy, HeyReach).

                                                               * Monitor the Slack channel and respond promptly.

                                                                           2. Skim AI notes in Close for additional context.

6. Opening the Call
                                                                                                1. Introduce yourself clearly.

 Example:

“Hey [Name], great to meet you. My name is [Your Name], I’m a Client Success Manager here at Dulcy.

 My job is to own your outbound setup, manage the campaigns, and make sure we’re moving toward booked meetings and revenue. I’ll be your main point of contact day-to-day.”

                                                                                                2. Clarify roles and avoid confusion.

                                                                                                   * Explain:

                                                                                                      * You are their primary contact.

                                                                                                      * How they’ll interact with:

                                                                                                         * Sales (if they see them again).

                                                                                                         * Any other Dulcy team members (copy, ops, etc.).

                                                                                                            * Goal: They should never wonder “Who’s actually taking care of me?”

                                                                                                               3. Set the agenda.

 Example:

“Today I want to:

                                                                                                                  * Make sure your onboarding form answers give us everything we need.

                                                                                                                  * Walk you through the exact process from today through launch and beyond.

                                                                                                                  * Align on expectations — how we test, what results we’re aiming for, and timing.

 Then we’ll leave with clear next steps on both sides.”

________________


7. Confirm Onboarding Form & Expectations
                                                                                                                     1. Ask if they had any trouble filling out the form.

                                                                                                                     2. If you see weak/incomplete answers, call it out gently:

                                                                                                                        * Example:

“On this question about your most recent monetary win, your answer was a bit light.

 This form is not ‘homework’ for you — it’s what we use to avoid emailing you 100 times later for missing info.

 Can we go a bit deeper on that now so we can set the campaign up properly?”

                                                                                                                           3. Use the call to refine or fill any gaps:

                                                                                                                              * Case studies.

                                                                                                                              * ICP details.

                                                                                                                              * Exclusion lists.

                                                                                                                              * Goals (revenue, booked calls).

Log clarifications in the onboarding form / client portal after the call.
________________


8. Explain the Onboarding Roadmap (Timeline & Phases)
Walk them through the high-level timeline using your roadmap diagram.
Phase 1 — Setup & Warmup (≈ 14 days)
                                                                                                                                 * Confirm or initiate:

                                                                                                                                    * Purchase + setup of domains and inboxes (if not already done).

                                                                                                                                    * Warmup on Dulcy’s private pool.

                                                                                                                                       * During this 14-day warmup period, Dulcy will:

                                                                                                                                          * Write initial scripts (first-step + follow-ups).

                                                                                                                                          * Run market research.

                                                                                                                                          * Build and verify lead lists.

                                                                                                                                          * Formulate their no-brainer offer(s).

                                                                                                                                          * Choose and prepare 2–3 segments/industries to test.

Important:
Explain clearly that the 14-day warmup is non-negotiable technical reality — but it is not idle time. You’re doing deep work behind the scenes.
Phase 2 — Offer Validation (First Launch)
                                                                                                                                             * Strategy:

                                                                                                                                                * Pick 2 primary industries/segments (usually “lowest hanging fruit” and top performers).

                                                                                                                                                * For each:

                                                                                                                                                   * Build a targeted list (10K–20K leads per segment).

                                                                                                                                                   * Test up to ~10 wildly different first-step sequences (no-brainer, educational, different hooks).

                                                                                                                                                      * Goal:

                                                                                                                                                         * Get offer validation fast, not tiny, slow A/B tests.

                                                                                                                                                            * Explain sample size:

                                                                                                                                                               * Each variant should have ~1,000+ leads to be statistically meaningful.

                                                                                                                                                               * Sometimes you find a winner in the first few thousand sends; sometimes it takes more.

Phase 3 — Scaling & Optimization
Once a winner is found:
                                                                                                                                                                  * Double down on:

                                                                                                                                                                     * Winning offer.

                                                                                                                                                                     * Winning segment.

                                                                                                                                                                     * Winning CTA.

                                                                                                                                                                        * Then:

                                                                                                                                                                           * Build out 2–3 step sequences.

                                                                                                                                                                           * Add hyper-personalization where appropriate.

                                                                                                                                                                           * Expand to related industries/segments.

                                                                                                                                                                           * Scale inbox sending safely (e.g. up to 150/day per inbox over time, as per Dulcy standards).

9. Set Expectations Around Results & Value of Setup
                                                                                                                                                                              * Be honest and confident:

                                                                                                                                                                                 * Some clients see meetings in first 2 weeks of live sending.

                                                                                                                                                                                 * Others see things really ramp up in months 2–3.

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Clarify:

                                                                                                                                                                                       * Their upfront investment heavily funds setup (domains, accounts, research, copy, systems).

                                                                                                                                                                                       * This is necessary to avoid deliverability issues and to find something truly scalable.

You want them to walk away thinking:
“Okay, there’s a clear plan. They’re not winging it. I understand why this takes time and what they’re doing during that time.”

                                                                                                                                                                                                               * There are deliverability issues.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Handled (who replies, tone, CTA).

Show them the basics of the inbox manager / RevReply setup and:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Ask what tone they prefer.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Clarify:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * We handle everything until booked call.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * If they want help improving their sales process, we can assist there too (cold sales audit).

SOP: Client Onboarding Form Analyzation
Owner: CSM
Applies to: Every new client immediately after they complete the onboarding form
Goal: Extract everything you need from the form to design a strong campaign thesis: target, offer, angles, exclusions, and goals.
________________


1. Purpose
The onboarding form is your primary source of truth for:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Who we’re targeting.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * What we’re selling.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Why people buy it.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * What results matter.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * What to avoid.

Done right, analyzing it means:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Fewer back-and-forth questions.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Faster and better campaign setup.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Higher odds of early results.

________________


2. When to Analyze
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * As soon as you get a Slack notification:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * “Onboarding form complete” in the contract events channel.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Before:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * The onboarding call.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Any campaign thesis or lead list build.

________________


3. Where to Find the Form
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    1. Go to Slack → Contract Events.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Look for the onboarding form completion notification (paper icon).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          2. Click the link → opens Airtable → Onboarding table.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          3. In Airtable:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Go to Data → Onboarding.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Find the record for the client.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Click the expand icon on the left to see the full record (do not try to read in grid view only).

This record is usually already linked to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Workspace ID.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Client workspace.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Internal automations.

________________


4. High-Level Flow of Analysis
You’re reading the form with three main goals:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   1. Define the ICP & list-building strategy

 → From “Identifying your ideal client” section.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   2. Define the emotional story & pain points

 → From “Client emotional journey” section.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   3. Define the offer, unique mechanism & proof

 → From “Offer & results” section.

Everything else supports those three.

5. Section 1 – Identifying the Ideal Client (For Lead Lists)
Use this section to map how you’ll build and segment lists in Apollo/Clay.
Key fields to analyze:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      1. Who takes the sales calls / sending identity

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Name of the person whose name emails will be sent under.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * This name should match the inboxes you create (recommended for familiarity).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            2. Profile picture for email accounts

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Must be provided.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * No picture = delay in account creation.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * If missing, you must chase it immediately.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  3. Website, LinkedIn, login details, 2FA secret

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * The LinkedIn 2FA secret key:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Not the 6-digit app code.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * The longer activation key from LinkedIn’s 2FA settings (see HeyReach doc).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Used to maintain persistent LinkedIn login for HeyReach.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              4. Meeting link

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Their existing calendar booking link, if any.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * This is what you’ll use in RevReply / inbox management.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * If “No”:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Note that we must create one for them.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       5. Ideal client details

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Who they want us to send personalized emails to.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Any special job titles that matter in their niche.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * 5–10 “dream clients”:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * These can be used to reverse-engineer lookalike lists.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                6. Existing prospect lists

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * If they upload lists or give us lists to start with:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Note what they contain (companies, contacts, etc.).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Decide how/if they should be blended into our strategy.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         7. Exclusion lists

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Current or past clients or any “do not contact”.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * You must:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Store this carefully.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Use it to build Apollo exclusions.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * This is critical to avoid embarrassing outreach to their own customers.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     8. Location, job titles, company size

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Location:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * US / Canada / EU / specific regions.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Job titles:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 * Use them as a base, but apply your own judgment (owners/founders often matter even when they didn’t list them).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Company size:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * 1–10 vs 11–50 vs 51–200 should become separate campaigns, not all lumped into one.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          9. Industry / additional industries / exclusions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Use their choices to:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Select Apollo industries.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Exclude wrong ones (e.g., marketing agencies if they’re a marketing agency).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   10. Keywords & technology tools

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * Keywords:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         * Used for Apollo / Clay filtering.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Tech tools:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * E.g., WooCommerce, Magento, Wix.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Critical for tool-based targeting (e.g., migrating to Shopify).

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  11. Funding / job postings

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Recently funded?

→ Helps decide whether you want a “funding” filter.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Job postings:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * E.g., companies hiring “Head of Marketing”.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Can become another filtered segment.

Outcome of this section:
You should be able to say clearly:
“We’re going to target [X company size] [Y industries] in [Z locations], using [these job titles], with [these tech signals and exclusions], split into [#] segments.”

6. Section 2 – Client Emotional Journey (For Copy & Offers)
This section exists to help you write highly relevant copy and offers, not generic fluff.
Look for:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           1. Before working with them:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * What were their clients doing to solve the problem?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * How much time did it take?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * What tools did they try?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * How did it make them feel?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 2. Why now?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Why are their clients motivated to fix this problem at this specific moment?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Timing = big lever for cold emails.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       3. Discovery path:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * How do their best clients usually find them?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Use this to mirror language and context in your messaging.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             4. Why they chose this provider:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * What did clients find most appealing?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * What made them choose these guys over competitors?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   5. After saying yes:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * What did clients expect their “day in the life” to become?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * What are they doing now that they couldn’t do before?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         6. Source quality of these answers

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Best: customer interviews.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Next: prospect interviews, communities, forums.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Weakest: random Google or generic GPT answers.

If answers are:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Vague (“save time”, “grow revenue”)

→ You must dig deeper on the onboarding call.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Specific & emotional (“stopped firefighting in Slack Fridays and started strategizing with the team instead”)

→ Copy gold. Use that language directly (or very close).

Outcome of this section:
You should be able to describe one of their ideal customers like a character in a story:
“Before working with them, [ICP] was doing [X], frustrated because [Y], and trying [Z tools]. They finally decided to act because [trigger], and what they really wanted was [emotional desire].”
That’s what you write emails from.

7. Section 3 – Offer, Unique Mechanism & Proof
This section defines what we’re actually selling and why it’s compelling.
Key questions to analyze:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  1. What exactly do you sell?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Look beyond “SEO” or “paid ads” or “lead gen”.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Translate their answer internally into:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * What tangible outcome they deliver.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * What vehicle they use to get there.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           2. Problems they solve

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * What pains do they directly fix?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * Connect these back to the emotional journey section.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 3. Case studies & results

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Look for:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * Clear before/after metrics:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Revenue growth.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Leads.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Booked calls.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * ROI.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Red flags:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                * Only vanity metrics:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * “10,000 more website visitors”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   * “Ranked higher on Google”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      * If so, you must push them for monetary or operational outcomes.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         4. Most recent case studies

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            * Extract:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Niche.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Starting point.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Ending point.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               * Time frame.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * This becomes your social proof lines in emails.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     5. Risk reversal / guarantee

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        * Do they have:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * Money-back guarantees?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           * “Work with you until X” style promises?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * This can be used as a strong angle if it’s credible.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 6. What makes them unique?

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Unique mechanism = “how” they do it differently.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Use this in copy as:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       * The thing that sets them apart from 100 other agencies.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          7. Goals

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Current revenue.

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             * Desired revenue.
