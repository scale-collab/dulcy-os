﻿How to Form a Campaign Thesis SOP
How to Form a Campaign Thesis - Google Docs - 11 December 2025
Purpose: To create a clear, strategic campaign thesis for every new client across cold email, LinkedIn, and Voice AI.
When to Use: Immediately after onboarding a new client or before launching any outbound campaign.
________________


1. Overview
The Campaign Thesis is the foundation of every outbound campaign.
It defines:
* WHO we are targeting

* WHY we’re reaching out

* WHAT we expect to test and achieve

It ensures that both our internal team and the client fully understand the strategy, direction, and purpose of the campaign before launch. The thesis is required for proper execution, QA, and for the AI agent that reads/uses this context.
________________


2. Inputs Required
2.1 Client Onboarding Form
This is ALWAYS your starting point.
✔ Must be reviewed in full
✔ If the client leaves unclear or incomplete sections, schedule a call to clarify
✔ YOU must extract the insights, not rely on the client’s vague responses
Example: VizImpact left fields blank → required a clarification call.
Example: Snowball completed everything perfectly → ideal onboarding form.
2.2 Client Website Review
Spend 3–5 minutes skimming:
   * Services

   * Case studies

   * Main value props

   * Past results

   * Pricing indicators (if available)

This gives context before building your thesis.
________________


3. What Goes Into a Campaign Thesis
A complete thesis ALWAYS includes three pillars:
________________


Pillar 1 — The WHO (ICP Definition)
Define exactly who we’re targeting.
Include:
      1. Pain present (they actually need this solution)

      2. Purchasing power (they can afford the offer)

      3. Easy to target (TAM ≥ 10,000; not ultra-niche)

      4. Growing industry (no dying markets like print newspapers)

      5. Role titles and company characteristics

This is where you write the “ideal buyer profile” clearly and precisely.
________________


Pillar 2 — The WHY (Strategy + Timing + Offer)
This section answers: Why are we even reaching out? Why now?
Include:
         * The strategy being used (whitepaper, value-first, research-based, direct callout, outbound awareness, etc.)

         * Timing (why this quarter, seasonality, market trend, etc.)

         * The offer being positioned (audit, consultation, done-for-you, trial, blueprint, etc.)

This MUST explain the reasoning behind the approach.
________________


Pillar 3 — The WHAT (Testing + Expectations)
This describes:
            * What hypotheses we’re testing

            * Expected campaign behavior

            * Type of campaign (lead gen, research, nurture, awareness, etc.)

            * Timeframe for sending

            * What success looks like

This helps the client and internal team align expectations.
________________


4. Building the Thesis in Atlassian
4.1 Create the Campaign
Navigate to:
Campaigns → Create Campaign Thesis
Fill out:
               * Client name

               * Campaign name (ex: “Snowball – Real Estate Agents – Direct Mail Expansion”)

This automatically creates a draft under the client.
________________


4.2 Attach Lead List
Lead lists must be attached through:
Lead List Creation → Upload Leads
Fill out:
                  * Client

                  * File name

                  * of leads

                  * Apollo or Clay link

                  * Trigger scrape = YES

When saved → leads show automatically inside the Campaign Thesis.
________________


4.3 Write the Thesis
Inside the thesis template:
Paste in three sections:
                     1. Who we are targeting

                     2. Why we’re reaching out

                     3. What we expect to test + timeline + KPIs

DO NOT overwrite.
This process should take 5–10 minutes max.
________________


4.4 Record the Campaign Thesis Video (Loom)
Every campaign thesis MUST include a short Loom video covering:
                        * The ICP

                        * The offer

                        * The strategy

                        * The copy direction

                        * The lead source

                        * Expectations

This helps clients visualize the strategy and gives context to our AI agent.
Paste the Loom link into the “Campaign Thesis Video” field.
________________


4.5 Add Initial Subject Lines + Body Copy Draft
At the bottom of the thesis, paste your first-pass copy:
Examples:
Subject: Any leads?


Hi {{first_name}},


• Pain point
• Value offer
• CTA
This is NOT the final version — just direction for the writing team.
________________


5. Full Steps Summary (Step-by-Step)
Step 1 — Review onboarding form
Identify gaps → schedule clarification call if needed.
Step 2 — Review client website
Understand services, results, core value props.
Step 3 — Define the WHO
Clarify ICP using the 4–5 requirements.
Step 4 — Define the WHY
Strategy, timing, offer.
Step 5 — Define the WHAT
Testing hypotheses, KPIs, expected outcomes.
Step 6 — Create Campaign Thesis in Atlassian
Fill in client name + campaign name.
Step 7 — Upload lead list
Attach through Lead List Creation.
Step 8 — Write Thesis in template
3 sections, short, clear, strategic.
Step 9 — Record Loom Thesis Video
Explain campaign direction.
Step 10 — Add initial copy
Subject lines + directional scripts.
________________


6. Notes & Best Practices
                           * Never write a thesis without full clarity on the client’s offer.

                           * Always ensure the ICP has both volume and urgency.

                           * Keep thesis language clear and non-technical.

                           * Remember this is a hypothesis, not the final campaign.

                           * Use this document as the foundation for optimization later.

________________


7. Common Errors to Avoid
❌ ICP too narrow or impossible to target
❌ Offer unclear or too high commitment
❌ Strategy not tied to timing
❌ Writing a thesis without checking case studies
❌ Forgetting to attach the lead list
❌ Overwriting the thesis into a long essay
________________


8. Expected Outcome
A campaign thesis should give your team:
                              * Absolute clarity on the target

                              * Absolute clarity on the direction

                              * Absolute clarity on messaging

                              * A documented reasoning behind the strategy

                              * A foundation for optimization

This ensures internal alignment + client transparency.
