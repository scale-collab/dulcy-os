Section 3: Sales Process & Pipeline Flow
________________


🛠️ Core Sales Process: 4 Stages from Cold to Close
Stage
	Description
	Goal
	1. Connect
	Cold outreach, inbound response, or referral opens dialogue
	Book a Simplify & Scale Session
	2. Discover
	Initial discovery call / Simplify & Scale Session
	Qualify fit + schedule Opportunity Analysis
	3. Diagnose
	Deep-dive audit + strategy call / Opportunity Analysis
	Present roadmap + confirm value → Proposal
	4. Close
	Agreement, signature, kickoff
	Launch and handoff to delivery team
	

________________


🔁 Sales Stage Breakdown
🧲 STAGE 1: CONNECT
Objective: Create interest + move to discovery
Channels:
                              * Cold email outreach (via outbound team)

                              * LinkedIn DMs

                              * Referrals / warm intros

                              * Inbound form fills

Key Actions:
                                 * Personalize message to ICP pain points

                                 * Reference catalog size / revenue tier if possible

                                 * Invite to book Simplify & Scale Session (Calendly or direct CTA)

Tools:
                                    * Cold email templates

                                    * LinkedIn messaging scripts

                                    * Rainmaker scheduling link

________________


📞 STAGE 2: DISCOVER
Objective: Qualify fit + frame problems
Call Name: Simplify & Scale Session
Duration: ~30 minutes
Format: Zoom or Google Meet
Led by: Sales rep or co-founder
What to Cover:
                                       * Quick rapport + context

                                       * "What’s working / what’s not" openers

                                       * Business model + SKU structure

                                       * Current marketplace presence (Amazon, Walmart, Target)

                                       * Internal resources (team vs. agency vs. solo)

                                       * Pain points + growth goals

                                       * Qualify urgency and ownership of solving problems

End Goal:
 → If aligned, pitch the Opportunity Analysis Audit
 → Book a follow-up call for presentation of findings
________________


📊 STAGE 3: DIAGNOSE
Objective: Deliver value, build trust, pitch partnership
Call Name: Opportunity Analysis Review
Duration: ~45–60 minutes
Led by: Sales + Strategy Team Lead / Founder
What’s Included in the Audit:
                                          * Ad spend efficiency + performance breakdown

                                          * Catalog performance mapping (top sellers vs. underperformers)

                                          * Listing quality scorecard (copy, creative, SEO, reviews)

                                          * Operational audit (fees, Buy Box, suppression, compliance)

                                          * Revenue & profit growth potential projections

What to Present:
                                             * Key findings and gaps

                                             * The before and after picture (with Adverio)

                                             * Your 4-Pillar Marketplace Command™ approach

                                             * Projected ROI range (custom to brand)

                                             * Your “No Product Left Behind” philosophy

End Goal:
 → Present clear next step: proposal + start date
________________


✍️ STAGE 4: CLOSE
Objective: Convert into a client + kickoff cleanly
Assets Needed:
                                                * Custom proposal doc

                                                * Contract/engagement agreement (3 or 6-month)

                                                * ROI Guarantee (in writing, if included)

Steps:
                                                   * Answer final objections (see Objection Handling in Section 4)

                                                   * Reframe cost as profit lever

                                                   * Confirm decision-maker involvement

                                                   * Book kickoff date & intro to Strategic Brand Manager

                                                   * Launch onboarding sequence (internal + client-facing)

________________


✅ Internal Pipeline Hygiene
Rule
	Why It Matters
	Update CRM weekly
	Prevents dropped deals and missed follow-ups
	Always set next step on calls
	No “drift” between meetings
	Use deal stage labels (Connect → Discover → Diagnose → Close)
	Aligns team across marketing/sales/delivery
	Tag deals by vertical & catalog size
	Helps refine ICP & scale personalization
	Send Opportunity Analysis recap deck before pitch call
	Preframes value + elevates perceived expertise

🎙️ Elevator Pitch (3 Variants)
▶️ 30-Second Version (Networking / LinkedIn / First Call)
“We help 7–8 figure brands with large product catalogs grow profitably on Amazon, Walmart, and Target by managing everything — from ads to operations. Our system is called Marketplace Command™, and it’s built to unlock profit from every SKU, not just top-sellers.”
________________


▶️ 60-Second Version (Discovery Call)
“Most agencies focus on top-line revenue or manage just the bestsellers. We don’t. At Adverio, we help brands with 100s or 1000s of products get more traffic, better conversion, stronger retention, and cleaner operations — across all marketplaces. We assign a full team, act like owners, and guarantee ROI. It’s all built into our 4-pillar system called Marketplace Command™.”
________________


▶️ 90-Second Version (VSL, Podcast, Event)
“What we’ve found is that most brands on Amazon plateau because they’re only optimizing their top 10% of SKUs, they’re stuck in operations, and they don’t have a multi-channel plan. We built Marketplace Command™ to fix that. It’s a complete marketplace management system where our team runs your ads, listings, design, strategy, and operations across Amazon — and helps you expand into Walmart and Target too. Everything we do is focused on generating real, measurable profit. And we stand behind it with a custom ROI guarantee.”

🧠 Discovery Call Prompts
Use these to unlock pain, urgency, and qualified fit:
Openers:
                                                      * “Walk me through your current Amazon setup — what’s working well?”

                                                      * “How’s the team structured today? Internal, agency, or hybrid?”

                                                      * “If Amazon’s not your biggest bottleneck, what is?”

Pain & Opportunity:
                                                         * “What % of your catalog do you feel is actually getting attention?”

                                                         * “Do you know your blended ad ROI right now?”

                                                         * “How are you tracking profit by SKU or product type?”

Future Vision:
                                                            * “If we had this convo 12 months from now, what would success look like?”

                                                            * “What’s stopping you from hitting that?”

                                                            * “If you had 20 more hours per week to focus on growth — what would you do?”

That’s a huge lift, and usually where we come in as an extension of your team — with specialists for each area.”

If we don’t deliver, it doesn’t work — for either of us.”
