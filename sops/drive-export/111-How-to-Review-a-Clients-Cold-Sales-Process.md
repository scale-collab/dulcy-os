﻿How to Review a Client’s Cold Sales Process SOP
Project Management: Project Management Board - Airtable - 1 December 2025’


(“Cold Sales Audit” – Step 3 of Onboarding)
1. Purpose
This SOP shows you how to audit a client’s cold sales process so they can actually convert the leads we generate.
Even if our outbound is perfect, clients will churn if:
* Prospects don’t show up

* Sales calls are weak or unstructured

* There is no follow-up or tracking

The goal of this audit is to:
   * Make sure the client has a functional, simple sales process for cold leads

   * Help them close in 1–2 calls whenever possible

   * Identify and fix gaps in scripts, objections, follow-up, and handoff

________________


2. When to Use This SOP
Use this SOP:
      * After the client has been onboarded and understands the service

      * After the cold funnel audit (LinkedIn, landing page, VSL, booking funnel)

      * As “Step 3” of onboarding:

         1. Client Onboarding Call

         2. Cold Funnel Audit

         3. Cold Sales Audit (this SOP)

This call is usually run by the Head of Sales / closer or a senior CSM who understands sales deeply.
________________


3. Outcomes of This Audit
By the end of the audit, the client should have:
            * A clear first-call structure (discovery + value + next step)

            * A plan to close in 1–2 calls where appropriate

            * A basic objection-handling framework

            * A post-call follow-up system (email + reminders)

            * Tracking in place for show rates, close rates, and revenue per call

            * A clean handoff process to fulfillment

            * Awareness of key red flags hurting their close rate

________________


4. Pre-Call Prep (Before You Audit)
Before running the audit, make sure you:
               * Review:

                  * Their offer (pricing, terms, guarantee, who it’s for)

                  * Their ICP and what we’re booking for them

                  * Any sales assets they already use (deck, script, proposal, etc.)

                     * Confirm they:

                        * Record calls (e.g., Zoom, Fathom, Gong, etc.) or are willing to start

                        * Have some kind of CRM or pipeline tracker (even if basic)

If they have no structure at all, your job is to give them a simple baseline that they can implement immediately.
________________


5. Section 1 – Pre-Call Audit Checklist
On the call, start by understanding their current sales setup.
5.1 Core Foundations
Check if they have:
                           * A structured sales call script (even if loose, not word-for-word)

                           * A call recording tool active on every sales call

                           * Clear understanding of:

                              * The core value of their offer

                              * Their unique mechanism (what makes them different)

                              * Their outcome (what the client actually gets)

                              * Their pricing and terms

                              * Their ideal buyer (role, company type, revenue level)

If any of these are missing, note them as gaps you’ll address later in the call.
________________


6. Section 2 – First Sales Call Script (Structure)
The first sales call should feel:
                                 * Authoritative (they are positioned as the expert, not a beggar)

                                 * Direct (no 10 minutes of random small talk)

                                 * Value-driven (prospect sees they will get clarity and strategy)

6.1 Recommended Opening (High-Level Structure)
Coach them to use an opening similar to:
                                    1. Quick rapport (30–60 seconds)

                                       * “Hey John, how’s your day going?”

                                       * Keep it short; don’t let it drag.

                                          2. Set the frame and authority

                                             * Who they are

                                             * Who they help

                                             * What this call will cover and what the prospect gets, even if they don’t buy

                                                3. Example frame (adapt it to the client):

“Awesome, let’s jump right in so we can respect your time.

 I’m [Name], [Role] at [Company].

 We help [ICP] get [specific outcome] using [high-level mechanism].

 On this call, I’ll ask a few questions about where you’re at, what you’ve tried, and what’s blocking you.

 Then I’ll walk you through the exact strategy I’d use in your shoes.

 At the end, if it feels like a good fit, we can talk about how we might work together. If not, you’ll at least leave with a clear plan.”

                                                4. Discovery (10–15 minutes)

                                                   * Current situation (volume, revenue, current acquisition channels)

                                                   * What they’ve tried (especially outbound)

                                                   * Pains and constraints

                                                   * Desired outcome (target revenue, meetings per month, timeline)

                                                   * Why now? (urgency)

Make sure the client understands:
                                                      * The first call is not a feature demo

                                                      * The goal is to Diagnose → Prescribe → Set next step

7. Section 3 – Going in for the Close (1–2 Call Close)
Explain to the client:
                                                         * For most B2B service offers in the $3k–$10k/month range, a 1–2 call close is realistic

                                                         * More complex, high-ticket deals (e.g., $30k–$50k+) may involve multiple stakeholders and more calls

7.1 Key Rules
                                                            * They must present price to the decision-maker

                                                               * No relying on a junior manager to “take it to the boss”

                                                                  * They should set expectations early:

                                                                     * “If this makes sense and we both agree it’s a fit, we’ll handle everything today and get the ball rolling.”

7.2 Flow Toward the Close
                                                                        1. Confirm alignment before price

                                                                           * “Based on everything we’ve gone through, do you feel like this would help you reach [their stated goal]?”

                                                                              2. Have them repeat back what they liked

                                                                                 * Get them to verbalize why they want this

                                                                                 * What the prospect says is what they believe

                                                                                    3. Only then present the price

                                                                                       * Clearly, confidently, and then shut up

                                                                                       * No nervous rambling after the price

                                                                                          4. Aim to close on the call

                                                                                             * Send contract + payment link live

                                                                                             * Stay on call while they complete it, when appropriate and aligned with ticket size & culture

If they truly need an internal approval, help the client plan:
                                                                                                * A follow-up call with the decision-maker

                                                                                                * What needs to be presented internally (deck, 1-pager, etc.)

________________


8. Section 4 – Handling Objections
Teach them a simple 3-step framework:
                                                                                                   1. Isolate

                                                                                                   2. Reframe (new perspective)

                                                                                                   3. Confirm

Example objection: “We tried this before, it didn’t work.”
                                                                                                      * Isolate

                                                                                                         * “Totally understand. When you say you tried it and it didn’t work, what exactly happened?”

                                                                                                            * Reframe (your ‘steak’ / perspective analogy)

                                                                                                               * Compare it to having a bad version of something once (bad steak at one restaurant) versus deciding that thing itself never works (never eating steak again).

                                                                                                               * Reframe: they didn’t fail because “outbound doesn’t work”; they failed because the execution wasn’t right.

                                                                                                                  * Confirm

                                                                                                                     * “Would you agree it’s more about how it’s done than whether the channel works at all?”

Then tie it back to:
                                                                                                                        * Their goals

                                                                                                                        * The risk of doing nothing

                                                                                                                        * How your system addresses the failure points they experienced previously

Do the same with:
                                                                                                                           * “I need to think about it”

                                                                                                                           * “I need to talk to my partner”

                                                                                                                           * “We’re too busy right now”

                                                                                                                           * “We tried something similar and it didn’t work”

Your job in the audit:
Make sure the client has written responses or at least a mental framework for their 3–5 most common objections.
________________


9. Section 5 – Post-Call Follow Up
Most clients drop the ball here. Your audit should enforce:
9.1 Minimum Follow-Up Standard
Immediately after the call, they should:
                                                                                                                              * Send a same-day recap email that includes:

                                                                                                                                 * Thank you + reminder of outcomes

                                                                                                                                 * Call recording link (if available)

                                                                                                                                 * Key points discussed

                                                                                                                                 * Clear next step (pay, sign, or schedule follow-up)

                                                                                                                                 * Relevant assets (case studies, interviews, decks, videos)

                                                                                                                                    * Log notes in the CRM:

                                                                                                                                       * Pain points

                                                                                                                                       * Budget + authority + timeline

                                                                                                                                       * Objections raised

                                                                                                                                       * Outcome of the call

                                                                                                                                       * Next action date & time

                                                                                                                                          * Enroll the lead into a follow-up sequence:

                                                                                                                                             * If “hot” but not closed → short, aggressive follow-up (daily or every 1–2 days)

                                                                                                                                             * If “warm but not now” → nurture (weekly/monthly touches)

9.2 Automations (Optional but Recommended)
Advise them to:
                                                                                                                                                * Use tools like Make / Zapier / n8n to:

                                                                                                                                                   * Trigger recap emails after calls (or after Fathom logs a meeting)

                                                                                                                                                   * Set tasks and reminders automatically

                                                                                                                                                   * Move deals between pipeline stages based on events

________________


10. Section 6 – Tracking & Metrics
You cannot improve what you don’t track.
Recommend that they track at minimum:
                                                                                                                                                      * Number of leads from us

                                                                                                                                                      * Booked calls

                                                                                                                                                      * Show rate (shows / bookings)

                                                                                                                                                      * Close rate (closed deals / shows)

                                                                                                                                                      * Revenue per deal

                                                                                                                                                      * Revenue per call (total revenue / total sales calls)

                                                                                                                                                      * Sales cycle length (first contact → close)

                                                                                                                                                      * Common objections per call

Explain the simple value logic:
                                                                                                                                                         * If each call is worth $X on average, and outbound provides Y calls per month, outbound ROI becomes obvious and easier to justify.

________________


11. Section 7 – Handoff to Fulfillment
Once a deal closes, the handoff should be frictionless.
Audit for:
                                                                                                                                                            * Is payment collected promptly?

                                                                                                                                                            * Is the contract properly signed?

                                                                                                                                                            * Is there an internal notification (e.g., Slack) that:

                                                                                                                                                               * Includes key context (ICP, offer, expectations, start date)

                                                                                                                                                               * Tags the right fulfillment team members

                                                                                                                                                                  * Does the client immediately:

                                                                                                                                                                     * Receive their onboarding email / form

                                                                                                                                                                     * Know who their main point of contact is

                                                                                                                                                                     * Get invited into the right tools/workspaces

                                                                                                                                                                        * Are “why they bought” notes captured?

                                                                                                                                                                           * Useful for future case studies and retention

________________


12. Section 8 – Red Flags to Call Out
During the audit, watch for these:
                                                                                                                                                                              * The rep talks 80–90% of the time

                                                                                                                                                                              * Discovery is shallow:

                                                                                                                                                                                 * No probing questions about “why now”, deeper pains, or money

                                                                                                                                                                                    * Vague metrics:

                                                                                                                                                                                       * “We helped them grow a lot” vs. specific ROI or revenue

                                                                                                                                                                                          * Avoidance of money talk:

                                                                                                                                                                                             * Rep is uncomfortable discussing revenue, budgets, or price

                                                                                                                                                                                                * No clear timeline or next step:

                                                                                                                                                                                                   * Calls end with “Okay, think about it and let me know”

                                                                                                                                                                                                      * No follow-up system:

                                                                                                                                                                                                         * No automation, no routine, no tracking

                                                                                                                                                                                                            * No tracking of show/close rate:

                                                                                                                                                                                                               * They’re flying blind

                                                                                                                                                                                                                  * Deals regularly “die” after:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * “I need to think about it”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * “I’ll get back to you”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                     * Or multi-call stretched-out nonsense with no real decision

For each red flag, your job in the audit is to:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                        1. Name it (call it out clearly but respectfully)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                        2. Explain why it’s hurting conversions

                                                                                                                                                                                                                        3. Suggest a simple fix (script change, process tweak, automation, etc.)

________________


13. How to Use This SOP in Practice
When you run a Cold Sales Audit call:
                                                                                                                                                                                                                           1. Open with context:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                              * “Today we’re going to go through how you’re handling the leads we send, from first call through follow-up and close, and plug any leaks so you can maximize ROI.”

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 2. Work through the SOP sections in order:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Pre-call foundations

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * First call structure

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Closing approach

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Objection handling

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Post-call follow-up

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Tracking

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Handoff

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    * Red flags

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       3. Take notes and at the end send them:

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * A summary of what’s good

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * A short list of priority fixes (1–3 things to implement now)

                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          * Any templates/scripts you referenced
