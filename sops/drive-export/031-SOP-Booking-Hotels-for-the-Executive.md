Booking Hotels for the Executive

🧾 Objective
To ensure all hotel bookings for the executive are handled with precision, comfort, and compliance, while aligning with personal preferences, travel plans, and budget guidelines.
________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all domestic and international hotel reservations made for the executive for business-related or approved personal travel, including booking, confirmation, and itinerary management.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
* Confirming travel dates and accommodation preferences

* Researching, booking, and managing hotel reservations

* Communicating booking details to the executive

* Updating the executive’s calendar and travel itinerary

* Coordinating with the hotel as needed before, during, or after stay

________________


🔁 Workflow: Booking Hotels
✅ 1. Confirm Travel Details
Before booking, confirm:
Detail
	Notes
	Travel Dates
	Check flight times to confirm accurate check-in/out
	Destination
	Specific city, neighborhood, or near a meeting location
	Trip Purpose
	Conference, meeting, overnight layover, etc.
	Duration
	Total nights required
	Arrival/Departure Times
	For early check-in or late checkout needs
	________________


🧑‍💼 2. Review Executive’s Hotel Preferences
Maintain a Travel Profile for the executive. Common preferences:
Preference
	Example
	Hotel Chains
	Marriott, Hyatt, Four Seasons, etc.
	Room Type
	King bed, high floor, quiet room
	Amenities
	On-site restaurant, fitness center, fast Wi-Fi
	Location
	Close to airport, downtown, or event venue
	Loyalty Programs
	Add membership numbers (Bonvoy, World of Hyatt, etc.)
	Safety/Accessibility
	Valet, 24-hr security, disability access (if needed)
	Budget Range
	Within company or personal limit, unless otherwise approved
	________________


🔍 3. Research Options
Use tools such as:
   * Google Hotels, Booking.com, Expedia

   * Direct hotel websites

   * Travel platform (e.g., Navan, Amex GBT, Concur)

   * Corporate negotiated rates or vendor list

Shortlist 2–3 best options, considering:
      * Proximity to meetings

      * Ratings (4 stars or higher; 8.5+/10 on Booking.com)

      * Amenities and cancellation policies

      * Pricing and refundable rate

Tip: Screenshot or save hotel page with full rate details and map location.

🛎 5. Book the Hotel
Once approved:
         1. Go to official hotel site or preferred travel platform

         2. Choose:

            * Executive’s full legal name

            * Loyalty program number

            * Correct room type

            * Check-in and check-out dates

            * Add early check-in/late checkout request if needed

               3. Choose refundable or flexible rate whenever possible

               4. Use company card or pre-approved method

               5. Add any special notes (e.g., late arrival, room away from elevator)

               6. Save confirmation email and receipt/PDF

✅ Double-check guest name, dates, and room type before confirming.
________________

📆 7. Add to Calendar
Add hotel stay to the executive’s calendar (Motion / Google / Outlook):
                  * Title: Hotel Stay – [Hotel Name], [City]

                  * All-day event (or mark duration from check-in to check-out)

                  * Location: Full address

                  * Notes: Booking ref, contact info, link to confirmation

                  * Mark as "Private" if personal stay

________________


🧳 8. Coordinate Transportation (If Needed)
If airport pickup, car rental, or local transport is required:
                     * Schedule through hotel concierge or ride service

                     * Confirm pickup time, location, and contact info

                     * Add to calendar with map links or instructions

________________


🔁 9. Manage Changes or Cancellations
                        * Monitor cancellation deadlines

                        * Track hotel reminders or check-in emails

                        * If plans change, cancel or modify reservation as early as possible

                        * Confirm refund policy before cancellation

                        * Update calendar and notify executive of any changes

________________


🛑 What NOT to Do
                           * Don’t book non-refundable rates unless explicitly approved

                           * Never assume preferences—always verify for first-time destinations

                           * Avoid hotels with low ratings or poor location (e.g., too far, unsafe areas)

                           * Don’t use personal cards unless instructed

                           * Don’t forget to request loyalty points or upgrades when applicable
