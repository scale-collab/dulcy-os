🧾 Objective
To maintain a well-organized, optimized, and protected executive calendar using Motion, ensuring time is used efficiently, priorities are honored, and meetings are scheduled appropriately with minimal conflicts or reschedules.


________________


🧩 Scope
This SOP applies to all calendar management tasks using Motion, including meeting scheduling, rescheduling, time blocking, priority management, and daily/weekly planning for the executive.
________________


🧑‍💼 Responsibilities
The Executive Assistant is responsible for:
* Managing the executive’s calendar in Motion daily

* Reviewing incoming requests and triaging them based on urgency and fit

* Prioritizing deep work, focus time, and external/internal meetings

* Preventing over-scheduling or unnecessary context switching

* Communicating schedule changes proactively

________________


🔁 Daily Workflow
✅ 1. Start-of-Day Review (by 8:30 AM)
   * Review Motion’s auto-scheduled tasks and confirm any manual overrides

   * Identify and adjust for:

      * Conflicts

      * Double bookings

      * Travel time gaps

      * Overly packed days

         * Send the executive a daily briefing with:

            * Day’s schedule summary

            * Meeting goals or prep items

            * Any pending reschedules or decisions

________________


🗂️ 2. Meeting Request Intake & Processing
Sources of Meeting Requests:
               * Emails

               * Calendly submissions (if used for inbound requests)

               * Slack/Signal messages

               * Internal team calendars

               * CRM reminders or follow-ups

Processing Steps:
                  1. Triage the request:

                     * Is it necessary?

                     * Can someone else attend?

                     * Does it require prep or context?

                        2. Assign meeting type:

                           * 🟢 External (clients, partners, vendors)

                           * 🔵 Internal (team 1:1s, all-hands, strategy)

                           * 🟣 Admin (medical, legal, personal, etc.)

                              3. Prioritize and schedule in Motion:

                                 * Use the Motion Scheduler to auto-place the meeting OR manually book if it involves high-touch planning

                                 * Avoid overlapping with existing deep work or recurring protected time

                                 * Add meeting link (Zoom/Google Meet), attendees, and notes

                                    4. Send invite and confirmation

                                       * Include purpose/agenda, call link, and reschedule option

                                       * Add [Executive Name] as host or optional if attending tentatively

________________


🧱 3. Time Blocking Best Practices
Time Block Type
	Description
	Notes
	🧠 Deep Work
	Focus time for strategy, writing, or planning
	Minimum 90-min blocks 2–3x per week
	🤝 1:1s / Syncs
	Standing internal meetings
	Stack to specific days if possible
	🧭 Executive Office Hours
	Reserved buffer for quick check-ins or urgent requests
	Use Calendly or Motion Booking Link
	💼 Client/External
	Partner or customer calls
	Mornings preferred, limit to 3/day
	🗂 Admin
	Finance, HR, legal
	End of day or light days
	🧘 Personal Time
	Lunch, workouts, medical
	Block as “Busy – Private”
	Use Motion’s Task Management to convert tasks into schedulable blocks.
________________


🔁 4. Rescheduling & Conflicts
                                          * Use Motion’s auto-reschedule feature first for non-critical tasks

                                          * For meetings:

                                             * Give 24-hour notice when possible

                                             * Offer 2+ alternative time slots

                                             * Communicate via email or Slack with proposed reschedule reason

                                                * Avoid back-to-back meetings > 3 in a row

                                                * Keep 15–30 min buffer between critical meetings when possible

________________


📆 5. Weekly Planning (Fridays by 4:00 PM)
Review:
                                                   * Upcoming week’s major meetings or deadlines

                                                   * Deep work sessions blocked?

                                                   * Any unnecessary calls to remove or consolidate?

                                                   * Are internal meetings aligned to the exec’s energy patterns?

Adjust:
                                                      * Lock high-priority tasks using Motion’s “Fixed Time”

                                                      * Reschedule low-priority meetings or placeholders

                                                      * Share weekly agenda with executive (optional Slack/email brief)

________________


📲 6. Motion Platform Tips
Feature
	Use
	Scheduler

	Auto-Planning
	Let Motion reorder tasks around confirmed meetings
	Fixed Tasks
	Prevent rescheduling of high-priority events
	Task Priority
	Set High, Medium, Low priorities to influence calendar placement
	Sub-calendars
	Toggle internal, personal, travel calendars on/off
	Meeting Notes
	Link prep docs in meeting descriptions
	Sync Motion with Google/Outlook calendars to avoid cross-platform conflicts.
________________


📥 Meeting Confirmation & Protocol
When Scheduling:
                                                         * Add descriptive title (e.g., “Client Sync – Q3 Review”)

                                                         * Attach Google Doc/Notion link for agenda or prep

                                                         * Invite all relevant parties with email + correct time zone

                                                         * Add Motion task reminder if action is needed before/after

When Cancelling:
                                                            * Use professional tone and provide alternate slots

                                                            * Keep executive CC’d or informed as needed

________________


🛑 What NOT to Do
                                                               * Never schedule over:

                                                                  * Personal or blocked time without explicit approval

                                                                  * Travel or flight windows

                                                                  * Executive focus blocks labeled as “No Meetings”

                                                                     * Do not use vague calendar titles (e.g., “Call”)

                                                                     * Don’t let internal syncs outweigh strategic deep work

                                                                     * Avoid scheduling calls within 10 minutes of another without buffer

🔐 Privacy & Access
                                                                        * Access to Motion must be secured with 2FA

                                                                        * Only the EA and the Executive have full editing permissions

                                                                        * Use naming conventions to keep calendar entries clean and searchable

                                                                        * Keep sensitive entries (e.g., legal, medical) marked as Private
