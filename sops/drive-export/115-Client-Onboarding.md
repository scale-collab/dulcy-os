﻿Client Onboarding SOP
Loom
Purpose
To ensure every new client entering Dulcy’s system is properly onboarded, informed, supported, and technically set up for a successful launch of their outbound campaign.
________________


1. Trigger: A New Client Signs a Contract
What Happens
* A member of sales (Alex or another team member) sends the contract.

* Once signed, the contract appears in the #contract-events Slack channel.

* This is your cue that onboarding has begun.

Your Responsibility
   * Verify the contract was signed.

   * Confirm the client has submitted payment (if not, payment will be collected before setup continues).

________________


2. Automatic Systems That Activate After Signature
Once the contract is signed, automations activate:
✅ 
Client receives “Welcome & Onboarding” email
The email includes:
      * Link to the Client Onboarding Form

      * Link to schedule their onboarding call

✅ 
Slack Automations
         * A private Slack channel is auto-created for the client.

         * Team members + client are invited automatically.

✅ 
Close CRM Automations
            * Opportunity is marked as Won.

            * An autogenerated AI note is added containing:

               * Pain points

               * Mindset

               * High-level context

✅ 
Workspace & Tool Setup
Automations create and configure:
                  * Dulcy Workspace

                  * Email Guard Workspace

                  * HeyReach Workspace

                  * Email Bison Workspace

                  * Invite the team

                  * Invite the client (first member only — additional members must be added manually)

👉 
Your job:
Once invitations land in your inbox → join all created workspaces immediately.
________________


3. Client Onboarding Form (MOST IMPORTANT STEP)
Clients are taken to the Onboarding Form after clicking the link in their email.
This form contains all core information needed to successfully run their campaign:
What the form collects:
                     * Their calendar link

                     * Ideal ICP

                     * Emotional buyer journey

                     * Offer details

                     * Avatar pain points

                     * Required logins

                     * Brand assets / profile pictures

                     * Market positioning

                     * Key expectations

This form is your “Golden Source of Truth” for building everything.
________________


4. Internal Data Processing & Airtable Setup
When the client submits the form:
Airtable automations generate:
                        * A full Onboarding Record

                        * Workspace IDs

                        * API tokens

                        * Suggested domains (e.g., .info domains)

                        * Profile pictures

                        * All question answers

                        * CheapInboxes order sheet link

                        * Client Portal record

                        * Airtable “Active Client” record

                        * Close CRM “Contact = 0” placeholder

                        * Client Portal document

Everything needed for infrastructure setup is auto-compiled.
________________


5. Onboarding Call Preparation
Before the onboarding call:
Review:
                           * Completed Onboarding Form

                           * LucidChart “Onboarding Process Flow”

                           * Slack channel status

                           * Workspace invites

                           * Suggested domains

                           * AI-generated CRM notes

During the onboarding call:
                              * Reinforce the partnership

                              * Walk through infrastructure overview

                              * Explain the next 14-day warmup & setup phase

                              * Show them the Lucidchart flow

                              * Set expectations about communication frequency

                              * Reassure them about daily progress updates

________________


6. Conducting the Onboarding Call
On the call:
Goal:
Make the client feel:
                                 * Heard

                                 * Supported

                                 * Clear on next steps

                                 * Confident in their decision

Review together:
                                    * Offer & ICP refinement

                                    * Domain purchase strategy

                                    * Email accounts setup

                                    * Sending volume

                                    * Messaging approach

                                    * Timeline

                                    * What you need from them

                                    * What they can expect from you

Great onboarding = future smooth campaign.
________________


7. After the Call: Daily Progress Communication
Do NOT go silent during the warmup period.
Every weekday, send a quick update such as:
                                       * “We purchased your domains”

                                       * “Your workspace is configured”

                                       * “We pulled your ICP data”

                                       * “We begin warmup today”

                                       * “We are populating your lead lists”

                                       * “Copy is being drafted & will be ready for review tomorrow”

Silence kills trust.
Frequent updates create confidence.
________________


8. Manual Step: Client Portal User Linking
When a client creates a Client Portal account:
You must manually:
                                          1. Go to Portal Users

                                          2. Select the new user

                                          3. Associate them with their correct client workspace ID

                                          4. Save

If you skip this, they will see ALL clients — which is not allowed.
________________


9. Optional Manual Steps
Only needed in specific cases:
If additional client team members need access:
Manually add them in:
                                             * Slack → Settings > Workspace > Members > Invite

                                             * HeyReach → Invite user

                                             * Email Guard → Invite user

                                             * Dulcy Workspace → Invite user

If multiple domains or inboxes are needed:
Confirm:
                                                * Quantity

                                                * Naming standards

                                                * Purchase timing

________________


10. Completion of Onboarding
Client onboarding is complete when:
                                                   * Their onboarding form is fully submitted

                                                   * All tools are created (Slack, HeyReach, Email Guard, Email Bison)

                                                   * Data is processed into Airtable

                                                   * Client is assigned to the correct workspace

                                                   * Domains and inboxes are prepared

                                                   * A successful onboarding call has taken place

                                                   * Daily communication has begun

The client is now ready for campaign build-out.
