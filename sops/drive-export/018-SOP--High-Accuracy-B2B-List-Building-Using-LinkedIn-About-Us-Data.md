Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
High-Accuracy B2B List Building Using LinkedIn About Us Data
________________


1. Purpose
This SOP outlines a repeatable, scalable, and high-accuracy process for building B2B outbound prospect lists using company-level LinkedIn “About Us” data as a semantic filtering engine. The goal is to dramatically improve list relevance, reduce reliance on inaccurate industry tags, and ensure outbound campaigns target the right companies with confidence.
________________


2. Scope
This procedure applies to:
* Sales Operations

* Growth & Outbound Teams

* Lead Generation Specialists

* Agencies and SDR teams using tools such as Clay, Apollo, Smartlead, or Google Sheets

This SOP covers company-level filtering only (pre-contact enrichment).
________________


3. Key Outcomes
By following this SOP, teams will:
   * Increase list accuracy from ~50–60% to ~90–95%

   * Eliminate guesswork caused by unreliable industry classifications

   * Build scalable, niche-specific outbound lists

   * Reduce spend on heavy AI classification tools

________________


4. Tools & Requirements
Required Inputs:
      * Company LinkedIn URL (already present in list data)

      * Basic company fields (industry, company name)

Recommended Tools:
         * Clay

         * Apollo

         * Google Sheets

         * Smartlead

         * Any enrichment or scraping tool capable of pulling LinkedIn About Us text
