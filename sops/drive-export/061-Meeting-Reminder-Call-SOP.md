Objective:
The goal is to remind prospects of their upcoming meeting with Dulcy, provide them with essential details, and ensure they’re prepared. This reminder will increase the likelihood of attendance and allow the prospect to come prepared with questions if needed.
Video Walkthrough: HERE
When to Call:
1. If the Meeting is More than 24 Hours Away:
•        Call the prospect 24 hours before the meeting time.
2. If the Meeting is Less than 24 Hours Away:
•        Call in the afternoon of the day before to give them a heads-up. Avoid calling too close to the meeting time.
Steps for Meeting Reminder Calls:
1. Prepare for the Call:
•        Open Close CRM and locate the lead’s page.
•        Review any relevant notes, previous communications, and other details to personalize the call.
•        Make sure 262-624-1152 (our business calling number) is selected for the call settings.
•        Adjust your microphone and audio settings in Close CRM before starting.
2. Make the Call Using Close CRM:
•        Navigate to the lead’s contact page and click Call. All calls will automatically be recorded for reference.
3. Reminder Script:
•        Use this template to ensure a consistent message:
“Hello [Lead’s Name], this is [Your Name] from Dulcy. I wanted to remind you of your Zoom meeting with Alexander tomorrow to discuss your current outbound strategies for [COMPANY NAME]. The meeting is scheduled for [time] EST. If you have any questions beforehand, please feel free to reach out.”
4. Notes after the Call:
•        Once the call is completed, add a note to the lead’s in Close CRM saying that they confirmed.
•        Add any relevant notes about the call, including any questions they asked or special requests.
•        If the prospect confirms or requests changes, update the meeting time in Calendly accordingly with the reschedule link.
•        If the prospects ask to cancel, politely try to reschedule the prospect, if they are hesitant and don’t want to, kindly ask what would be the reason for the cancellation and try to find their real objection.
5. Notify team:
•        Notify the team with a ping in the slack channel 30 minutes before a meeting begins or of any changes that may have occurred
Summary:
•        24-hour Reminder Call: Call 24 hours before the meeting if time allows; otherwise, call the afternoon before.
•        Use the Reminder Script: Keep the reminder polite and informative.
•        Update CRM and Slack: Record notes in Close CRM, reschedule as needed with the reschedule link in the lead’s info, and confirm updates with the team.
